Pick Otterly.AI if you need a tracker with a price you can budget this week: it lists $29, $189 and $489 a month. Pick Profound if SSO, SOC 2, API access and dedicated support decide the purchase, and you can buy through a sales quote. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested either product hands-on.

## Which should you choose, Profound or Otterly.AI?

For most teams asking this question, Otterly.AI. It is the only one of the two with a published monthly price, so it is the only one you can put in a budget today. Profound is the right call when procurement requirements, not price, drive the decision.

Our rule of thumb:

- **Budget of a few hundred dollars a month, one team, one or two brands:** Otterly.AI. Standard at $189 for 100 prompts is the realistic starting plan.
- **Security review, SSO/SAML, SOC 2, dedicated support, many regions:** Profound Enterprise.
- **Undecided:** run both 7-day trials, but know what each tests. Otterly's trial takes your prompts; Profound's does not.

One piece of common advice we disagree with: "Profound covers more engines, so it is better." Engine count matters only if your buyers use those engines. Otterly's base four plus its three add-ons reach seven engines, and Profound's Enterprise plan reaches up to nine. Check where your buyers actually ask before paying for the last two.

## What does Otterly.AI offer, and what does it cost?

Otterly.AI is a self-serve AI search monitor. Per its [pricing page](https://otterly.ai/pricing) on October 3, 2026, Lite is $29 a month for 15 prompts, Standard $189 for 100, and Premium $489 for 400. Enterprise starts at 1,000 prompts and is custom. Annual billing is advertised at 15% off.

What the page lists for every plan:

- **Engines included:** ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity and Microsoft Copilot, tracked daily.
- **Engines as add-ons:** Google AI Mode and Gemini ($9, $59 or $149 a month by plan) and Claude ($29, $109 or $439).
- **Standard and above:** API access, MCP access, Agent Analytics and a Looker Studio connector.
- **Extra prompts:** $99 per 100 on Standard and Premium; not available on Lite.
- **Enterprise:** custom prompt tracking, Single Sign-On and a dedicated customer success manager.

Otterly's [free trial](https://help.otterly.ai/free-trial) runs 7 days with no credit card and 50 prompts. That is more than three times Lite's 15, so a good trial does not prove Lite will be enough.

Otterly says it reads most engines through their public web interfaces, because [API data can differ from what users see](https://help.otterly.ai/how-otterlyai-collects-data). Claude is the exception: it is tracked through the API. We have not tested either method.

## What does Profound offer, and what does it cost?

Profound publishes no monthly price for brands. Its [pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) on October 3, 2026 shows a free Trial and a custom-quoted Enterprise plan. Earlier reviews quote fixed monthly Profound prices; the page we loaded lists none for brands, so confirm any plan name with sales.

What each plan includes, per that page:

- **Trial (free):** 50 prompts run daily for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, one language, one region, limited AI Marketer credits. No history, exports, API or support.
- **Enterprise (custom):** up to nine engines, a tailored prompt plan, all-time history, CSV and JSON export, an API, SSO/SAML with SOC 2 compliance, dedicated support and unlimited seats.

Profound's nine engines differ by page. The pricing page lists ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, DeepSeek, Claude, Google AI Overviews and Exa Search. The [Answer Engine Insights page](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights) names Grok instead of Exa. Ask which nine your contract covers.

Profound says it captures answers "directly from the browser" rather than APIs, and runs every tracked prompt daily. It is also more than a tracker. Its FAQ says Profound is not only for AI search: AI Marketer and Agents run on credits and build marketing workflows. If you only want visibility data, part of Enterprise is capacity you will not use.

## How do Profound and Otterly.AI compare side by side?

Otterly.AI wins on price transparency and speed to start; Profound wins on enterprise controls and a content-workflow layer. The table uses each vendor's own pricing pages as of October 3, 2026.

## Which fits your team and budget?

Match the tool to the constraint that binds you: budget points to Otterly.AI, compliance points to Profound, and unclear needs point to neither yet. These are the cases we see most.

- **Solo marketer or small business:** Otterly.AI Standard, or Lite if you track fewer than 15 questions in one country. Work through [Otterly.AI pricing](/blog/otterly-ai-pricing) before you add engines.
- **Lean agency:** Otterly.AI, which has unlimited workspaces from Standard and a separate Agency Partner programme per its pricing page.
- **Mid-size brand, several markets:** price Otterly Premium against a Profound quote. Ask Profound for a number early; our [Profound pricing](/blog/profound-pricing) guide lists what to ask.
- **Regulated or large enterprise:** Profound, because SOC 2 and SSO/SAML are stated on its Enterprise plan. Check whether Otterly's Enterprise terms meet your security review.
- **You only want to know if AI names you:** neither. A tracker is the wrong first purchase.

## What should you do before paying for either?

Write down your real buyer questions and test them by hand first. A tracker watches only what you feed it, and one AI answer tells you little. SparkToro's study had [600 volunteers run 12 prompts 2,961 times](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) across ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI, and found less than a 1-in-100 chance of the same brand list twice.

That is the reason both vendors run prompts daily, and why a 7-day trial gives you about seven samples per prompt: a first look, not a baseline. Use the trial to judge the interface and the questions, then decide on a quarter of data.

Three steps:

1. List 15 to 30 questions a buyer would ask without naming you.
2. Run them in each engine, or use [AEOeye's free audit](/), which asks three on ChatGPT. The $29 full report covers 10 questions across five engines, once.
3. Only then pick a tracker. AEOeye is not a daily tracker; for monitoring across dozens of prompts, Otterly.AI and Profound are the right category.

**What we saw on each vendor's own site.** In AEOeye's [crawler access study](/resources/ai-crawler-access-study-2026-10.json) (October 2026, 391 sites), otterly.ai had no robots.txt, and its homepage and /llms.txt returned 403 to our self-identifying crawler. tryprofound.com blocked no AI bots, named none, returned 404 for /llms.txt, and showed no JSON-LD in served HTML, which can mean schema injected by JavaScript. One fetch on one day, not a verdict on either product; the [study hub](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) explains the method.
