Pick AthenaHQ if you want to start monitoring AI answers this week with a card and a fixed monthly price. Pick Profound if you are running an enterprise program, expect to go through sales, and want its agents and up to nine tracked engines. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026 (both vendors' own pricing and product pages, fetched on October 3), and we have not tested either product hands-on.

The old advice to choose by "depth versus speed" no longer holds. Both vendors now sell monitoring plus agents that draft or fix content, so the real split is how you buy and how usage is counted.

## Which should you choose, AthenaHQ or Profound?

Choose AthenaHQ when you need a self-serve start and a price you can read. Choose Profound when AI search is already a funded program with a sales-led buying process. Our position: do not pay for either until you know which questions matter to your buyers.

- **AthenaHQ fits** teams that want to start alone: a [Free plan](https://athenahq.ai/pricing) exists, and Starter is $295/month.
- **Profound fits** larger programs: the only paid brand route on [its pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) is a custom Enterprise quote.
- **Neither fits** if you only need to know whether ChatGPT names you today. That is a one-off check, not a subscription.

## What does AthenaHQ offer in October 2026?

AthenaHQ calls itself ["the command center for AEO and GEO"](https://athenahq.ai): it tracks brand visibility across AI platforms, finds citation gaps and suggests on-page and off-page fixes. Entry is free, and the first paid tier is a $295 monthly subscription.

What its own pages state:

- **Free:** $25 free credit, 300 credits, five platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Copilot), unlimited members.
- **Starter:** $295/month for 3,600 credits and 11 models, adding Claude, Grok, Google AI Mode, DeepSeek and Meta AI. API access and extra credits are paid add-ons with no published price. Annual billing is shown as 17% off.
- **Enterprise:** custom credits, SSO, audit logs, multi-region support and BI-tool dashboards.
- **Action layer:** "AthenaHQ Content" recommends fixes mapped to passages AI models pull from, and "Ask Athena" answers questions about your own data.

The [homepage FAQ](https://athenahq.ai) also lists SOC 2 Type II and GDPR compliance, plus GA4, Search Console and Shopify integrations.

## What does Profound offer in October 2026?

Profound now describes itself as an "AI marketing platform" built around an AI Marketer, a set of agents that research, write and report. Its [Answer Engine Insights](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights) module covers prompt tracking, citations, sentiment and a FactCheck feature for wrong claims. Paid access is quote-only.

What its pricing page states:

- **Trial (free):** limited AI Marketer credits; 50 prompts run daily for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews; a recommended prompt set you cannot customize; no exports or API.
- **Enterprise (custom):** daily tracking of up to nine engines, including Perplexity, Claude and Microsoft Copilot; custom prompts, regions and languages; SSO/SAML, SOC 2, CSV/JSON export, API and dedicated support.
- **Agencies:** a self-serve Agency Growth plan with 400 credits a month per client workspace. We did not verify its price.

The old $99 Starter and $399 Growth cards are gone from that page. Treat any article quoting them, including some vendor comparison pages, as stale.

## How do AthenaHQ and Profound compare side by side?

On the vendors' own pages, AthenaHQ is the easier one to start and price; Profound is the one with a larger engine ceiling and enterprise packaging. The table reflects what each site said on October 3, 2026.

Differences that move the decision are in the first four rows. The rest are close to parity.

## Can you compare AthenaHQ credits with Profound prompts?

Not directly, and neither vendor tells you the cost of a real program. AthenaHQ's [pricing page](https://athenahq.ai/pricing) says one credit equals one AI response; Profound counts tracked prompts and a separate pool of agent credits.

Here is our own arithmetic, not a vendor figure. At one credit per response, 3,600 credits cover 3,600 responses a month, which is 40 prompts on 3 engines checked daily for 30 days. AthenaHQ's [comparison page](https://athenahq.ai/comparison/profound) says some models cost 5 credits per response; at that rate the same credits cover 720 responses.

Its comparison page is dated September 9, 2026 and still lists Profound's $99 and $399 plans. Profound's own page no longer does. Either the plans changed after that date or the comparison is stale, so get Profound's number in writing.

## Which fits your team?

Match the tool to who will act on the data, not to the feature count. A dashboard nobody reads is the most expensive plan on either price list.

- **Small in-house marketing team:** AthenaHQ Starter. You can buy it without a sales call, and it lists Shopify, Webflow, GA4 and Search Console connections that suit lean teams.
- **Agency with several clients:** Profound's agency route, since its self-serve agency plan is organized around client workspaces. Confirm the price in writing, because we could not read it.
- **Large brand with procurement and security review:** Profound Enterprise or AthenaHQ Enterprise. Both state SOC 2 Type II, so compare SSO, audit logs and support terms in the contract.
- **Team still unsure which prompts matter:** neither, yet. Start with the free entry points below.

## How do you test either tool before paying?

Use the free entry points, and test against questions you wrote yourself. A tool you cannot check against your own manual results is a tool you are trusting blindly.

1. Write 15 to 20 questions a buyer would ask, without your brand name in them.
2. Ask them yourself in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity and Google AI, and record who is named.
3. Run the same questions through AthenaHQ's Free plan or Profound's Trial, and compare mentions and sources.
4. Check whether the tool's [recommended fixes](/blog/athenahq-review) match what you can verify.

A tool that cannot reproduce what you saw by hand is not ready to guide spend. Research on [generative engine optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) reports that tactics can raise visibility by up to 40% in generative engine responses, and that effectiveness varies by domain. Measure your own domain before believing any platform's headline lift.
