Lovable pricing is best understood as an iteration budget, not simply a monthly subscription. The visible plan is admission; your practical cost depends on how many changes you make, how much Cloud capacity your live app uses, and how often in-app AI runs. Since June 2026, Lovable has used one shared credit balance across building, Cloud, and in-app AI, without changing plan prices. The useful question is not “Which plan is cheapest?” but “How much product change will this workspace produce?”

## How does Lovable pricing work now?

Lovable combines several types of usage into a credit system. Building changes, Cloud usage, and in-app AI can draw from one broader balance, so the subscription is only the starting constraint. Your actual cost follows the complexity and volume of work.

The June 2026 billing update unified the balance without changing plan prices. A small application can consume credits through repeated UI revisions, authentication, generated images, database-backed features, and use of the deployed app.

See [Lovable pricing](https://lovable.dev/pricing) and [Simplifying billing](https://lovable.dev/blog/simplifying-billing) for current terms. The model described here was checked on September 11, 2026.

## What is a credit?

A credit is a unit of Lovable usage, but it does not represent a fixed amount of work in every plan or task. Credits differ in value by plan, and consumption depends on what Lovable must do.

A color adjustment is different from changing data models, authentication, several screens, and generated assets. A narrowly scoped request can therefore cost less than a broad “redesign the whole app” prompt, even if both produce visible changes.

Lovable’s [Plan Mode documentation](https://docs.lovable.dev/features/plan-mode) states that Plan Mode costs 1 credit per message. That can be useful before an architectural or permission change, because clarifying the implementation may reduce wasteful iterations. Planning is still metered.

Treat credits as a production resource:

- Small changes consume fractions of a credit.
- Larger features consume more.
- Planning messages have a stated cost.
- Cloud and AI usage can draw from the shared balance.
- Credits have different value across plans.

## What is included on Free and paid plans?

The Free plan includes 5 daily build credits, capped at 30 build credits per month, plus 20 Cloud credits per month and 4 AI credits per month. That is useful for testing Lovable’s workflow, but it is not a promise that a complete production app can be built without payment.

A few substantial iterations can consume the monthly build allocation. Cloud and AI introduce additional practical limits, even when the initial prototype is small.

Paid workspaces provide larger allowances under their published terms. Their important team characteristic is that workspace credits are shared across unlimited members. “Unlimited members” does not mean unlimited usage: everyone draws from one finite balance.

| Usage area | Free allowance | Main consideration |
|---|---:|---|
| Build credits | 5 daily, capped at 30/month | Feature and UI iterations |
| Cloud credits | 20/month | Live-app infrastructure and activity |
| AI credits | 4/month | In-app AI usage |
| Paid workspace members | Unlimited members share credits | Team demand uses one pool |

Compare the current details on [Lovable’s pricing page](https://lovable.dev/pricing), rather than relying on an old sticker price.

## How much does a real change consume?

Lovable’s official examples show why iteration count matters. Changing a button color costs 0.50 credits. Removing a footer costs 0.90 credits. Adding authentication costs 1.20 credits. Creating a landing page with three generated images costs 1.70 credits.

These are benchmarks, not a guaranteed calculator. The result can vary with scope and project state. Recoloring one component differs from changing an entire design system; authentication may involve screens and configuration beyond the first request.

Use the examples as rough categories:

- **Micro-change:** similar to a button color adjustment.
- **Small cleanup:** similar to removing a footer.
- **Foundational feature:** similar to adding authentication.
- **Multi-asset page:** similar to a landing page with three generated images.

The practical lesson is to price the number of iterations, not just the number of months. Ten small corrections after every review are part of the budget. A broad first prompt followed by repeated repairs may cost more than a slower, better-scoped build.

## When do Cloud and AI add cost?

Cloud and in-app AI matter when a Lovable project becomes a live product rather than a static prototype. Cloud usage relates to the deployed application’s infrastructure and activity. In-app AI consumes its allowance when builders or users invoke AI features inside the application.

The unified balance makes this interaction easy to miss. A team may budget only for code generation, then find that live-app use competes for the same broader resource. A modest prototype may use little Cloud capacity while consuming many build credits, or the reverse.

Track these categories separately:

- Build credits used to create or modify the app.
- Cloud credits used by the live app.
- AI credits consumed by in-app AI.
- Planning credits used before implementation.
- Rework caused by unclear or oversized requests.

Deployment is not necessarily the end of usage. A successful live application can continue consuming Cloud and AI capacity after the prototype is finished.

## How do shared workspace credits change team economics?

Shared paid-workspace credits avoid calculating a separate allowance for every collaborator. That can suit small teams, agencies, and cross-functional groups where several people contribute occasionally.

It also creates a shared-pool risk. One person’s large experiment can reduce the capacity available to everyone else. An unlimited-member workspace means unlimited access to a common pool, not unlimited independent budgets.

Set simple operating rules:

- Assign an owner to watch the remaining balance.
- Separate experiments from production work.
- Use Plan Mode for architectural or permission changes.
- Review large multi-screen requests before execution.
- Track Cloud and AI alongside build usage.

Compared with conventional per-seat pricing, a shared-credit model can be efficient when usage is uneven. It requires someone to manage the pool.

## What should a pilot measure?

A two-week or one-month pilot should measure completed outcomes, not prompts sent. Record the starting balance, major requests, credits consumed, rework, and whether each result reached a usable state.

Track:

1. Credits per accepted feature.
2. Credits spent on rework.
3. Build, Cloud, and AI usage separately.
4. Time from request to deployed result.
5. Collaborators drawing from the workspace.
6. Remaining balance at the period’s end.

This shows whether Lovable fits your real workflow. A team that produces a working feature in two focused iterations may get strong value. A team that repeatedly requests broad redesigns may consume credits without equivalent progress.

Compare accepted features, not raw credit totals. An inexpensive but unusable change is not efficient, while an expensive authentication feature may be good value if it prevents substantial manual work.

## Which plan should you choose?

Choose Free when you are testing Lovable’s interaction model, building a small proof of concept, or evaluating how clearly your team can describe changes. Its 30-build-credit monthly cap makes it a trial environment, not a dependable production budget.

Choose a paid plan when the workspace needs sustained iteration, multiple collaborators, or meaningful Cloud and AI usage. The right choice depends on published allowances and measured consumption, not on a generic “best” plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Lovable free to use?

Yes. Free includes 5 daily build credits capped at 30 per month, 20 Cloud credits per month, and 4 AI credits per month. That supports workflow testing, not unlimited app development.

### Do Lovable credits expire?

Check the current official billing terms for expiration and rollover details. Rules can change, so do not rely on an old review or assumed renewal behavior.

### Does Lovable charge per seat?

Paid workspace credits are shared across unlimited members instead of assigned as separate paid allowances per member. The workspace still has a finite balance, so additional collaborators can increase demand.

### Does a live Lovable app cost extra?

Cloud and in-app AI usage can consume credits in addition to build work. Budget live-app activity separately because deploying a prototype does not make future Cloud or AI usage free.
