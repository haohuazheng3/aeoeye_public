## What do Geneo credits mean?

Geneo publishes plan allowances in credits, but a credit should be treated as a metering unit rather than a guaranteed “one AI response.” Public pricing alone does not justify a universal conversion between credits and completed prompts, reports, tracked queries, or other outputs.

An AEO workflow can consume capacity differently depending on engines, projects, refresh frequency, markets, and other settings. The rules may also change as Geneo adds features.

Before committing, ask Geneo to clarify four points in writing:

1. What action consumes credits: a query, refresh, project, report, seat, or another event?
2. Do credits roll over, expire, or reset on the monthly billing date?
3. Is pay-as-you-go priced per additional credit, and does it require a separate balance?
4. Which limits apply to seats, domains, competitors, historical data, and exports?

You can forecast cost only after you know what your recurring monitoring loop consumes.

## How can you compare the plans with reproducible math?

You can calculate a nominal capacity ratio by dividing the monthly price by the included credits. This is a transparent comparison of plan economics, not a promised response price.

**Formula:** monthly price ÷ included monthly credits = nominal dollars per included credit.

Using the published figures:

- Pro: $39.90 ÷ 1,000 = **$0.0399 per included credit**.
- Agency Starter: $129 ÷ 5,000 = **$0.0258 per included credit**.
- Agency Growth: $249 ÷ 15,000 = **$0.0166 per included credit** (rounded).
- Agency Scale: $499 ÷ 50,000 = **$0.00998 per included credit**, or about **$0.0100**.

These are **capacity ratios, not guaranteed response costs**. They do not say that a Pro answer costs 3.99 cents, because Geneo may meter actions differently. They show the lower nominal price per included credit if your team uses the full allowance.

The break-even question is equally important. If your organization would use only 2,000 credits in a month, Starter’s full allowance may be unnecessary even though its ratio is lower. Growth or Scale becomes relevant only when recurring volume and the credit rules justify it.

## Which Geneo plan fits an AI visibility workflow?

The free trial fits a product check: inspect the workflow, test a small set of brand questions, and record what you still need to verify. It is not a substitute for a documented measurement plan.

Pro is the clearest starting point for a solo consultant, one brand, or a small marketing team that needs a recurring monthly allowance without immediately buying an agency tier. At $39.90/month, it is easier to evaluate against a defined set of markets and prompts.

Agency Starter is a more plausible fit when multiple clients or brands need regular monitoring. The decision should depend on actual expected credit use, not the attractive ratio alone. ## What should you verify before buying?

Do not purchase on the headline credit count alone. Build a small acceptance checklist around your own AEO workflow:

- **Coverage:** Which AI engines, countries, languages, and query types are supported?
- **Freshness:** How often can data refresh, and is refresh included in the credit allowance?
- **Evidence:** Can you inspect the underlying prompts, cited sources, and timestamps?
- **Collaboration:** How many seats, brands, projects, competitors, and exports are included?
- **Overage:** What does pay-as-you-go cost, and can spend be capped?
- **Retention:** How long are historical results stored, and can you export them?

Run these questions against a fixed pilot design: five buyer questions, two markets, two competitors, and one monthly refresh. Ask Geneo how many credits that design consumes. That answer is more useful than extrapolating from a generic “1,000 credits” label.
