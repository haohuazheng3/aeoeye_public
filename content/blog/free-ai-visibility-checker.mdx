## Can you check your AI visibility for free?

Yes. Three kinds of check cost $0: a manual test you run yourself, a free automated scan, and the data Google and Microsoft already give site owners.

AI visibility is [the frequency and prominence of brand mentions in generated responses](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.07585), as researchers measure it. "Free" here means no card, no sales call and no account. A trial is not free, and a "checker" that wants your phone number before showing a result is a lead form. Lists of "free AI SEO tools" mostly cover keyword, writing and content tools; this guide covers the checks that show whether AI engines name you.

## How do you run a 15-minute manual check?

Write five to ten unbranded buyer questions, ask each three times in a clean session and log every answer. Five questions on one engine take about 15 minutes.

1. **Write questions a stranger would type.** Cover category ("best [category] for [use case]"), comparison ("[competitor] alternatives"), constraint ("[category] that [requirement]") and criteria ("what to look for in a [category]"). Add your city if you serve one. Never include your brand name: asking about it tests recall, not recommendation. Start from real phrasing in [Search Console queries](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553).
2. **Start clean.** Use a signed-out window or a private mode: ChatGPT's Temporary chat set to Unpersonalized, Claude's [incognito chat](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/12260368-using-incognito-chats) (it does not use Claude's memory) or Gemini's [Temporary chat](https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/13275745) (it does not personalize responses). Your own history flatters you.
3. **Test what buyers see.** On each engine your buyers use, test the free default model, not your paid one, and note whether web search ran. In a [University of St. Gallen preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.07585) (Swiss-market prompts, repeated runs, March 2026), 57.8% of ChatGPT runs returned no citations, because it searches only for some queries. An answer without sources probably came from memory. [Google AI Overviews often don't trigger](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), so none appearing is a result.
4. **Ask each question three times, each in a new chat.** In that preprint, cited-source sets overlapped by only 34–42% between consecutive days. Its subsampling analysis puts the 95% interval on how often a brand is named at about ±37 points after three runs and ±16 after seven, and the authors advise at least seven runs per prompt per day. Treat three as a smoke test: enough to expose flicker, not to give you a rate.
5. **Log and tally.** Copy the template below, write "named in 2 of 3" per question, and count which competitors recur. That list is the most useful output.

| Question | Engine | Run | Search ran? | You named? | Position | Your page cited? | Competitors named |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| best [category] for [use case] | ChatGPT | 1 | yes | no | none | no | A, B, C |

## What can a free check tell you, and what can't it?

A free check shows whether you are named for the questions you tried, who is named instead and which pages get cited. It cannot give you a stable rate, your buyers' real phrasing or a trend, so treat it as a smoke test: a clear failure is informative, a lucky pass is not.

- **Your buyers' questions.** Your questions are a sample, and Google says AI Overviews and AI Mode [may fan one question out into several related searches](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), so answers rest on queries you never typed.
- **Direction or cause.** One snapshot shows neither. Re-run monthly, and read the cited sources: they are the nearest thing to a reason.

For scale, in AEOeye's audit records (127 completed audits, 27 brand names, June to September 2026) the audited brand was named in 157 of 892 engine answers, or 17.6%. The sample is self-selected, engine samples are unequal and not comparable, and four of five engines have under 10 mentions, so treat it as scale, not a market rate. When a brand was named and ranked, it came first in 71.4% of 147 ranked mentions, and answers named 2.9 competitors on average ([aggregates file](/resources/aeoeye-recommendation-benchmark-2026-09.json)). Being named at all is the hard part.

## What does "AI citation checker" mean?

It means two different jobs: verifying the citations an AI gave you, so you do not repeat an invented source, or checking whether AI engines cite your site.

**Verifying citations an AI produced.** Open every link behind a claim you will repeat, confirm the page exists and says what the AI says it does, and check author and date separately. A 2023 [Scientific Reports study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-023-41032-5) found 55% of GPT-3.5 and 18% of GPT-4 citations were fabricated (636 checked); those are older models, but the lesson stands. The Tow Center's 2025 test of eight AI search tools found [incorrect answers to more than 60% of queries](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php) tracing news excerpts to their source, plus fabricated links.

**Checking whether AI cites you.** This is the manual test above with the "cited" column filled in. A mention names your brand in the answer text; a citation links your page as a source. Log them separately, because an engine can name you without linking you, and link you without recommending you. For longer-term tracking, see [what AI citation tracking is](/answers/what-is-ai-citation-tracking).

## What should you do with the result?

Match the pattern to the fix: absent, borderline, uncited and misdescribed each need a different move. Then repeat the identical test monthly, with the same questions, engines and run count, to tell change from noise.

| What you see | What it usually means | First move |
|---|---|---|
| Never named; the same competitors recur | Engines have little to draw on for you | Get onto the sources the answers cite, or publish a better answer |
| Named in 1 of 3 runs | Borderline, at the edge of the shortlist | Add corroboration on third-party pages; re-check next month |
| Named, never cited | Known, but your pages are not the source | Publish a page that answers the question first, with specific facts |
| Named, but facts are wrong | Stale or conflicting sources | Fix the pages the answer cites; align your profiles |

Rule out access first. OpenAI [recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) in robots.txt, and allowing its published IP ranges, to appear in ChatGPT search answers.
