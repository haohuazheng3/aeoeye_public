## What does Qwairy cost in 2026?

## What do Qwairy’s plans include?

Each paid tier combines three purchasing units: credits, prompts, and workspaces. Credits govern model consumption; prompts describe the published prompt allowance; workspaces indicate how many separate operating contexts the plan supports.

Do not read “100 prompts” as 100 guaranteed monthly checks. A 100-prompt review on the 1-credit core engine has a different budget profile from the same review on a 6-credit premium model.

Starter is the smallest paid commitment: €65 per month, 1,300 credits, 100 prompts, and one workspace. It is the natural baseline for a single brand or a team that wants to establish a repeatable visibility workflow before coordinating multiple workspaces.

Growth raises the allowance to 3,900 credits and 300 prompts, with five workspaces, for €165 per month. That combination is more relevant when separate brands, markets, or internal teams need their own working areas rather than one shared workspace.

Business provides 10,400 credits, 800 prompts, and 20 workspaces at €374 per month. The larger allowance supports a broader cadence, but the same model-weighting rule remains: premium runs draw down credits faster than core-engine runs.

Enterprise is custom. Because a custom plan is not a published number, treat it as quote-only until you receive a written offer that states the credit, prompt, workspace, billing, and model terms you would actually receive.

## How do Qwairy credits and prompts work?

Credits are the variable-cost control, while prompt capacity is a published ceiling that should not be mistaken for a universal monthly run count. Qwairy’s documented engine pricing is 1 credit for a core engine run and 2–6 credits for premium API models.

Here is a simple calculation using the Starter allowance:

1. A 100-prompt sweep on the 1-credit engine uses 100 credits: `100 prompts × 1 credit = 100 credits`.
2. The same 100-prompt sweep on a 6-credit model uses 600 credits: `100 prompts × 6 credits = 600 credits`.
3. Starter has 1,300 credits, so the remaining credit balance after either sweep would be 1,200 or 700 credits, respectively, before any other activity.

This illustrates consumption, not result quality or guaranteed total sweeps. Prompt capacity does not equal monthly runs across all model mixes. Estimate credit usage per run before committing to a cadence.

A planning sheet should record for each recurring check:

- prompt count;
- engine or model class;
- credits per run;
- workspaces involved; and
- repeat frequency.

That turns a headline allowance into an auditable decision. For AI search visibility work, repeatability matters: a smaller weekly check can be more useful than a one-off prompt count exhausted by premium-model usage.

## What does a Qwairy plan cost with annual billing?

Qwairy documents annual billing as two months free. Using the Starter monthly price, the calculation is €65 × 10 = €650 before tax.

This is before tax; the accessible official copy does not specify tax treatment or fees. Confirm the final checkout total and allowances before paying.

Annual billing can make sense when your visibility program is stable for a year. It is less attractive while you are still deciding on model mix, workspace structure, or cadence. The discount does not solve a capacity mismatch.

## Which Qwairy plan fits your visibility workflow?

Choose by operating design, not by the biggest number in the pricing table. The following framework keeps the purchase tied to actual AI visibility work.

**Choose Free for orientation.** The no-card 100-credit offer is appropriate when you want to inspect the product and learn the workflow before making a paid commitment. It is not evidence that a production monitoring program will fit within a free allowance.

**Choose Starter for one repeatable workspace.** Starter fits a single brand or team that can work inside one workspace and wants 100 published prompts with a 1,300-credit budget. Model selection still determines how quickly that budget is consumed.

**Choose Growth for separation and collaboration.** Growth becomes more plausible when up to five workspaces matter—for example, distinct brands, markets, or teams—and 300 published prompts better matches your planned coverage.

**Choose Business for broader operational coverage.** Business is designed for a larger allowance and up to 20 workspaces. The price is justified only if your operating design can use that breadth; unused workspaces do not automatically create more insight.

**Request Enterprise when requirements are genuinely custom.** Ask for comparable written terms rather than accepting a vague “contact sales” answer. Request the included credits, prompts, workspaces, supported model costs, billing period, and any overage rules in the quote.

## What should you verify before buying Qwairy?

Before checkout, validate the parts most likely to change your real Qwairy cost:

1. Confirm that the displayed EUR price, billing period, and annual discount still match the official pricing page.
2. Map your planned prompt sweep to core versus premium models and calculate credits per sweep.
3. Confirm whether your teams need one workspace or several, and whether workspace separation changes your process.
4. Ask what happens when credits or prompts are exhausted, especially for a recurring monitoring schedule.
5. Separate vendor-documented capacity from your own accuracy test. This guide does not measure how well Qwairy identifies, explains, or tracks AI visibility.
