## What does Visibility.so cost?

Visibility.so’s official [pricing page](https://visibility.so/pricing/), checked September 29, 2026, lists three options. Starter is $166 per month when paid monthly, or $83 per month with annual billing at $996 per year. Growth is $333 per month when paid monthly, or $166 per month with annual billing at $1,992 per year. Enterprise is custom priced. The page describes the product as early access and offers a seven-day trial.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Listed credits |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $166/month | $83/month equivalent; $996/year | 5,000 |
| Growth | $333/month | $166/month equivalent; $1,992/year | 5,000 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Confirm in proposal |

Starter and Growth both list 5,000 credits, so Growth should not be assumed to buy more credits. Compare the confirmed features and scope for each tier. Treat Enterprise as a separate quote; its price cannot be derived from the public table. Confirm checkout still shows the intended plan, term, currency, and amount.

## How much can annual billing save?

Using the listed monthly prices for twelve months, Starter would total $166 × 12 = $1,992. The listed annual price is $996, so the arithmetic difference is $1,992 − $996 = $996 for the year. Growth would total $333 × 12 = $3,996 at the monthly rate. Against the listed $1,992 annual price, the difference is $3,996 − $1,992 = $2,004.

| Plan | Twelve months at monthly rate | Listed annual total | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $1,992 | $996 | $996 |
| Growth | $3,996 | $1,992 | $2,004 |

These are differences between listed rates, not guaranteed final totals; verify taxes, whether annual payment is due upfront, renewal, and cancellation terms. Annual billing can lower the stated total, but choose it only if the product scope and longer commitment fit your needs. For Starter, the listed annual amount equals six monthly payments; for Growth, it is also six monthly payments. That comparison is useful for cash planning, but does not tell you whether you can recover the payment if your needs change midterm.

## What do 5,000 credits mean?

The pricing page lists 5,000 credits for both Starter and Growth, but does not establish that a credit equals an answer, prompt, or completed measurement. The count alone does not tell buyers how many responses they can collect, which engines or regions are covered, or how often items refresh.

Check Visibility.so’s [documentation introduction](https://docs.visibility.so/introduction) for product terminology, then ask how a credit is consumed: per query, engine, refresh, tracked brand, or another unit. Confirm treatment of failed or repeated requests, unused credits, and monthly or annual resets. These details are not established by the published count.

Estimate fit only after the unit is clear. List prompts or topics, engines, markets, refresh cadence, and competitors, then ask the vendor to map that workload to credit consumption in writing, including what happens when the allowance is exhausted. A concrete sample month is more useful than a general assurance that the allowance is sufficient: it lets the team compare its own assumptions with the vendor’s accounting before selecting a tier.

## What does early access mean for buyers?

The pricing page labels Visibility.so early access. Ask whether the listed prices are available to new customers, whether scope may change, and what support commitments apply. The label alone establishes neither a specific limitation nor a guarantee.

The page also says early adopters are grandfathered. Confirm the exact wording, who qualifies, which price or terms are protected, and for how long. Do not assume new purchasers qualify or that the term promises a permanent price; get eligibility, renewal, and plan-change conditions in writing.

The page offers a seven-day trial. Confirm its start and end dates, payment-card requirement, paid conversion, and cancellation steps; assign an owner to track the deadline.

## What should you check before checkout?

Confirm the plan, credit mechanics, and renewal terms before checkout. Save the current pricing and terms with the purchase record.

Before paying, verify:

- The selected tier, currency, monthly or annual billing interval, amount due now, and tax treatment.
- The total annual commitment, renewal date, cancellation process, and any required notice period.
- The definition of a credit, the event that consumes one, reset or expiry rules, and overage handling.
- Engines, regions, refresh frequency, prompt or brand limits, seats, exports, reporting, and support included.
- The exact early-access terms and which early-adopter statements apply to new customers, if any.
- The trial start and end dates, card requirement, billing conversion, and cancellation steps.

Resolve decision-critical questions in writing. For Enterprise, get the complete price and scope in the proposal. For Starter or Growth, check the checkout details against the listed figures and clarify differences.
