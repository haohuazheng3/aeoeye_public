Speechify cost depends on which product you mean. The current Reader pricing page lists a Free plan and Premium at $29 per month, while Studio uses a separate credit-based budget: Free includes 600 credits, Starter is $100 per year, and Creator is $300 per year. Reader is for listening; Studio is for producing audio.

The practical question is not “How many voices do I get?” It is “How many finished reading hours or publishable audio hours will I complete?” A reader should calculate recurring monthly value. A creator should calculate usable, approved audio—including revisions, credit consumption, and commercial-rights requirements.

## What does Speechify Reader cost now?

Speechify Reader currently presents two main choices on its official pricing page: Free at $0 and Premium at $29 per month. Premium lists 1,000+ voices, 60+ languages, and speeds up to 5x.

| Reader plan | Current listed price | Best fit |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | Occasional listening and evaluation |
| Premium | $29/month | Frequent reading with expanded controls |

At $29 monthly, Reader Premium is a recurring subscription, not a one-time purchase. Someone listening several times weekly may see strong value; someone opening it twice a month may pay for unused capacity.

Check the live [Speechify Reader pricing page](https://speechify.com/pricing/) rather than a screenshot or search snippet. Pricing, billing, promotions, taxes, and plan presentation can change. ## Is Speechify Free enough for regular reading?

The Free plan is enough when your immediate goal is testing the reading experience. It lets you decide whether the voices, speed, pronunciation, and workflow fit your material before adding a subscription.

Free is most defensible when:

- You listen occasionally rather than daily.
- You are testing whether text-to-speech fits your routine.
- You need a limited number of documents or sessions.
- You want to compare readers before paying.

Premium becomes easier to justify when listening already has a place in commuting, exercise, household tasks, or review sessions. Tie the value to material completed, not the number of voices advertised.

If Free solves the normal job, keep it. Upgrade only when a recurring limitation interrupts a workflow you use often. Review the current [Speechify Terms](https://speechify.com/terms/) and [Privacy Policy](https://speechify.com/privacy/) before uploading personal, confidential, or proprietary documents.

## What does Speechify Studio cost?

Speechify Studio is a separate product and budget. The current [Studio pricing page](https://speechify.com/pricing-studio/) lists Free with 600 credits, Starter at $100 yearly, and Creator at $300 yearly.

| Studio plan | Current listed price | Positioning |
|---|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | 600 credits |
| Starter | $100/year | Paid creation plan |
| Creator | $300/year | Higher-tier creation plan |

Credits can be consumed by generation, narration, revisions, or other Studio actions. Real cost depends on script length, takes, pronunciation fixes, and the amount approved for publication. Commercial rights also differ across plans, so a personal experimentation tier may not cover marketing, training, client, course, or podcast distribution.

Reader helps consume text through speech; Studio creates audio from a production workflow. Buying Reader Premium does not make Studio unnecessary, and buying Studio does not improve a personal reading workflow.

## Why do older Speechify price quotes conflict?

Older official material remains in search results after packaging changes. A September 2023 Speechify explainer listed Premium at $159 yearly; that is historical and stale for a current purchase, not today’s checkout price.

A company can change packaging, regional offers, billing presentation, or monthly/annual relationships. Use old articles only for context. Before subscribing, recheck the live product page, final checkout, billing cadence, promotion terms, renewal, cancellation, taxes, and region. Record the date of your comparison rather than presenting a past quote as permanent.

## How should you calculate the cost of finished audio?

For Reader, divide monthly price by completed listening hours. For Studio, divide annual plan cost by publishable audio hours. These outcome-based figures reveal whether a headline plan price creates usable value.

`monthly Reader cost ÷ completed listening hours = cost per finished listening hour`

At $29, 30 completed hours are about $0.97 each before taxes; three hours are about $9.67 each. The subscription is identical, but practical value differs.

`annual Studio plan cost ÷ publishable audio hours = cost per finished audio hour`

Include script cleanup, pronunciation corrections, rejected takes, pacing, review, unpublished generations, and the rights required for distribution. Track subscription cost, credits consumed, and approved minutes published. That gives you an operating cost instead of a marketing headline.

## What should you check before cancellation or renewal?

Review actual listening hours for Reader or generated and published minutes for Studio before renewal. If Speechify no longer belongs in your routine, cancellation may save more than switching plans.

Check the current plan, renewal date, scheduled amount, cancellation timing, post-cancellation access, remaining credits, and applicable use rights. Save invoices and confirmations for business expenses. Do not cancel during production without confirming what happens to saved work, credits, exports, and access.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Speechify free?

Yes. Reader lists a Free plan. Studio separately lists a Free plan with 600 credits. Capabilities and limits differ, so check the relevant official page.

### How much does Speechify cost per month?

Reader Premium is listed at $29 monthly as of September 2026. Older annual quotes are historical; verify the current checkout before buying.

### Are Speechify Reader and Studio the same subscription?

No. Reader is for listening, while Studio uses credits for audio creation and has different plan-level commercial rights.

### Can I use Speechify audio commercially?

Commercial use depends on the applicable Studio plan and terms. Review current Studio details and [Speechify Terms](https://speechify.com/terms/) before publishing or delivering audio commercially.
