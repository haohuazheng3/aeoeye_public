## Why are old Hall price pages misleading now?

An indexed page may preserve an earlier price accurately while failing to answer what a new buyer can purchase today. G2 is useful for historical context; Hall’s official site shows the current public route to access. A historical record is not a supplier commitment.

The acquisition changes the procurement question. A buyer needs to know who contracts, what product is delivered, whether Hall remains standalone, and what happens to prior plans. The official site’s waitlist does not answer those points. Without a current written offer, the old figures cannot be used to budget a purchase with confidence.

This is a continuity issue as well as a price issue. AI visibility monitoring often involves tracking changes over time. If a product or supplier changes, buyers need to understand data access, export options, and contract exit terms. A low monthly number has no practical value if the plan cannot be ordered or its deliverable is unclear. Availability is part of price.

## What should a written Hall/Tracksuit quote include?

A useful quote identifies the seller, product, scope, price, and contract term. Ask Tracksuit whether an offer is for Hall, a successor product, or a combined package. Without that information, a number cannot be compared fairly with historical Hall tiers or another monitoring vendor.

Request written confirmation of:

- Product name, contracting entity, current availability to new customers, and any waitlist conditions.
- Included engines, markets, languages, seats, prompts, query volume, and refresh frequency.
- Reporting, exports, integrations, retention, and support included.
- Currency, billing frequency, total fees, minimum term, renewal, taxes, and setup or overage charges.
- Data ownership, deletion terms, service commitments, and what happens if the product changes or is discontinued.
- Expected access timing, with dates identified as commitments or estimates.

Ask the vendor to label details as “not included” or “not yet confirmed” instead of leaving them implied. A scoped evaluation using your prompts or a sample report may help if the vendor can provide one. Until a current offer clarifies availability and terms, hall cost remains unknown to a new buyer.
