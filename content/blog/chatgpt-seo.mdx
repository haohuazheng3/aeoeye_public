ChatGPT SEO is the work of getting ChatGPT to name your brand and link your pages when someone asks a buying question. You can't rank on ChatGPT the way you rank on Google, because it writes one answer instead of listing ten results. You can become the obvious answer: let its crawler in, publish the clearest answer to each buyer question, then get independent sources to say the same about you.

## What does "ChatGPT SEO" mean, and which meaning matters?

It means two jobs: getting ChatGPT to recommend and cite your brand, and using ChatGPT to speed up your own SEO work. The first decides whether buyers hear your name, so it gets most of this guide.

That first job is [answer engine optimization (AEO) or generative engine optimization (GEO)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization). Alongside rankings and clicks, you now track how often ChatGPT names and links you.

## How does ChatGPT decide which brands to mention and cite?

ChatGPT answers either from what it learned in training or from a web search it runs first. When it searches, it rewrites the question into one or more queries, reads the results and writes one answer with citations. OpenAI documents that path, not its ranking formula; anyone selling a formula is guessing.

1. **It decides whether to search.** [OpenAI says](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) ChatGPT may search automatically when a question would benefit from current information. Otherwise it answers from training data, which [includes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7842364-how-chatgpt-and-our-foundation-models-are-developed) public web pages (see [how ChatGPT chooses its sources](/answers/how-does-chatgpt-choose-sources)).
2. **It rewrites the question** into one or more queries for its search providers, and may send more specific follow-ups after reading the first results. The queries that surface you are usually narrower than the buyer's own question.
3. **It reads and answers.** Search responses may include citations and a Sources list; OpenAI warns results and citations can be incomplete, outdated or wrong.

**Where do the results come from?** Outside search providers plus OpenAI's own crawler. OpenAI's help page says ChatGPT search sometimes partners with outside providers and points to Microsoft's and Shopify's privacy policies; its crawler, [OAI-SearchBot](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), also surfaces sites. Seer Interactive's [February 2025 test](https://www.seerinteractive.com/insights/87-percent-of-searchgpt-citations-match-bings-top-results) (100 queries, mostly "best" and "top" searches) found 87% of citations in Bing's top results for the same question, against 56% in Google's results.

Treat "Bing rank equals ChatGPT rank" as a hypothesis, not a law: OpenAI does not say how results are split between providers, and 100 queries is a thin test. Get indexed in both Bing and Google ([does ChatGPT use Bing?](/answers/does-chatgpt-use-bing)).

## What's the difference between being mentioned and being cited?

A mention is your brand name appearing in ChatGPT's answer text. A citation is one of your pages appearing as a linked source. They are independent: ChatGPT can name you without linking you, or link you while recommending someone else.

| | Brand named | Brand not named |
|---|---|---|
| **Page linked** | Best case: recommended and clickable | Cited as evidence while a rival is recommended |
| **Page not linked** | Recommended from training or someone else's page | Invisible for that question |

For buying questions the linked page is often someone else's. A September 2025 [arXiv preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.08919v1) ran 1,000 consumer-ranking prompts through search-enabled AI engines, an OpenAI search model among them. In its U.S. software test, AI answers drew 72.7% of citations from earned media (independent publishers, review and comparison sites) and 26.7% from brand sites, against 45.4% and 43.7% for Google. Much of your visibility is decided on pages you do not own.

## How do you get ChatGPT to recommend your brand?

Work in this order: be reachable, be the clearest answer on your own site, then get independent sources to confirm you. Each step fails silently when the one before it is broken.

1. **Let OAI-SearchBot in.** Allow `OAI-SearchBot` in robots.txt. [OpenAI says](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots) opted-out sites do not appear in ChatGPT search answers, and changes take about 24 hours to apply; GPTBot (training) is a separate switch. Few brands get this wrong: in AEOeye's [crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) (384 well-known sites, 3 October 2026), only 16 (4.2%) blocked OAI-SearchBot.
2. **Check the firewall too.** OpenAI also asks you to let its published IP ranges through your CDN. In our study ([data and method](/resources/ai-crawler-access-study-2026-10.json)), 24 of 88 e-commerce and consumer-brand homepages (27.3%) returned a block or bot challenge to our self-identified crawler, so look for OAI-SearchBot in your logs.
3. **Put the answer in the raw HTML.** A [December 2024 Vercel analysis](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler) found no major AI crawler, OpenAI's included, executing JavaScript, so a claim missing from view-source may be invisible.
4. **Get indexed where its search partners look.** Verify your site in Bing Webmaster Tools and push changes through [IndexNow](https://www.indexnow.org/), a notification protocol Bing supports.
5. **Write the clearest answer on your own site.** Give each narrower question a page: pricing, X vs Y, alternatives to X, who it suits and who it doesn't. Open with the answer, then facts a model can quote: numbers, named sources, direct quotations. In the [GEO benchmark](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (KDD 2024) those additions lifted visibility in generative answers by up to 40%; it used a GPT-3.5-based engine and Perplexity, not ChatGPT search, so read it as a direction. Keep names and prices identical everywhere and show an updated date; [OpenAI tells users](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt) to check when a source was published or updated.
6. **Get confirmed where ChatGPT looks.** Ask your buyer questions, open Sources and list the domains that appear: roundups, review sites, comparison pages and, for some questions, community threads. Earn real coverage there with honest comparisons, customer reviews and useful answers.
7. **Selling products? Feed the shopping layer.** OpenAI says [product results](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11128490-shopping-with-chatgpt-search) are chosen by ChatGPT, not bought; Shopify stores are included and other merchants can apply for a direct product feed.

Never named or linked? Work down the same list: blocked, not indexed, answer buried, not corroborated. Our reading: for most brands the gap is the last item, not the first. For Perplexity and Gemini too, see [how to get your business recommended by AI](/blog/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-ai).

## What doesn't work?

Paying for a recommendation, stuffing keywords and faking reviews fail or backfire, and the OpenAI pages we read give llms.txt no role.

- **Buying a recommendation.** As of October 2026, OpenAI shows ads to some Free and Go users, below the answer and labeled sponsored, and says [ads do not influence what ChatGPT writes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001047-ads-in-chatgpt).
- **Keyword stuffing.** The [GEO paper's full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2311.09735v3) reports little to no improvement from it.
- **Fake reviews and planted threads.** The FTC's [final rule](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials) (August 2024) bans fake reviews, AI-generated ones included, and allows civil penalties against knowing violators.
- **llms.txt.** 173 of the 330 sites we could check (52.4%) publish one, yet the OpenAI search and crawler pages we read never mention it. We keep ours because it is cheap, not because it moved citations.

## How do you track whether ChatGPT recommends you?

Ask the same buyer questions several times each and log how often ChatGPT names you and links you. One answer proves nothing: [OpenAI says](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7842364-how-chatgpt-and-our-foundation-models-are-developed) the model has an inherent element of randomness, and [SparkToro's study](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) (run late 2025, published January 2026) found less than a 1-in-100 chance that ChatGPT or Google's AI returned the same brand list twice.

1. **Write 10 to 20 buyer questions without your brand name:** "best X for Y", "X vs Y", "alternatives to X", "X pricing".
2. **Run each at least five times** (our rule of thumb) in a temporary chat set to Unpersonalized, or logged out. OpenAI says [Memory can shape the search ChatGPT runs](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt), so your own history can flatter or hide you. [ChatGPT also uses approximate location](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/9237897-searching-the-web-with-chatgpt), so name the city in local questions.
3. **Log four things and report percentages, not ranks:** named or not, linked or not, which competitors appeared, and which domains showed in Sources.
4. **Watch referrals.** [OpenAI says](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/12627856-publishers-and-developers-faq) ChatGPT adds `utm_source=chatgpt.com` to links for sites that allow OAI-SearchBot, so analytics can show the clicks.

AEOeye's [free audit](/) is a quick first look: it asks ChatGPT three unbranded buyer questions live and shows the answers and the competitors named. In AEOeye's audit records (127 audits, June to September 2026), ChatGPT named the audited brand in 8 of 161 buyer-style answers (5.0%): a small, self-selected sample with repeat audits, not a market rate.

For hundreds of prompts on a schedule, use a dedicated tracker; [our comparison](/blog/ai-visibility-tracker) lists them. No tool can promise placement, only measure it.

## Can ChatGPT help with your own SEO work?

Yes, for structure and speed, but not for facts or strategy. Use it to cluster keywords, draft briefs and meta tags, write schema markup and check whether a draft leads with the answer.

Check every number it gives you against Search Console or a real keyword tool. [Google's guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/using-gen-ai-content) allows AI-assisted content but warns that mass-produced pages without added value can break its scaled content abuse policy. Our [30 ChatGPT prompts for SEO](/blog/chatgpt-prompts-for-seo) shows what to ask.
