Otterly.ai is often the first stop for small teams dipping a toe into AI visibility monitoring — the category of tools that track how ChatGPT, Perplexity, and other AI engines talk about your brand when someone asks for a recommendation. It's built to be quick to set up and light on the wallet, which is exactly why it shows up on so many *best AI visibility tools* shortlists. This review covers what Otterly.ai actually does, where its add-on pricing gets in the way, and who it fits best — so you can decide in five minutes instead of an afternoon.

## What is Otterly.ai?

Otterly.ai is an AI search monitoring tool aimed at small businesses and solo marketers — quick to set up and affordable, tracking how AI engines mention your brand. You load in a set of prompts — the kind of questions your buyers actually type into ChatGPT or Perplexity when they're comparing options — and Otterly checks how those prompts get answered over time, rather than making you check manually every week.

The job it's doing is the same job every AI visibility tool is trying to do: replace guesswork about whether AI engines recommend you over a competitor with a recurring, structured answer. Where Otterly.ai differentiates is in how it does that — a lighter setup and a lower price point than most enterprise-oriented platforms, built for someone who wants a fast, useful read rather than a full analytics department.

That focus on small businesses and solo marketers isn't an accident — the AI visibility category as a whole skews toward tools built for large enterprise marketing teams with matching budgets, and Otterly.ai was clearly built to fill the gap underneath that.

That trade-off — simplicity over depth — shows up throughout the rest of this review, in the feature list, the pricing model, and in who ends up happiest with the tool.

## Key features

Otterly.ai's feature set centers on prompt tracking, share-of-voice reporting, and citation visibility — with Gemini and Claude coverage sold as add-ons rather than bundled in by default. None of the individual features are unusual for the category; what matters more is how they're packaged and priced.

The core pieces:

- **Prompt tracking** — set up the prompts your buyers would realistically ask an AI engine, and monitor how the answers change week to week.
- **Share of voice** — see how often your brand shows up in AI answers relative to the competitors you name, not just a raw mention count.
- **Citation tracking** — check which sources and domains AI engines are pulling from when they answer questions about your category.
- **Sentiment** — a read on whether your brand's mentions skew positive, neutral, or negative, so you're not just counting mentions blindly.
- **Trend history** — see how your visibility shifts week over week and month over month, instead of relying on a one-time snapshot.
- **Multi-engine coverage** — ChatGPT and Perplexity are covered in the core plans; [Gemini and Claude are priced as add-ons](/answers/otterly-ai-gemini-claude-add-on-cost) rather than included from the start.
- **Reporting** — dashboards and exportable reports built for sharing with a small team or a client, not for feeding a full BI stack.

That add-on structure for Gemini and Claude is the one feature decision that shapes almost everything else about how Otterly.ai fits your budget. It's worth reading closely before you assume "AI visibility monitoring" automatically means every major engine is included from day one.

## Pricing

Otterly.ai is positioned affordably, though some engines may be add-ons — check their site for current pricing before you commit to a plan. Pricing pages change often, so treat any specific figure you see anywhere, including on this page, as a starting point rather than a locked-in number.

What's been consistent, as of 2026: plans scale with prompt volume and engine coverage, the entry-level tiers are built to be approachable for a single marketer or a small business rather than a large marketing org, and Gemini and Claude sit outside the base price as an add-on layer rather than being bundled in with ChatGPT and Perplexity.

That last part matters more than it sounds. Google's AI Overviews and Claude are both an increasingly common part of how buyers research purchases, so skipping them to keep costs down can leave a real blind spot in exactly the engines your competitors might be showing up in instead. Our breakdown of [what the Gemini/Claude add-on actually costs](/answers/otterly-ai-gemini-claude-add-on-cost) walks through how to weigh that trade-off before you buy, rather than discovering it after your first invoice.

If you're evaluating the cost, the smart move is to price out the plan with every engine you actually need included, add-ons and all, rather than anchoring on the lowest advertised number — that's the figure that will actually show up on your card each month.

## Who Otterly.ai is best for

Otterly.ai is best for small teams and solo marketers who want an easy, affordable entry into AI visibility monitoring — not for teams that need every major engine covered under one price from day one.

If you're a solo marketer, a founder, or a small in-house team who mainly wants a directional answer to "does AI search even mention us, and how do we compare to the obvious competitor?", Otterly.ai does that job with very little setup time and a gentle learning curve. You don't need a dedicated analyst to read the dashboard or make sense of the reports it generates.

Where it stops being enough is less about the tool being bad and more about scope. Agencies juggling several client accounts, or brand teams that need Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Overviews included from day one rather than layered on later, will likely find themselves paying more than the sticker price suggests once every engine is added up. ## Pros and cons

**Pros:**

- Fast to set up — you can be tracking prompts within minutes, not days of onboarding calls.
- Genuinely affordable relative to enterprise-grade AI visibility platforms aimed at larger marketing teams.
- Solid core coverage of ChatGPT and Perplexity, the two engines most buyers reach for first.
- Clean, digestible reporting that doesn't require a data analyst to interpret it.

**Cons:**

- Gemini and Claude cost extra, so your real monthly bill can climb well past the advertised starting price.
- Analysis depth is lighter than platforms built for larger, multi-brand, multi-market operations.
- The add-on model means budgeting for "full engine coverage" takes an extra step that's easy to miss until your first renewal.
- Because the category is evolving fast, feature parity with larger platforms can shift quickly — worth re-checking before you renew a longer-term plan.
