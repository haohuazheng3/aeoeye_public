Trakkr pricing starts at $100 per month for Growth, with a $1,000 annual option, and $500 per month for Scale, with a $5,000 annual option. Enterprise starts at $1,000 per month when billed annually. The practical buying question is not simply “How many prompts do I get?” It is whether the plan gives your team repeatable coverage across the brands, prompts, AI models, seats, and evidence exports you need.

This is a documentation-based buyer guide, not a hands-on review. AEOeye did not run a paid Trakkr account or test output accuracy. The figures and plan features below come from Trakkr’s official [pricing page](https://trakkr.ai/pricing) and [FAQ documentation](https://trakkr.ai/learn/docs/faq), checked on October 2, 2026. Treat the vendor documentation as the source of truth for a purchase, especially for Enterprise terms and any feature changes.

## What does Trakkr cost in 2026?

Trakkr’s published entry point is Growth at $100 per month, while Scale is $500 per month and Enterprise starts at $1,000 per month billed annually. The exact published prices are:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing | Included brands | Prompt allowance | Seats and notable features |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Growth | $100/month | $1,000/year | 1 | 50 prompts per brand | 3 seats, 25 article credits/month, MCP |
| Scale | $500/month | $5,000/year | 10 | 50 prompts per brand | Unlimited seats, 100 article credits, white-label client portal, REST API |
| Enterprise | Starts at $1,000/month | Billed annually | Custom | Custom | Custom volume, security, and support |

For context, the plans are designed around recurring AI-search visibility monitoring rather than a one-time audit. ## What does each Trakkr plan include?

Growth provides one brand, 50 prompts per brand, daily tracking, all eight listed models, 25 article credits per month, three seats, and MCP. The eight models are ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Grok, DeepSeek, and Meta AI.

Scale keeps the 50-prompts-per-brand allowance and expands the account to 10 brands. It includes all eight models, 100 article credits, unlimited seats, a white-label client portal, and a REST API. Those collaboration and integration features matter when monitoring is part of a client reporting process or an internal data workflow.

The strongest reason to pay for a monitoring platform is reproducible coverage and exportable evidence—not a large prompt count alone. A prompt library should represent real buyer questions, categories, competitors, and locations. Use the [measuring AI visibility](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) framework to decide what your prompts need to prove.

## How many data points can Trakkr produce?

Trakkr describes one prompt run against one model as one data point. Using the vendor’s 30-day math, Growth can produce 12,000 data points and Scale can produce 120,000 data points, assuming the stated prompt, model, brand, and daily-tracking dimensions.

The calculations are straightforward:

- Growth: 50 prompts × 8 models × 30 days = 12,000 prompt-model data points.
- Scale: 10 brands × 50 prompts per brand × 8 models × 30 days = 120,000 prompt-model data points.

These are volume calculations, not accuracy or outcome claims. They do not establish that every run will produce a useful citation, a recommendation, or a business result. They also do not add unlisted overages, taxes, extra models, or additional tracking frequencies. For a serious program, define what a data point means in your reporting: a response, a mention, a citation, a rank observation, or another vendor-defined event.

## How much do Trakkr annual plans save?

Annual billing saves $200 on Growth and $1,000 on Scale compared with paying the published monthly price for twelve months.

| Plan | Twelve months at monthly price | Published annual price | Annual saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Growth | $100 × 12 = $1,200 | $1,000 | $200 |
| Scale | $500 × 12 = $6,000 | $5,000 | $1,000 |

That is a 16.7% reduction from the monthly equivalent for each listed annual plan, based only on the published prices. The annual option makes sense when you already know the monitoring program will run for the year and the included allowances match your forecast. Monthly billing costs more over a full year but preserves a shorter commitment for a new workflow.

## Which Trakkr plan fits a brand or agency?

Growth is the clearest fit for one in-house brand that needs daily, cross-model monitoring and can work within three seats. Confirm how Trakkr counts a parent company, product line, or client portfolio as a “brand.”

Scale is the more natural fit for an agency or an in-house team managing several brands. Ten brands, unlimited seats, a white-label client portal, and a REST API support client-facing reporting and system integration. The price jump is substantial, so buy Scale for those operational requirements—not merely because its headline data-point number is larger.

Enterprise belongs in a custom evaluation for greater volume or specific security and support requirements. Before selecting any plan, document required brands, daily prompts, models, collaborators, integrations, and exportable evidence.

## What should you check about the trial and contract?

The Growth trial lasts 14 days and requires a card. That means the trial is not a no-payment-method evaluation. Confirm when the trial starts, what happens at its end, which plan it converts to, and how cancellation works before entering payment details.

Growth includes three seats, and extra Growth seats cost $20 per month. Calculate seat needs before comparing Growth with Scale: a low base price can become less attractive if several collaborators need access. The published information here does not establish extra-seat pricing for Scale or Enterprise, so request those details rather than extrapolating.

The documentation also does not establish taxes, overage prices, refunds, service levels, data retention, or contract rights. Keep a written record of the quote, billing cadence, cancellation terms, and promised security or support scope.

## What should you verify before buying Trakkr?

Verify the operational details that determine whether the numbers become useful evidence. Start with prompt ownership: can you edit, organize, and preserve a stable set of buyer questions? Then ask how daily tracking is represented in exports and whether historical observations remain comparable when a model changes.

Next, confirm the eight named models, how Google AI Overviews is represented, how brands are separated, and whether geography or language variations consume the prompt allowance.

Finally, verify evidence and workflow. Ask for a sample export or documentation of the API and MCP behavior, without treating an example as a performance guarantee. Clarify who can access white-label reporting, how seats are counted, and what support covers. A monitoring tool earns its cost when your team can reproduce a finding, explain its limits, and act on it.

### How many brands does Trakkr Scale support?

Scale supports 10 brands, with 50 prompts per brand and all eight listed models.
