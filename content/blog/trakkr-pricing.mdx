Trakkr costs $100 a month for Growth (one brand, 50 prompts, eight AI models) and $500 a month for Scale (ten brands), or $1,000 and $5,000 a year on annual billing. Enterprise starts at $1,000 a month. Our verdict for a small business: not as a first purchase. Pay once a one-time baseline shows AI engines skipping you and someone will act on the data weekly.

This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026: Trakkr's [pricing page](https://trakkr.ai/pricing), [billing guide](https://trakkr.ai/learn/docs/account/billing), [FAQ](https://trakkr.ai/learn/docs/faq) and [terms](https://trakkr.ai/terms), checked on 3 October 2026. We have not tested Trakkr hands-on, so nothing here judges its data quality. Prices are in USD.

## What is Trakkr, and what does it cost in 2026?

Trakkr (trakkr.ai) is a subscription tracker for AI visibility: every day it asks your chosen buyer questions to eight AI models and records which brands each answer names, and in what order. Growth costs $100 a month, Scale $500, Enterprise from $1,000.

"AI visibility" means how often and how prominently an assistant names your brand when someone asks a buying question, [as Trakkr defines it](https://trakkr.ai/learn/docs/faq). The eight models are ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, DeepSeek, Grok, Meta AI and Google AI Overviews, on every paid plan.

You may still see $79 for Growth and about $399 for Scale quoted elsewhere; an earlier version of our own answer used them too. They are not on Trakkr's pricing page today, and the billing guide says older subscriptions can stay on an earlier price, so your own Billing tab is what counts.

What we saw on trakkr.ai itself: in AEOeye's crawler access study (one fetch on 3 October 2026), its [robots.txt](https://trakkr.ai/robots.txt) names ten AI crawlers, including GPTBot, ClaudeBot and PerplexityBot, and blocks none; it also publishes an [llms.txt](https://trakkr.ai/llms.txt). That shows openness to AI crawlers, not a verdict on the product. The [study method](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) is public.

## What does each Trakkr plan include?

Growth gives one brand, 50 prompts, all eight models and three seats for $100 a month. Scale gives ten brands, the same 50 prompts each, and unlimited seats for $500. Both refresh daily and include competitor and citation tracking, perception analysis, shared dashboards, and CSV and Google Sheets export.

| | Growth | Scale | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Monthly price | $100 | $500 | From $1,000, billed annually |
| Annual price | $1,000 | $5,000 | Custom contract |
| Brands | 1 | 10 | Unlimited |
| Prompts per brand | 50 | 50 | Unlimited |
| Seats | 3 (extra $20 each) | Unlimited | Custom |
| Also included | MCP access | Growth's, plus REST API, Looker Studio, agency workspace, white-label client portal | SSO, security review, priority support SLA |

Scale buys breadth, not depth. [Trakkr's pricing FAQ](https://trakkr.ai/pricing) says per-brand depth is identical on both plans, so one brand that needs more prompts should add a prompt pack to Growth. MCP, the [Model Context Protocol](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/introduction), is an open standard for connecting AI applications to external systems.

## What do add-ons and annual billing do to the real price?

The headline price is a floor. Extra brands cost $50 a month each, extra Growth seats $20, and extra prompts $39, $59 or $99 per brand for 50, 100 or 200 more. Annual billing charges ten months for twelve, saving $200 on Growth and $1,000 on Scale.

| Growth setup | Monthly cost |
|---|---|
| Base: 1 brand, 50 prompts, 3 seats | $100 |
| Plus 50 prompts | $139 |
| Plus 200 prompts | $199 |
| Plus 1 extra brand | $150 |
| 9 brands (8 extra) | $500 |

The [billing guide](https://trakkr.ai/learn/docs/account/billing) lists these add-ons, says older subscriptions may pay $39 per extra brand, and says Trakkr previews the exact total before you confirm. At nine brands, Growth with add-ons costs the same $500 as Scale's ten; beyond that Scale is cheaper and adds the API and client portal.

The annual discount is modest: Growth is $1,200 on monthly billing against $1,000 annually, Scale $6,000 against $5,000, a 16.7% cut Trakkr rounds to 17%. The [terms](https://trakkr.ai/terms) say annual plans are billed upfront and renew automatically. We would start monthly; annual pays only if you keep the tool ten months anyway.

## How much data does each plan produce?

Trakkr counts one prompt sent to one model as a data point. Growth's 12,000 a month is 50 prompts × 8 models × 30 days; Scale's 120,000 is the same sum across 10 brands. That is capacity, not accuracy.

Trakkr's FAQ suggests starting with 5 to 20 questions. At 15 prompts, Growth yields 3,600 data points a month, about 2.8 cents each, against 0.8 cents at full use, for the same $100. You pay for slots, not usage.

Daily repetition is what the subscription really buys. In [SparkToro's study](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/), 600 volunteers ran 12 prompts through ChatGPT, Claude and Google's AI results 2,961 times. The tools rarely gave the same brand list twice (under 1 time in 100) and almost never the same order (about 1 in 1,000). The authors called visibility percentage across dozens to hundreds of repeated prompts a reasonable metric, and ranking position unreliable.

So read Trakkr's presence figure, the share of prompts that name you, before its position-weighted visibility score (10 points for first place, down to 1 for tenth); the FAQ defines both. SparkToro also lists as unresolved whether API calls match what real users see.

## Is Trakkr worth it for a small business?

For most small businesses, not yet. $1,200 a year ($1,000 annually) only pays when AI answers already cost you buyers and someone acts on the data weekly. Start with a baseline, then decide.

Pay when all three are true:

- **Buyers ask AI before buying.** [Pew Research](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) found Google users who saw an AI summary clicked a regular result in 8% of visits, against 15% without one, and a link inside the summary in 1% (900 US adults, March 2025). If the answer names a rival, the click rarely comes back.
- **A baseline shows a gap.** Competitors are named for your buyer questions and you are not.
- **Someone owns the follow-up.** Trakkr's FAQ says Perplexity and AI Overviews can change within days, while ChatGPT and others usually take weeks to months.

Wait if you would look at it monthly, have a handful of buyer questions, cannot edit pages, or only want to know whether you are named. Otherwise $1,200 a year buys a dashboard nobody reads.

Break-even is annual cost divided by gross profit per new customer. At $1,200 a year and $600 profit per customer, Trakkr must help win two extra customers; at $60 profit, twenty.

The 14-day trial is a fit test, not a results test. It needs a card, converts to $100 a month unless you cancel, and upgrading mid-trial ends it and charges immediately, per the billing guide. Check that your questions return real competitor lists, and set a day-12 reminder.

## What should you verify before buying Trakkr?

Verify six things in writing before you enter a card.

1. **What counts as a brand.** The pages we read do not say how a second location, product line or client maps to a brand; each extra one is $50 a month.
2. **Markets and prompts.** Each tracked market runs as a separate analysis, with extra markets priced from a live invoice preview. Only active prompts count toward the 50.
3. **API versus real apps.** Trakkr says it queries the providers' own APIs, and AI Overviews from live Google results. Run your top three questions in ChatGPT yourself and compare the brands named with Trakkr's report.
4. **Your own price.** Your Billing tab, not any web page including this one, is authoritative.
5. **Refunds, renewal and tax.** New subscribers get a 14-day money-back guarantee; ask when the 14 days start. Renewals are automatic, and prices exclude VAT, GST and other taxes.
6. **Exports and exit.** CSV and Sheets export is on both plans, the REST API only on Scale, and after cancelling you get 30 days to export your data.

Compare tools on cost per active prompt and engines covered, not headline price. At full use Growth is $2 per prompt a month ($100 ÷ 50) and Scale $1 ($500 ÷ 500); our [best AI visibility tools](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) guide compares the alternatives.
