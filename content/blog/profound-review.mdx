Profound is an enterprise platform that tracks how AI answer engines talk about your brand every day, and since 2026 it also sells an "AI Marketer" that drafts and updates content. It is not hard to start, but it is hard to run well: the work is choosing prompts and acting on the data. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on.

## What is Profound, and what has it become?

Profound began as an AI visibility tracker and now describes itself as an AI marketing platform. Its [homepage](https://www.tryprofound.com/) pitches an AI Marketer, a teammate that finds work worth doing, drafts it and asks for approval, on top of the original monitoring.

The tracking core is the same job the field calls [generative engine optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization) (GEO), also known as answer engine optimization (AEO): improving how often your brand appears in AI-generated answers. Profound's pricing FAQ is explicit that it is not only that. Asked "Is Profound only for AI search?", it answers no, and lists research, campaign shaping and content work.

That shift matters when you read older reviews. Many describe a monitoring dashboard with Starter and Growth price cards. In our check on October 3, 2026, the pricing page showed neither.

## What are Profound's main features?

Profound has five product areas: Answer Engine Insights, Prompt Volumes, Agent Analytics, Shopping, and the agent layer. Each solves a different problem, and you can need one without the others.

- **[Answer Engine Insights](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights)** is the tracker. It reports visibility score, share of voice, average position, citations and sentiment, plus FactCheck, which compares what AI says about you with your own source of truth.
- **Prompt Volumes** estimates what people ask AI engines, drawn from what Profound says are 1.5 billion-plus real conversations. It is keyword research for prompts.
- **Agent Analytics** reads server or CDN logs to show which AI bots crawl your site and whether visits convert. It does not use a JavaScript tag.
- **Shopping** tracks product visibility in ChatGPT Shopping at SKU level, including which merchant owns checkout.
- **Agents, Context Manager and AI Marketer** run content workflows (refresh pages, write FAQs, brief competitive gaps) with a human approval step before publishing.

Profound says its tracker captures answers from the consumer experience in the browser, not from API calls, and runs every prompt daily because AI platforms "don't return the same answer twice". Independent research backs the daily-repeat logic: [SparkToro's study](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) found under a 1-in-100 chance that ChatGPT or Google's AI repeats the same brand list across two responses.

## Is Profound hard to use?

Profound's own pages say setup is "Instant", and that part is plausible. What is hard is everything after sign-up: deciding what to track, reading the metrics, and wiring up data sources. The difficulty is in the decisions, not the interface.

Here is what the documentation shows:

- **You build the prompt set.** The [help-center guide](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/4881350168-how-to-create-topics-prompts-to-track-in-profound) tells you to define overarching topics, expand each into prompts (informational, comparative, problem-solving, transactional), and upload them. It suggests 5-10 topics to start, then around 15 topics with about 10 prompts each. That is roughly 150 prompts you must write, and weak prompts produce weak data.
- **Data is not instant.** Visibility, share of voice and citation metrics fill in after the first 24-48 hour cycle; the guide calls an initial "No Data" status normal.
- **Metrics need interpretation.** [Visibility score](https://help.tryprofound.com/articles/3443229936-answer-engine-insights-overview) is your mentions divided by responses naming at least one brand. Share of voice is your mentions divided by all brand mentions. Sentiment and position stack on top.
- **Agent Analytics needs infrastructure access.** The [getting-started guide](https://docs.tryprofound.com/agent-analytics/self-serve-onboarding) requires access to your hosting provider and routes you to Cloudflare, Vercel, CloudFront, Fastly or another log integration.
- **Profound teaches its own tool.** [Profound University](https://university.tryprofound.com/) lists a "Profound 101" course of 272 minutes and a two-week pod that ends in certification.

Our view: that is fair for a platform with this many modules, but it describes a tool for someone whose job includes AI search. If nobody owns that job, the dashboards will go unread, however clean the design.

You may also read that Profound has no trial and needs one to three weeks before data appears. The first is out of date; the pricing page lists a free Trial. The second does not appear in Profound's documentation, which says 24-48 hours for first metrics. We could not load G2's review pages (the server refused the request), so we quote no review scores.

## How much does Profound cost?

As of October 3, 2026, Profound's pricing page lists a free 7-day Trial and a quote-only Enterprise plan for brands, so there is no public monthly price. The Trial runs 50 prompts daily for seven days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, and its prompts cannot be customized.

Enterprise is "Custom": a tailored prompt plan, up to nine engines, API access and exports, SSO and dedicated support. Agents run on credits, and the page says overage can be billed or paused. Agencies have separate plans. For the arithmetic and the old Starter and Growth prices that have disappeared, read our [Profound pricing guide](/blog/profound-pricing).

## What did we see on Profound's own site?

On October 3, 2026, Profound's marketing site did not block AI crawlers, but it also showed little machine-readable help for them. This is a neutral observation from AEOeye's [crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory), not a verdict on the product.

"None in served HTML" can mean schema is injected by JavaScript, which Google renders and many AI crawlers do not, so we do not call it "no schema". Profound's own [Dynamic Bot Rendering docs](https://docs.tryprofound.com/dynamic-bot-rendering/overview) make the same point about client-rendered pages, and add that rendering "does not guarantee inclusion or citation in an answer".

## Who is Profound best for?

Profound fits enterprises and agencies with a named owner for AI search, a budget for a quoted contract, and a use for logs, shopping data and content agents. It is a poor first purchase for a small team that only wants to know whether AI engines recommend it.

Choose it if most of these are true:

- Someone's job description includes AI visibility reporting.
- You manage many brands or clients and need all-time history, exports and API access.
- You sell products through ChatGPT Shopping, or need to trace AI-cited misinformation back to its sources.
- You will use agents to change content, not only watch scores.

Skip it, for now, if you have one site, no analyst, and one question. A tracker cannot tell you which question to ask.
