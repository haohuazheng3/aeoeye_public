Manus AI pricing is best understood as a variable credit budget, not a simple “messages per month” subscription. Free is useful for testing; Pro makes sense for frequent individual work; Team is for shared usage, administration, and collaboration. Because Manus can change checkout prices and plan limits, verify the live [official pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing) before paying.

## Verdict: who should pay for Manus?

Manus is worth paying for when you repeatedly use it to complete multi-step work that would otherwise consume substantial time. That might include research, document production, spreadsheet analysis, coding tasks, or workflows that require an agent to browse, reason, create files, and revise its output.

Do not buy Pro because “more AI” sounds useful. Buy it when the free allowance has exposed a repeatable workflow and you can estimate how often you will run it. If you are still exploring what Manus can do, Free is the sensible starting point.

Pro is the likely choice for a solo professional who needs a larger monthly pool and full capabilities. Team is justified when several people need a shared pool, administrative controls, or a collaborative workspace. It is not automatically better value for one person.

For context, compare this decision with [ChatGPT Enterprise pricing](/blog/chatgpt-enterprise-pricing), [Perplexity Pro pricing](/blog/perplexity-pro-pricing), and [OpenAI Codex pricing](/blog/openai-codex-pricing). Manus is especially relevant when you want an agent to execute a project, not merely answer a question.

## What do Manus credits mean?

Manus uses credits to meter agent work. Credits are consumed as Manus performs tasks, and the amount used depends on what the task requires: its complexity, duration, tools, and computational resources.

That means a credit is not equivalent to one prompt, one message, or one guaranteed task. A short request may use relatively little, while a request involving extensive browsing, file generation, repeated revisions, or code execution can consume substantially more.

The [Manus plans documentation](https://manus.im/docs/introduction/plans) explains the plan structure and should be your reference for current allowances and capabilities.

### What do credits not guarantee?

Credits do not guarantee a fixed number of successful deliverables. They also do not make every task equally expensive. A monthly pool can run out faster than expected if you assign Manus open-ended work without constraints.

Credits should therefore be treated like a usage budget. Define the outcome, limit unnecessary iterations, provide the right source material, and ask for a staged plan when a project is large. Those habits improve both quality and predictability.

## How do Free, Pro, and Team compare?

The practical difference between plans is access, capacity, and how usage is organized. Exact checkout prices and limits are dynamic, so confirm them on the official site rather than relying on a screenshot, old review, or claimed fixed amount.

| Plan | Best for | Credit model | Capability and workflow fit | Main limitation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | First-time users and occasional experiments | Limited monthly use | Test Manus and learn whether agent workflows fit | Limited capacity can interrupt repeated work |
| Pro | Frequent individual users | Larger monthly credit pool | Full capabilities for recurring professional or technical work | One person still owns the budget |
| Team | Small groups and organizations | Shared credits | Shared usage, administration, and collaboration | Shared consumption needs internal discipline |

The table is intentionally qualitative because exact numbers can change. Manus’s [live pricing page](https://manus.im/pricing) is the authority for current checkout terms.

### Is Free enough for most people?

Free is enough if your goal is evaluation, occasional experimentation, or a small number of bounded tasks. It is also useful for learning how to phrase requests so Manus does not spend credits on avoidable exploration.

Free becomes frustrating when you depend on Manus for deadlines, run several substantial tasks each month, or need capabilities reserved for paid access. The question is not whether Free is “bad”; it is whether its limited allowance creates more friction than the subscription is worth.

### What does Pro add for a solo user?

Pro is designed for a user who wants a larger monthly pool and full capabilities. The value is continuity: you can assign meaningful work more regularly instead of treating every run as a scarce experiment.

Pro makes sense when Manus saves enough time to cover manual work. Infrequent users may leave a larger pool unused.

### When does Team make sense?

Team is for shared work, not merely a larger personal allowance. Shared credits help a group coordinate usage, while administration and collaboration matter when multiple people need consistent access and oversight.

A team should establish lightweight rules: which tasks are business-critical, who can run resource-heavy jobs, and how unfinished or duplicate runs are handled. Without them, a shared pool can disappear without producing proportional value.

## Why is Manus AI hard to budget?

AI agents are difficult to budget because the requested outcome hides the work required. “Create a market report” could mean a short synthesis of supplied documents or hours of browsing, comparison, extraction, formatting, and revision.

Three variables make usage unpredictable:

1. **Task complexity:** More decisions, files, constraints, and dependencies usually require more work.
2. **Resources:** Browsing, code execution, file handling, and other tools add execution effort.
3. **Iteration:** Multiple rewrites, expansions, or corrections extend the run.

This is why “How many tasks does Pro include?” is usually the wrong question. Ask instead: “How many tasks of my typical type can my monthly pool support?”

For better control, start with a narrow brief, provide authoritative inputs, and separate research from production. Use the [LLM Brand Visibility Checker](/tools/llm-brand-visibility-checker) when the goal is evaluating how AI systems represent a brand.

## What will different workloads cost in credits?

Exact consumption varies, so these are workload patterns—not promised prices or fixed credit estimates.

### Scenario 1: a short research brief

You ask Manus to summarize supplied documents, identify differences, and produce a two-page brief with citations. The source set is known, the format clear, and revisions limited. Free may handle occasional briefs. Pro becomes attractive if you produce them weekly or need reliable access during busy periods.

### Scenario 2: a competitive market report

You ask Manus to research competitors, compare positioning, collect evidence, and produce a report. This can justify Pro. Specify the competitor list, date range, fields, and length first.

### Scenario 3: a multi-step technical project

You ask Manus to inspect a codebase or dataset, diagnose an issue, create an artifact, test it, and revise it. The agent may need several tools and execution cycles.

For an individual, Pro may be appropriate if this work is regular. For a team, shared credits and collaboration may matter more than one person’s allowance. Divide large projects into milestones so you can stop, inspect, and redirect before unnecessary work accumulates.

## When should you upgrade?

Upgrade when you hit Free’s limit during valuable work, access interruptions cost more than the plan, you need paid capabilities, or you can identify a repeatable workflow with measurable time savings.

Do not upgrade because a task failed. Determine whether the problem was unclear instructions, poor sources, unsuitable workflow, or insufficient access.

For Team, add one trigger: multiple people need shared access and centralized administration. If only one person uses Manus, Team may add complexity without value.

## What happens when you cancel or have unused credits?

Cancellation and unused-credit rules are account-specific terms that can change. Before subscribing, check the current billing language shown at checkout and in Manus’s official plan documentation.

Do not assume unused monthly credits roll over, convert to cash, or remain available indefinitely. Confirm the effective date, remaining access, renewal behavior, and treatment of unused credits in the live terms.

Review usage near the end of each billing period. If you consistently leave much of the pool unused, downgrade or stay on Free. If you repeatedly run out before important work is finished, upgrade only after identifying which workflows consume the budget.
