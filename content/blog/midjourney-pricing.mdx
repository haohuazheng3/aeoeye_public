Midjourney’s plan ladder is a capacity-and-privacy ladder: Basic buys limited Fast access, Standard adds relaxed image throughput, and Pro/Mega add higher concurrency plus private generation. The right choice depends on iteration volume, delivery speed, privacy requirements, and commercial terms.

Basic suits casual creators learning the workflow. Standard is usually the practical starting point for recurring image production because Relax Mode makes exploration inexpensive. Pro serves teams and professionals needing privacy or parallel production; Mega makes sense only when measured demand consistently exceeds Pro’s capacity.

## How much does Midjourney cost?

Midjourney lists four auto-renewing plans: Basic $10, Standard $30, Pro $60, and Mega $120 per month, with 20% annual savings paid upfront. Annual prices are $96, $288, $576, and $1,152. Midjourney’s official comparison is the best reference for current limits, modes, and rights. [Compare Midjourney plans](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans)

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Fast GPU time | Relax image generation | Stealth Mode |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| Basic | $10 | $96 | 3.3 hours | No | No |
| Standard | $30 | $288 | 15 hours | Unlimited | No |
| Pro | $60 | $576 | 30 hours | Unlimited | Yes |
| Mega | $120 | $1,152 | 60 hours | Unlimited | Yes |

Annual billing lowers the effective monthly price when you expect to subscribe for a year. Monthly billing preserves flexibility while you validate the workflow. The [official comparison](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans) says the annual discount requires paying the full year upfront. Teams should treat a plan as production capacity, not simply a software seat: the key question is how much approved work it helps produce.

## How much GPU time does each plan include?

Fast GPU time—not image count—is the scarce unit. Basic includes 3.3 hours, Standard 15, Pro 30, and Mega 60 each month. Extra Fast time costs $4 per hour. Standard, Pro, and Mega include unlimited image generations in Relax Mode; Pro and Mega also include unlimited SD video generations in Relax Mode. [See Midjourney’s speed and mode guidance](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32199405667853-Speed)

“How much does Midjourney cost?” is therefore a workload question. One concept can consume many generations through composition, consistency, aspect-ratio, reference, and variation tests. A few carefully planned prompts may use little Fast time.

Use Fast when deadlines, launches, or reviews make responsiveness valuable. Use Relax when work is exploratory and queue delays can be absorbed. Turbo can prioritize speed where available, but it consumes GPU time faster, so reserve it for moments when waiting has a real business cost.

A useful model is:

`monthly capacity cost = subscription price + (extra Fast hours × $4)`

Then connect it to output:

`cost per approved asset = monthly capacity cost ÷ approved production assets`

The second figure matters more than cost per generation. If a $30 plan produces 20 approved campaign assets, its practical cost is $1.50 per approved asset before labor. If a cheaper plan causes more waiting, rework, or coordination, its savings may disappear.

## When does Relax Mode change the economics?

Relax Mode makes Standard the practical floor for high-volume image exploration when the team can tolerate queue speed. Basic has no Relax access, so every generation uses its limited Fast allocation. Once that allocation is exhausted, Basic is a poor fit for repeated ideation.

Standard’s unlimited Relax images suit moodboards, visual directions, social concepts, thumbnails, and early campaign development. These activities need breadth more than immediate delivery. Designers can submit multiple directions, return later, and move the strongest concepts into Fast for refinement.

Relax is less suitable when a creative director is waiting during a live review. The advantage appears when queued work can run alongside other tasks. Estimate how often results must be immediate versus background exploration.

A simple policy keeps Standard predictable:

- Run broad ideation and low-confidence variations in Relax.
- Reserve Fast for shortlisted concepts, client-facing iterations, and deadline-sensitive fixes.
- Track approved assets, not just generations.
- Review whether Fast time is being spent on avoidable experimentation.

Standard’s value is not merely its larger allowance; it separates urgent production from inexpensive exploration. Midjourney's [speed guide](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32199405667853-Speed) should be rechecked when estimating that split because mode behavior is a product rule, not a permanent assumption.

## When do concurrency and privacy justify Pro or Mega?

Pro and Mega earn their price when parallel production or private client work matters because Stealth Mode is restricted to those tiers. Pro and Mega provide 12 concurrent Fast image jobs, compared with 3 on lower plans. For video, Pro allows 6 concurrent jobs and Mega allows 12. [Review the official limits matrix](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/27870484040333-Comparing-Midjourney-Plans)

Concurrency matters when several people, tabs, or production stages run simultaneously. A studio may explore concepts while refining approved directions; a marketing team may prepare multiple campaigns. Frequent queuing can interrupt reviews and make scheduling unreliable.

Privacy is a separate decision. Stealth Mode matters for unpublished campaigns, client concepts, internal brand work, or material that should not be publicly visible through the applicable Midjourney experience. If private generation is contractual or reputationally necessary, Pro is the entry point. Standard cannot become equivalent through lighter usage.

Mega should be supported by evidence: sustained Fast demand, repeated concurrency bottlenecks, or video workloads that restrict Pro. Do not choose it merely because it is the largest plan. If exploration fits in Relax and private work fits within Pro, the additional $60 per month may be unused capacity.

## What do commercial users need to know?

Subscribers receive broad commercial usage rights, but companies above $1 million in annual gross revenue must use Pro or Mega under Midjourney’s published terms. Before using images in paid campaigns, products, client deliverables, or brand systems, review the current [Midjourney Terms of Service](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083055291277-Terms-of-Service).

For larger organizations, plan selection is a compliance question as well as a performance question. Smaller creators can focus on Fast hours and Relax access; larger companies must also confirm that their tier satisfies the published commercial conditions.

Terms do not replace internal review. Check account ownership, contractor coverage, privacy handling, and additional client requirements. Document the subscription and rights decision when multiple people contribute.

## Which plan should you choose?

Choose Basic to learn, Standard for steady image work, Pro for private or parallel production, and Mega only when measured GPU demand exhausts Pro.

Basic fits a solo creator testing Midjourney, learning prompting, or producing occasional work. Its $10 price minimizes commitment, but no Relax access makes it weak for sustained experimentation.

Standard fits most recurring image workflows. It combines 15 Fast hours with unlimited Relax images, separating urgent refinement from high-volume exploration. It is the default when privacy is not required and queue-based ideation is acceptable.

Pro fits professional work where Stealth Mode, 30 Fast hours, and higher concurrency have clear value, especially when client confidentiality or simultaneous production matters.

Mega fits high-throughput operations needing 60 Fast hours, more parallel video work, or additional headroom. If you cannot identify the bottleneck that extra capacity removes, stay with Pro and measure usage.

Before subscribing, estimate:

`approved assets per month × target cost per approved asset`

Compare the target with subscription cost, likely extra Fast usage, review time, and the financial impact of waiting. The cheapest plan is the one that meets production needs without avoidable delays or privacy risk.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Midjourney have a free plan?

The listed plans are paid subscriptions: Basic, Standard, Pro, and Mega. Midjourney’s getting-started guidance explains the subscription requirement and account flow; check the current guide for availability and onboarding details. [Getting Started Guide](https://docs.midjourney.com/hc/en-us/articles/33329261836941-Getting-Started-Guide)

### Which plan has Stealth Mode?

Stealth Mode is available on Pro and Mega. Basic and Standard do not include it, so teams requiring private generation should begin with Pro.

### Is Relax Mode unlimited?

Standard, Pro, and Mega include unlimited Relax image generations. Pro and Mega also include unlimited SD video generations in Relax Mode. Relax is queue-based, so “unlimited” means access, not guaranteed immediate results.

### Can businesses use Midjourney images commercially?

Subscribers receive broad commercial usage rights under Midjourney’s published terms. However, companies with more than $1 million in annual gross revenue must use Pro or Mega. Review the current Terms of Service before relying on those rights for a specific business or client engagement.
