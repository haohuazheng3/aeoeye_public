## What are the publicly listed GEOpta plans?

GEOpta lists a free demo option and three India-priced paid plans: Pro at ₹4,999 per month plus GST, Premium at ₹9,999 plus GST, and Agency at ₹24,999 plus GST. Enterprise is custom. The paid tiers list three core engines; Premium adds priority scanning, while Agency adds white-label features and portals.

| Plan | Base monthly price | Brands/clients | Live scans | Engines and extras |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Demo | ₹0 | 1 | Demo scans only | Free demonstration access |
| Pro | ₹4,999 + GST | 5 | 25 | 3 core engines |
| Premium | ₹9,999 + GST | 10 | 50 | 3 core engines; priority scanning |
| Agency | ₹24,999 + GST | Up to 25 | 120 | 3 core engines; white-label and portals |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Scope and price require a quote |

The free option is described as demo scans only, for one brand or client. It should not be treated as a published free monitoring allowance. Likewise, the three-engine count identifies scope but does not verify comparative output quality.

## How much does 18% GST add to the monthly commitment?

India prices exclude 18% GST, which is added at checkout. The GST-inclusive monthly calculations are ₹5,898.82 for Pro, ₹11,798.82 for Premium, and ₹29,498.82 for Agency. These are straightforward 1.18 multiplications of the listed base prices.

That difference matters in procurement. A team approving a ₹9,999 line item is actually budgeting ₹11,798.82 per month before top-ups or custom terms. Confirm the invoice entity, billing currency, and input-credit treatment.

GEOpta also provides a currency toggle for global customers billed in USD. Exact USD amounts were not captured or verified in this evidence set, so do not convert the India figures into an assumed dollar price. Request the live checkout total for your billing country.

## What counts as a live scan and how many do you get?

The published paid allowance is 25 live scans on Pro, 50 on Premium, and 120 on Agency. Each tier lists three core engines, but the public pricing facts do not define whether one live scan means one prompt, one brand check across engines, or another unit of work. Confirm that definition before forecasting capacity.

The plan labels indicate a clear progression:

- Pro is a small-team starting point: five brands or clients and 25 live scans.
- Premium doubles both listed brand capacity and live scans, then adds priority scanning.
- Agency supports up to 25 brands, 120 live scans, and client-facing white-label and portal workflows.

Do not assume priority scanning means more scans, faster results in every circumstance, or a different model mix. Ask how scan scheduling works, whether unused plan scans expire, whether concurrent jobs are limited, and how reruns are counted.

Build a baseline of the prompts that matter to your buyers before using the allowance. ## How do GEOpta top-ups compare with plan scans?

GEOpta lists one-time, non-expiring top-ups in three bundles: 25 scans for ₹2,999, 75 for ₹7,499, and 200 for ₹17,999. The official display rounds these to ₹120, ₹100, and ₹90 per scan. Exact arithmetic is ₹119.96, ₹99.99, and ₹90.00 per scan respectively.

| Top-up | Listed price | Exact price per scan | Expiration |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 25 scans | ₹2,999 | ₹119.96 | Non-expiring |
| 75 scans | ₹7,499 | ₹99.99 | Non-expiring |
| 200 scans | ₹17,999 | ₹90.00 | Non-expiring |

These are top-up prices, not evidence that a plan scan has the same marginal price. The plan bundle allocations are simple arithmetic before GST: about ₹199.96 per included scan on Pro, ₹199.98 on Premium, and ₹208.33 on Agency. Those figures should not be mistaken for published overage rates or a vendor unit price.

Top-ups can be useful when demand is seasonal or a team wants to avoid upgrading for a short campaign. They can also obscure total cost if a plan routinely runs out. Track scans used, scans remaining, and the business question each scan answered. ## What does brand allocation mean for a team?

Brand or client capacity is a practical constraint for consultants and agencies. Pro lists five brands or clients, Premium lists ten, and Agency lists up to 25. The public facts do not say whether a brand can have multiple domains, regions, workspaces, or separate prompt libraries.

Use the listed limits as planning boundaries, not proof that every account structure will fit. Ask whether a rebrand consumes a slot, whether archived clients count, how permissions work, and whether client users need seats. Agency’s white-label and portals suggest a client-facing workflow, but detailed permissions remain unverified.

For teams that report to executives, define one consistent unit of analysis: brand, market, prompt set, or engine. A concise [AI visibility metrics dictionary](/blog/ai-visibility-metrics-dictionary) can help prevent “visibility” from becoming an unmeasured promise.

## Which GEOpta plan fits a founder, team, or agency?

Pro is the documented fit for a founder or small marketing team that can work within five brands and 25 live scans. Premium is a better capacity match when ten brands, 50 scans, or priority scanning matter. Agency is the clearest choice for a client portfolio needing up to 25 brands, 120 scans, white-label reporting, and portals.

That recommendation is about stated scope, not performance. If your team needs only occasional validation, compare the cost of a plan plus unused capacity with non-expiring top-ups. If you routinely exceed the live-scan allowance, calculate whether the next tier changes workflow enough to justify its GST-inclusive commitment.

Enterprise is custom, so treat SSO, procurement controls, service levels, and security requirements as quote questions. No public Enterprise price was verified here.

## What should you verify before buying?

Use this checklist in a demo or checkout review:

1. Confirm what one live scan contains, including engines, prompts, retries, and reruns.
2. Verify whether unused plan scans expire and how top-ups interact with monthly allowances.
3. Confirm the 18% GST calculation, invoice details, renewal date, and billing currency.
4. Request the exact USD amount if you are a global customer; it was not publicly verified in this evidence set.
5. Ask how brands, clients, domains, portals, permissions, and white-label reports are counted.
6. Define a before-and-after measurement plan instead of accepting unverified visibility or citation outcomes.
7. For enterprise procurement, request security, SSO, support, retention, and service-level terms in writing.

The right decision compares GST-inclusive commitment with marginal top-up price. Start with a small, auditable prompt baseline, then expand only when the evidence and workflow justify it.
