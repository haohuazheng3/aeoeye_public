MentionOwl’s published plans range from $19 to $399 per month: Starter includes 15 prompts weekly, Growth 50 weekly, Pro 50 daily, and Agency 200 daily. A $1 seven-day trial automatically converts to the applicable full-price plan at its end. The vendor says annual billing gives two months free, and bringing your own provider (BYO) saves 25% while provider API fees remain separate. This is a documentation-based buyer guide; no paid account was used and no output accuracy was tested. Review the [official pricing page](https://www.mentionowl.com/pricing) and [terms](https://www.mentionowl.com/terms) before purchase.

For a brand or agency, the real budget depends on recurring prompt capacity, separate provider charges, and the trial renewal date—not just the subscription headline. Treat prompt arithmetic as a capacity estimate, not a promise of distinct or useful answers.

## What does MentionOwl cost?

The official pricing page lists Starter at $19/month, Growth at $49/month, Pro at $99/month, and Agency at $399/month. Starter and Growth have weekly prompt limits; Pro and Agency have daily limits. Annual billing is advertised as two months free, or ten months of the listed price for a full-year term.

| Plan | Monthly price | Prompt allowance | Annual price at 10× monthly |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Starter | $19 | 15 weekly | $190 |
| Growth | $49 | 50 weekly | $490 |
| Pro | $99 | 50 daily | $990 |
| Agency | $399 | 200 daily | $3,990 |

The annual totals are calculations from the published monthly prices and “two months free” offer. Confirm whether annual billing is charged upfront, the exact checkout total, and the renewal terms. These figures exclude provider API fees. The advertised 25% BYO subscription saving does not remove those separate usage charges.

## How does the trial and renewal work?

The trial costs $1 for seven days and automatically converts to the full-price plan at the end. If it continues, the stated monthly subscription is $19 for Starter, $49 for Growth, $99 for Pro, or $399 for Agency. The public terms cited here do not establish whether the $1 is credited, whether annual billing begins after trial, or how cancellation affects access. Check the current checkout and [terms](https://www.mentionowl.com/terms) for the billing date and cancellation process before entering payment details.

Use the trial period to decide whether the workflow fits your monitoring needs, but do not treat a functioning interface as evidence that outputs are accurate or representative. This guide did not test MentionOwl’s output accuracy. Note the end date and selected billing option, and decide before conversion whether to continue.

## What do weekly and daily prompt limits mean?

The listed cadence defines prompt capacity, not a guaranteed number of distinct AI answers. For rough comparison, annualize the limits and divide by twelve: 15 weekly is about 65 nominal prompt runs per month for Starter; 50 weekly is about 217 for Growth. At 365 days divided by twelve, 50 daily is about 1,521 nominal runs monthly for Pro, and 200 daily about 6,083 for Agency. These are rounded calculations, not measured activity or an assurance of execution.

Weekly and daily limits should not be compared without converting them to a common time basis. Ask whether prompts can be edited or reused, how schedules run, and how failed or repeated runs count. Begin with real buyer questions—category discovery, product comparisons, and recommendation requests—then remove near-duplicates and decide how often each needs a fresh observation.

The objective is meaningful tracking of mentions, citations, and recommendations, rather than maximizing nominal prompt volume.

## How does bringing your own provider change the price?

MentionOwl says BYO saves 25%, while provider API fees remain separate. The discount affects the subscription, but the total cost also depends on provider usage and cannot be calculated from MentionOwl’s listed prices alone.

Ask which subscription amount receives the discount, whether it applies to every tier and annual billing, and how provider usage is billed. The published facts do not establish provider choices, API rates, or expected consumption. Compare the discounted subscription plus your estimated provider charges with the standard subscription using the same prompt schedule. Do not subtract 25% from an assumed all-in cost.

BYO may fit teams that already manage provider access and can estimate usage; separate billing may add work for teams that need costs consolidated. AEOeye’s [AI visibility tools comparison](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) offers broader criteria for comparing coverage and workflow.

## Which plan fits a brand or agency?

Choose according to the prompt set and cadence you can review: Starter has 15 prompts weekly, Growth 50 weekly, Pro 50 daily, and Agency 200 daily. Plan names and limits alone do not establish workspace or client-management features.

An in-house brand can start with priority products and buyer decisions. If weekly observation meets the need, compare Starter and Growth; if daily capacity is required, assess Pro. Confirm how scheduling works before promising a reporting cadence.

An agency should estimate capacity across clients and ask how prompts, users, projects, and billing are allocated. Agency’s 200 daily prompts do not by themselves confirm client-specific reporting or workspace behavior. For either buyer type, unused prompts that no one reviews are not useful coverage. Include trial conversion and separate provider fees when comparing plans.

## What should you check before checkout?

Confirm the trial conversion, recurring price, annual commitment, cadence, and provider charges. These determine the budget and operating fit.

- What date does the seven-day trial end, and when does the full charge occur?
- How do you cancel before conversion, and what happens to access after cancellation?
- Is annual billing upfront, and does “two months free” apply to the chosen tier?
- Which subscription amount gets the 25% BYO discount, and how are provider fees billed?
- How are weekly and daily limits scheduled; do unused prompts carry forward?
- How do failed runs, retries, edits, and duplicate prompts count?
- For Agency, how are limits allocated across client accounts or projects?

During an evaluation, keep a fixed set of realistic buyer prompts and dated examples. Check whether a colleague can understand the question and the reported brand appearance. That assesses workflow clarity, not output accuracy; consistent observations and independent comparison are still needed for confidence.

### How much is MentionOwl for a year?

At the listed prices, annual billing with two months free calculates to $190 for Starter, $490 for Growth, $990 for Pro, and $3,990 for Agency. These amounts exclude provider API fees; verify checkout terms.

### Does bringing my own provider eliminate usage fees?
