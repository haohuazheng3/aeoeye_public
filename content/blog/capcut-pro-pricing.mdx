## How much does CapCut Pro cost?

According to CapCut’s official reference, Pro costs $19.99 monthly or $179.99 annually for an individual. These are references, not a guarantee of every checkout.

| Plan | Reference price | Effective monthly | Best for |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Free or Standard | $0 | $0 | Basic editing and occasional projects |
| Pro monthly | $19.99/month | $19.99 | Short-term or flexible use |
| Pro annual | $179.99/year | About $15.00 | Consistent yearly use |
| Team | About $24.99/month | About $24.99 | Collaborative workflows |

The annual math is $179.99 ÷ 12 = about $15.00 monthly. Paying $19.99 for 12 months totals $239.88, so annual reference pricing is $59.89 lower.

Actual charges may differ because region, taxes, promotions, and purchase platform can change the displayed total.

## Monthly or annual pricing?

Monthly is the flexibility option. At $19.99, use Pro for a campaign, freelance project, school term, or burst of production without a year commitment.

Annual is cheaper per month for consistent users. The break-even comparison is:

- One month: monthly $19.99; annual $179.99 upfront.
- Six months: monthly $119.94; annual $179.99.
- Nine months: monthly $179.91; annual nearly equal.
- Twelve months: monthly $239.88; annual $179.99.

If you expect roughly nine to twelve months of use, annual becomes attractive. If usage is uncertain, monthly avoids paying for idle months.

Before subscribing, inspect the checkout screen. The actual price depends on where you purchase, country, taxes, and current promotion.

## How much does Team cost?

CapCut Team costs about $24.99 per month at the supplied 2026 reference point. Confirm in your account because regional pricing, taxes, platform, and offers may affect it.

Team is worth considering when more than one person needs a shared process. A solo creator should not choose it only because it costs a little more than individual Pro. Ask whether the workflow benefits from collaboration.

For a small agency or content department, compare monthly price with time saved by coordinated editing. If shared workflows prevent duplicate transfers or slow handoffs, the higher amount may be justified. If you are the only editor, compare monthly and annual Pro first.

## What does Pro include?

CapCut Pro adds advanced AI capabilities, premium assets, and about 1,024GB of storage compared with Free or Standard. Those benefits matter when they solve a recurring production problem.

Advanced AI can help frequent creators reduce repetitive work. Premium assets matter when a project relies on a larger library of creative resources. Added storage is relevant when you keep substantial project material inside CapCut.

Free or Standard retains basic editing, making it a sensible starting point for testing, occasional videos, and straightforward work without premium requirements.

## Can you use CapCut for free?

Yes. Free or Standard can handle basic editing and occasional projects. If you trim clips, arrange footage, add ordinary text, and export simple social content, you may not need Pro.

Stay free when you are learning, edit only a few videos, do not need advanced AI, premium assets are not central, storage needs are modest, or you are still comparing editors.

Upgrade when a missing feature repeatedly slows you or premium resources belong in the deliverable. The strongest reason is recurring need, not curiosity about a feature list.

If your separate goal is understanding how a brand appears in AI-generated search, use the [AI Search Preview](/tools/ai-search-preview) independently of the editing decision.

## Which plan should you choose?

Choose Free or Standard for basic, occasional, or developing needs. It is the lowest-risk evaluation path.

Choose Pro monthly when you need advanced features now but may not use them all year. It also works for a defined project with an end date.

Choose Pro annual if you expect steady use over most of the year and can pay $179.99 upfront. Its effective monthly reference is about $15.00.

Choose Team when collaborators genuinely require a team workflow and its approximately $24.99 monthly reference is justified. Do not assume it includes unlimited access; that is not established by the supplied facts.

The buyer rule: start free for basic needs, use monthly for flexibility, annual for predictable use, and Team for collaboration.

### What is the cost per finished video?

Subscription price alone does not tell you the cost of a publishable video. Divide the monthly charge by the number of finished projects you actually ship. A monthly Pro user publishing two videos effectively assigns about $10 of subscription cost to each; at 20 videos, it is about $1 before labor, assets, storage outside CapCut, or distribution.

That calculation makes a recurring need easier to test. If Pro saves only a few minutes on one occasional edit, Free may remain rational. If premium assets and AI remove hours from a weekly production schedule, the subscription can be inexpensive relative to labor.

Do not count unfinished drafts as value. Track exports used by your business, the premium features applied, and whether the same result was possible with Free. Review after one monthly cycle before committing annually.
