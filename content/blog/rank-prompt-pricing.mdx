## Which Rank Prompt plans and prices are published for 2026?

Rank Prompt publishes four paid tiers. The main differences are credit capacity, brand limits, and the number of seats; Agency Plus also adds a client portal and 100 client seats.

| Plan | Monthly price | Credits | Brand limit | Seats | Published annual total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $49 | 150 | Up to 10 | Owner only | $470 |
| Pro | $89 | 500 | Up to 100 | 2 | $855 |
| Agency | $149 | 1,000 | Up to 500 | 10 | $1,430 |
| Agency Plus | $299 | 2,000 | Up to 1,000 | 25 | $2,870 |

Starter is the narrowest fit: one owner and a small brand portfolio. Pro adds a second seat and a larger brand ceiling. Agency and Agency Plus target larger teams; Agency Plus is the only listed tier with a client portal and 100 client seats. Effective cost depends on platforms, prompt frequency, and credit-consuming features such as AI Articles or Brand Audits.

## How do Rank Prompt credits work across platforms and features?

The standard bundle includes ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Mode, and Claude. One prompt run across that standard bundle costs one credit total. Each premium platform enabled adds one additional credit per prompt. In practical terms, platform selection can change the burn rate more than the plan name does.

The published rules are:

- One standard-bundle prompt: 1 credit.
- Each premium platform enabled: +1 credit for that prompt.
- One AI Article: 10 credits.
- One Brand Audit: 10 credits plus 1 credit for each citation page analyzed.

Do not treat a credit as “one report.” A prompt is a monitoring unit; an article or audit has a different cost. A Brand Audit with 20 citation pages uses 30 credits: 10 for the audit plus 20 for analyzed pages. That is a calculation, not a claim about usefulness or accuracy.

Monthly plan credits reset. Purchased top-up credits do not expire. The listed top-ups are 50 credits for $18, 100 for $35, and 200 for $65. Their unit prices are $0.36, $0.35, and $0.325 per credit respectively, before any terms not stated in the documentation.

## What does a realistic prompt workload cost?

Start with the simplest theoretical case: if every run uses only the standard bundle, the number of full standard-bundle prompt runs equals the plan’s included credits. Starter therefore provides a theoretical 150 standard-bundle runs in a billing period, Pro 500, Agency 1,000, and Agency Plus 2,000.

That ceiling changes as soon as premium platforms enter the mix. One hundred prompts using the standard bundle cost 100 credits; with two premium platforms, they cost 300 credits. Starter would not cover that month, while Pro would leave 200 credits before other features.

For example, 20 prompts per week for four weeks use 80 standard-bundle credits. Add one AI Article (10 credits) and a Brand Audit analyzing 15 citation pages (25 credits), and the total is 115 credits. That fits Starter only without premium additions or other runs.

An agency might run 300 prompts in a month with two premium platforms enabled. That is 900 credits, before articles or audits. Agency’s 1,000-credit allowance would leave only 100 credits for those extras. Agency Plus provides more headroom, but the right comparison is still the modeled workload, not the headline plan size.

## How much do monthly and annual plans cost in practice?

The published annual totals are $470 for Starter, $855 for Pro, $1,430 for Agency, and $2,870 for Agency Plus. Paying the listed monthly price for twelve months would total $588, $1,068, $1,788, and $3,588 respectively.

| Plan | Twelve monthly payments | Published annual total | Difference |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $588 | $470 | $118 |
| Pro | $1,068 | $855 | $213 |
| Agency | $1,788 | $1,430 | $358 |
| Agency Plus | $3,588 | $2,870 | $718 |

The difference is the annual-payment saving against twelve monthly payments: $118, $213, $358, or $718. These are published figures; this guide does not infer taxes, refunds, proration, renewal treatment, or other unstated terms.

For budgeting, separate annual subscription spend from expected top-up spend. If usage regularly exceeds an allowance, a larger annual tier may be more predictable than repeated add-ons. Unused monthly credits reset, so irregular workloads can make a seemingly cheaper allowance wasteful.

## Which Rank Prompt plan fits an agency or in-house team?

For an in-house marketer with up to 10 brands and one operator, Starter is the first capacity check. Pro fits when a second person needs access or the portfolio approaches Starter’s ceiling. Estimate runs, premium platforms, articles, and audits before upgrading.

For agencies, seat and brand limits may matter as much as credits. Agency supports 500 brands and 10 seats. Agency Plus supports 1,000 brands and 25 seats, with a client portal and 100 client seats. Those features may justify the tier even when raw prompt volume does not.

The clear buying stance is to model the exact prompt-engine mix before valuing a credit allowance. A 500-credit Pro plan is not “500 reports,” and premium coverage can quickly consume Agency credits. ## What is actually free, and what requires a trial card?

New accounts receive 10 free credits with no card. That is an orientation allowance, not a substitute for estimating workload or output performance.

Paid plans include a seven-day trial with 50 credits and require a card. Before starting, record the trial end date, selected plan, and live cancellation or conversion terms.

## What should you check before buying Rank Prompt?

Use the official [Rank Prompt pricing page](https://rankprompt.com/pricing/) and [plans and pricing help article](https://help.rankprompt.com/en/articles/15-plans-and-pricing) as the final source of truth. Verify:

1. Which exact platforms are in your prompt bundle, and how many are premium?
2. How many prompts will each brand receive per week or month?
3. Will AI Articles or Brand Audits consume credits in the same period?
4. Does the plan’s brand ceiling cover growth, not just today’s list?
5. How many operators or clients need seats or portal access?
6. Will unused monthly credits reset before your team can use them?
7. Are top-ups cheaper than moving to the next tier for your modeled workload?

Keep the calculation auditable: record prompt count, premium-platform count, article count, audit citation-page count, included credits, and top-ups. This creates a defensible estimate without pretending to know untested output performance.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Rank Prompt pricing based on credits or reports?

It is based on credits. A standard-bundle prompt costs one credit, while premium platforms, AI Articles, and Brand Audits use additional documented amounts. A credit does not always equal a report.

### Do Rank Prompt credits expire?

Monthly plan credits reset. Purchased top-up credits do not expire, according to the supplied official documentation.

### Which plan includes a client portal?

Agency Plus includes a client portal and 100 client seats, plus up to 1,000 brands, 25 seats, and 2,000 monthly credits.

### Is there a free trial without a card?

New accounts receive 10 credits without a card. The seven-day paid-plan trial provides 50 credits and requires a card.

Build a 30-day workload model and compare it with the four published allowances and annual totals. Resolve the premium-platform mix before judging value.
