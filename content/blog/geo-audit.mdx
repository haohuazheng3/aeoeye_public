## What Is a GEO Audit?

A GEO audit (also called an AEO audit) measures whether AI engines — ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Claude — mention and recommend your brand when real buyers ask real questions, plus whether your site gives those engines the technical signals they need to find and trust you in the first place.

Quick disambiguation, because Google blends the terms: this is not a geographic or local SEO audit — the kind that checks your Google Business Profile and map-pack rankings. "GEO" here means Generative Engine Optimization, the discipline of earning citations and recommendations from generative AI rather than clicks from ten blue links.

Most people who land on this term aren't researching AEO in the abstract. They run a business, they've noticed AI answers quietly replacing search results in their category, and they want one number: are we visible in those answers or not. A GEO audit exists to give you that number, honestly, before you spend money fixing the wrong thing.

You've probably felt the shift already — organic traffic holding flat or slipping while a competitor's name keeps showing up in AI summaries you never wrote a word of. That's not a ranking problem your old SEO tools were built to catch, because it isn't happening on a results page you can screenshot. It's happening inside a conversation, one prompt at a time, and the only way to see it is to ask the engines directly and log what they say.

## What a Real GEO Audit Covers

A real GEO audit reports on five things: how you show up engine by engine, who AI recommends instead of you, the exact questions where you're invisible, whether your site is technically readable by AI, and a ranked list of fixes — not just a diagnosis with no next step.

| Component | What It Checks | Why It Matters |
|---|---|---|
| Engine-by-engine visibility | Mention rate, rank position, and sentiment across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI, and Claude | Each engine pulls from different sources and weighs signals differently — a strong showing in one says nothing about the others |
| Competitor share | Which brands AI recommends instead of you, and how often, on the same buyer questions | Tells you exactly who you're losing mindshare to, not just that you're losing it |
| Gap questions | Specific questions where competitors appear and you don't | These are your highest-priority fixes — proof buyers are asking and AI has an answer, just not yours |
| Site AEO signals | Crawlability, structured data (schema), and whether content answers questions directly | AI engines can't recommend what they can't parse or trust |
| Prioritized fix plan | Ranked list of what to fix first, ordered by expected impact | Turns a diagnosis into an actual to-do list instead of a wall of data |

If you want the mechanics behind terms like "mention rate" and "sentiment," we've broken that down separately in [how AI visibility measurement actually works](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) — worth a read if you want to understand the number before you act on it.

## GEO Audit vs. Local SEO Audit vs. Classic SEO Audit

The three audits measure different things entirely: a classic SEO audit checks technical health and rankings on Google's ten blue links, a local SEO audit checks map-pack and Google Business Profile visibility, and a GEO audit checks whether generative AI engines cite and recommend you inside conversational answers.

| Audit Type | What's Measured | Typical Tools | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|
| Classic SEO audit | Site health, backlinks, keyword rankings in Google search results | Ahrefs, Screaming Frog, Google Search Console | Ranking report + technical fix list |
| Local SEO audit | Map-pack position, Google Business Profile completeness, local citations and reviews | BrightLocal, GBP Insights | Local ranking report + citation fix list |
| GEO / AEO audit | Mention rate, rank, and sentiment inside AI-generated answers across engines | AEOeye, manual prompt testing | AI visibility score + prioritized fix plan |

If your business has a physical location and foot traffic, you probably need a local SEO audit too — but it won't tell you anything about whether ChatGPT recommends you when someone asks for the "best" option in your category. That's a separate signal, and it's the one growing fastest.
