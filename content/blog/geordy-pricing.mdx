Geordy’s published pricing, checked September 26, 2026, is in US dollars and billed annually: Free is $0 for 40 files, Pro is $39 per month ($468 per year) for 500 files, Business is $319 per month ($3,828 per year) for 5,000 files, and Growth is $1,999 per month ($23,988 per year) for 50,000 files. Monthly-list references are $49, $399, and $2,499. The allowance is files, not pages, and unused files do not roll over.

This is a documentation-based buyer guide, not a hands-on test. AEOeye did not create an account, submit a page, or measure an export. Prices and rules come from Geordy’s official [pricing page](https://geordy.ai/pricing), [formats documentation](https://geordy.ai/formats), and [URL documentation](https://geordy.ai/docs/urls). The calculations are reproducible against your schedule.

## What does each Geordy plan include?

Geordy’s public plans are annual commitments with a monthly file allowance. The annual charge is the honest budgeting number.

| Plan | Published annual billing | Annual total | File allowance | Formats | Best fit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | 40 | Standard set | Trial or small proof of concept |
| Pro | $39/mo | $468/year | 500 | Standard set | Small recurring content program |
| Business | $319/mo | $3,828/year | 5,000 | Standard set | Multi-page, recurring production |
| Growth | $1,999/mo | $23,988/year | 50,000 | 16 formats | Large-scale or high-frequency production |

The standard offering has eight formats; Growth lists 16. Confirm the exact format mix before signing because a larger allowance does not automatically mean every format is available on every tier.

The monthly-list references shown by Geordy are $49 for Pro, $399 for Business, and $2,499 for Growth. For example, twelve months at the listed monthly Business price would be $399 × 12 = $4,788, compared with $3,828 on the annual-billing figure: a $960 difference. Confirm whether monthly billing is purchasable in your region and whether its allowances are identical.

## What is a Geordy file?

A Geordy file is a unit of format work for one page, not a simple count of URLs. Geordy’s documentation describes one file as one format for one page on an initial build or refresh. That means one URL can consume several files in a single production pass.

The practical formula is:

`files per cycle = pages × formats per page`

If you refresh that output repeatedly, add the cadence:

`files per month = pages × formats per page × refreshes per month`

This is the accounting detail that makes page-only comparisons misleading. A 60-page site using one format once is 60 files. The same 60 pages using eight formats every week is a different purchasing problem entirely.

Geordy’s [URL documentation](https://geordy.ai/docs/urls) should be part of your implementation review. Before paying, confirm how refreshes and initial builds are counted; those rules affect capacity more than the plan names do.

Unused files do not roll over. Treat the allowance as a recurring operating limit, not a bank of credits. If your schedule is seasonal, model the busiest month and ask about expansion rather than assuming quiet months offset peaks.

## How much capacity do pages really consume?

The official calculator example is useful because it exposes the multiplication. Suppose you have 60 pages, use eight formats, and refresh weekly:

`60 pages × 8 formats × 52 weeks ÷ 12 months = 2,080 files/month`

Geordy’s vendor calculator presents this scenario as approximately 2,078 files per month, likely because it uses a slightly different monthly conversion. The conclusion is unchanged: the workload fits within Business’s 5,000-file allowance, not Pro’s 500-file allowance.

Here is the same comparison using the published annual prices:

| Scenario | Approx. monthly files | Lowest listed capacity | Annual price |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: |
| 60 pages × 1 format × 1 monthly refresh | 60 | Pro (500) | $468 |
| 60 pages × 8 formats × weekly refresh | ≈2,078–2,080 | Business (5,000) | $3,828 |
| 60 pages × 8 formats × daily refresh | ≈14,600 | Growth (50,000) | $23,988 |

The second row is the important buyer lesson: the page count did not change, but the required plan jumped because format count and cadence multiplied together. The third row is illustrative, not a recommendation to refresh daily; that may be wasteful unless pages genuinely change that often.

Business capacity minus the weekly example is approximately 5,000 − 2,078 = 2,922 files per month. Reserve part of that headroom for re-runs, failed imports, and launch work instead of planning to consume 100% of the quota.

## Which Geordy plan fits an AI visibility workflow?

Choose based on the number of output-format operations you will run, then separately evaluate whether those outputs improve discoverability in AI answers. Geordy capacity answers a production question; it does not by itself tell you whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, or Google AI will cite or recommend your brand.

Free is appropriate for a small trial capped at 40 files. It can help a team understand the unit economics before committing, but 40 files disappears quickly if one page uses multiple formats.

Pro is the sensible starting point for a small site or a limited pilot. At 500 files, it supports 62 page-format combinations if each page uses eight formats once, before any recurring refreshes. That is enough for a focused collection, not an entire high-frequency content operation.

Business is the more realistic tier for an established site with recurring production. In the 60-page weekly example, its 5,000-file allowance leaves room after the approximately 2,078-file monthly workload. Verify that your own refresh cadence, retries, and format selection fit inside the allowance every month.

Growth is designed for very large or unusually frequent workloads and adds the 16-format offering. At $23,988 annually, request written confirmation of included formats, overages, and what support or throughput changes justify the jump from Business.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Geordy Pro really $39 per month?

Yes, Geordy’s verified annual-billing figure is $39 per month, charged as $468 per year, with 500 files. The pricing page also references a $49 monthly-list amount, so confirm the term and checkout total before purchasing.

### Do unused Geordy files roll over?

No. The documented allowance is recurring, and unused files do not roll over. Model your peak monthly workload and keep operational headroom for refreshes and re-runs.

### Is 60 pages enough to estimate my plan?

No. Multiply pages by formats and refresh frequency. Sixty pages at eight formats refreshed weekly is approximately 2,078–2,080 files per month, which points to Business rather than Pro.

### Does a larger Geordy plan improve AI search visibility?

Not automatically. A larger plan buys more production capacity. Visibility still requires useful, crawlable content and measurement of whether answer engines mention, cite, or recommend your brand.
