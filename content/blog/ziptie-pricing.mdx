## Is ZipTie pricing easy to compare in 2026?

ZipTie pricing is a usage-and-configuration quote rather than a conventional set of fixed software tiers. The visible presets begin at **$35.63 per month for Starter**, rise to **$549.67 per month for Professional**, and reach **$2,614.34 per month for Enterprise**, all in USD. Your actual total depends on the configuration selected in the pricing tool.

That distinction matters. A preset is a starting point, not necessarily the amount you will pay after selecting API/MCP access, content contexts, or UGC impact. If you are comparing ziptie pricing with another AI visibility product, record the exact preset and every option first. Otherwise, two “monthly price” screenshots may represent different workloads.

## What does each visible ZipTie preset cost?

The published configurator shows three visible monthly starting points. They are useful for establishing budget ranges, but they should not be treated as a promise that every customer receives the same included usage or final bill.

| Visible preset | Published monthly amount | How to interpret it |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Starter | $35.63 USD/month | Entry preset shown in the configurator |
| Professional | $549.67 USD/month | Higher preset for a larger configured workload |
| Enterprise | $2,614.34 USD/month | Largest visible preset and budget range |

The practical question is not simply “Which tier is cheapest?” It is “Which configuration produces the visibility work I need, and what does that configuration cost?” The [official pricing page](https://ziptie.ai/pricing/) is the source of truth for the selections shown to you.

## Which ZipTie add-ons change the monthly total?

Three published choices are especially important when estimating ziptie ai pricing: API/MCP access, content generation contexts, and UGC impact. Each should be treated as an explicit line item in your worksheet.

- **API/MCP:** $10 per month.
- **Content generation:** $20 per month per context.
- **UGC impact:** selecting it increases the relevant amount by **80%**.

Projects and team access are described as unlimited. That helps organizations managing several brands or collaborators, but it does not remove the need to understand configured usage.

Annual billing changes the prompt subtotal: the published offer applies a **20% discount to the annual prompt subtotal**. Do not automatically discount every add-on unless the pricing page explicitly applies the same rule to that item. Keep prompt subtotal and add-ons as separate rows so your annual comparison remains reproducible.

## How do you calculate a realistic ZipTie monthly bill?

Start with the exact visible preset, then add only the options you actually selected. This avoids the most common ziptie cost mistake: combining a base price with add-ons that were never chosen, or assuming that every optional feature is included.

Here is a reproducible Starter example:

| Line item | Monthly amount |
| --- | ---: |
| Starter preset | $35.63 |
| API/MCP | $10.00 |
| One content-generation context | $20.00 |
| **Estimated monthly total before tax** | **$65.63** |

The arithmetic is straightforward:

`$35.63 + $10.00 + $20.00 = $65.63 per month before tax`

This example does **not** add UGC impact, because UGC was not selected. If you select an option whose configured amount is subject to the published 80% UGC impact, recalculate that line rather than silently reusing the Starter total. The pricing tool’s displayed result should win over a generic spreadsheet formula.

For an annual comparison, first identify the prompt subtotal and apply the stated 20% reduction to that subtotal. Then add the annual treatment of API/MCP and content contexts as displayed by the configurator. Save a screenshot or written record of the inputs, date, currency, and final displayed amount. This is the only reliable way to explain a later difference between your estimate and your checkout total.

## What does the free trial include?

ZipTie offers a **seven-day trial with no card required**. The published trial includes **25 daily prompts** across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity.

That allowance is useful for inspecting the workflow and deciding whether the product fits your research process. It is not evidence that paid reporting will be accurate for your brand, market, or prompt set. A careful trial plan should use a small, repeatable question set and save the outputs so you can compare what changed from day to day.

The trial also lets you test whether your team needs the integrations and whether a content context is worth $20 per month.

## Should you choose ZipTie for your AI visibility workflow?

Choose ZipTie when its configurable workflow matches the way you measure AI visibility and you are willing to keep a precise record of usage choices. The repeatable design is more important than anchoring on the lowest visible preset.

Use this decision framework:

1. **Define the job.** Are you monitoring ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and Perplexity, generating content, or connecting through API/MCP? Write the job first.
2. **Select the smallest honest preset.** Start with the visible preset that can support that job, not the one that merely has the lowest sticker price.
3. **Add required options only.** Add $10 for API/MCP or $20 for each content context only when your workflow needs them. Decide explicitly whether UGC impact belongs in the configuration.
4. **Run the same prompts during the trial.** The no-card, seven-day window gives you a bounded opportunity to inspect the process without pretending it is a paid accuracy study.
5. **Compare like with like.** Use the same engines, prompt set, time window, integrations, and add-ons. A base price against an all-in configuration is not fair.
6. **Save the configuration.** Keep the date, currency, preset, options, prompt subtotal, discount treatment, and displayed total. Reproduce it at checkout.

## What are the limits of this pricing analysis?

This article reports published documentation checked on September 23, 2026; it does not claim a paid-account observation. Dynamic configurators can change by region, selection, or date, so visible amounts are a starting point, not a checkout guarantee.

The calculation above excludes tax and excludes UGC because the example does not select it. It also does not infer usage ceilings beyond the published descriptions. Before purchase, verify the current pricing page, reproduce your own configuration, and retain the displayed result.
