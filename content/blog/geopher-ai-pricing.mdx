## How much does annual billing save?

Annual billing saves $58 on Starter, $158 on Pro, and $398 on Agency compared with twelve monthly payments at the published monthly prices.

| Plan | Twelve monthly payments | Annual price | Saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Starter | $29 × 12 = $348 | $290 | $58 |
| Pro | $79 × 12 = $948 | $790 | $158 |
| Agency | $199 × 12 = $2,388 | $1,990 | $398 |

The nominal annual monthly equivalents are about $24.17, $65.83, and $165.83. These are arithmetic conversions, not separate vendor prices or renewal promises.

## How are prompts and scans different?

Monitored prompts are the plan’s saved monitoring capacity, while scans are the scheduled or on-demand occasions when those prompts are checked. A prompt limit is therefore not the same thing as a monthly scan limit.

Starter includes 10 monitored prompts, weekly automated scans, and 20 on-demand scans per month. Pro includes 50 prompts, twice-weekly automated scans, and 60 on-demand scans. Agency includes 200 prompts, twice-weekly automated scans, and 200 on-demand scans. The platform allowance is three AI platforms on Starter and five on Pro and Agency.

Do not multiply the prompt count by the platform count and treat the result as a verified number of billable or included observations. The pricing page does not define that counting rule. Instead, model your workflow using the documented dimensions separately:

- How many stable buyer questions must remain monitored?
- How often must automated scans run for your decisions?
- How many manual investigations need an on-demand scan?
- Which of the available AI platforms belong in the standard set?
- Will different brands need distinct prompt libraries?

This is a capacity and cadence comparison, not an output-quality claim. A smaller prompt set with clear ownership can be more useful than a larger set no one reviews.

## What do history and team limits include?

History is 30 days on Starter, 90 days on Pro, and 365 days on Agency; member allowances are 1, 5, and 20 respectively. These limits affect how well a team can retain context and collaborate around recurring observations.

Starter is designed around one member and one brand. Pro expands to three brands and five members, while Agency supports 10 brands and 20 members. Pro and Agency also list GA4, and Agency lists priority support. The documented features do not establish the exact permissions within a member allowance, so confirm who can edit prompts, review reports, manage integrations, or administer billing.

Do not assume a 365-day history means every export, result, or prompt revision is retained identically; ask how history is displayed and exported.

## Which Geopher.ai plan fits a brand or agency?

Starter is the natural first comparison for one brand, one member, 10 monitored prompts, three platforms, and weekly automated scans. It can fit a tightly scoped pilot when 30 days of history is enough and manual investigations stay within 20 on-demand scans per month.

Pro is the middle option for a small in-house team or a portfolio of up to three brands. Its 50 prompts, five members, five platforms, twice-weekly automated cadence, 60 on-demand scans, 90-day history, and GA4 inclusion provide more operating room without moving to the Agency tier.

Agency is the stronger fit for an agency or larger brand group that needs up to 10 brands, 200 monitored prompts, 20 members, 200 on-demand scans, 365-day history, GA4, and priority support. The higher price should be justified by those combined requirements—not by an assumption that more prompts automatically create better visibility.

Pick the smallest tier that covers all five dimensions together: brands, prompt set, scan cadence, team access, and retained history. If one dimension fails, the cheapest headline price may not be the cheapest workable plan.

Use the seven days to test your operating assumptions rather than to claim measurement accuracy. ## What should you check before buying?

Start by asking Geopher.ai to clarify how a monitored prompt is counted when it is checked across multiple AI platforms. The page lists both prompt and platform allowances, but it does not define a multiplication rule. Keep your forecast conservative until that behavior is confirmed.

Then verify scan cadence and history in the exact plan quote. Ask whether weekly or twice-weekly scans run on fixed days, how on-demand scans are consumed, and what happens when the monthly allowance is reached. Request a description of exports and retained history so your team knows what it can reproduce later.

Check collaboration and integration details as well. Confirm the member permissions behind the 1/5/20 limits, the scope of GA4 on Pro and Agency, and what “priority support” means for Agency. Finally, confirm taxes, billing cadence, trial conversion, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and any overage policy in writing; none should be invented from the comparison table.
