## What AERank plans and prices are published for 2026?

AERank lists three access levels: Free Scan, Pro, and Agency. The free option is a one-time scan; the two paid options are monthly subscriptions.

| Plan | Published price | Scope | Main capabilities |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| Free Scan | $0, one-time | One scan | Instant visibility score, competitor snapshot, basic playbook |
| Pro | $29/month | One business | Weekly automated scans, four engines, change alerts, competitor tracking over time, full playbook |
| Agency | $99/month | Up to 15 client businesses | White-label PDF reports and priority support |

The four named engines are ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude. Pro is positioned for a business that wants recurring monitoring across those engines. Agency adds multi-client capacity and agency-oriented deliverables rather than simply presenting a larger single-business allowance.

No public annual discount or separate trial was verified in the supplied official source. Do not assume that paying annually, starting a trial, or receiving a refund follows a particular policy unless the live checkout or AERank support documentation states it.

## What is free, and what requires paid monitoring?

The Free Scan costs $0, requires no signup, and is described as one-time. It provides an instant visibility score, a competitor snapshot, and a basic playbook. That makes it useful for an initial orientation: you can see the kind of summary AERank presents before committing to recurring monitoring.

The free scan is not ongoing monitoring. Pro is the listed tier for weekly automated scans, four-engine coverage, change alerts, competitor tracking over time, and a full playbook.

The one-time score is a snapshot; paid cadence creates useful history only when the team preserves underlying observations.

## Which engines and monitoring cadence does AERank describe?

AERank names ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude as its four engines. Pro is described as providing weekly automated scans across four engines, with change alerts and competitor tracking over time. The public description therefore supports a weekly monitoring model, not a claim of continuous or real-time observation.

For an in-house marketer, weekly cadence can support review, change logging, ownership, and response. For fast-moving campaigns, confirm timing, query controls, retention, and alert behavior before building a service process around it.

Treat the score as a summary signal. A defensible local record should include:

- The exact query or prompt used.
- The scan date and engine.
- The answer or result captured at that time.
- Your brand’s position or mention and the competing brands observed.
- The content, product, or market change that might explain movement.

Local monitoring is useful only when teams archive the exact queries, dates, answers, and competitor changes behind the score. Without that record, an alert may provide too little context for a reliable decision.

## What does AERank cost for multiple businesses or clients?

Agency costs $99 per month for up to 15 client businesses. If all 15 slots are actually used, a simple allocation is $6.60 per client per month ($99 divided by 15). This is an arithmetic allocation, not a vendor-published per-client rate and not a statement that every client receives identical value.

Since $99 divided by $29 is about 3.41, four separately purchased Pro subscriptions cost $116 per month. Agency costs $99, or $17 less in this narrow comparison. Capabilities differ: Agency supports up to 15 businesses and adds white-label PDF reports and priority support, so this is not a feature-equivalent comparison.

Annualized at the published monthly prices, Pro costs $348 over twelve months ($29 × 12). Agency costs $1,188 over twelve months ($99 × 12). These are monthly-pay totals, not verified annual-contract prices or claimed annual discounts.

Agency’s allocation is $19.80 per client at five active clients, $9.90 at ten, and $6.60 at 15, excluding labor and unpublished terms.

## Which AERank plan fits an in-house team or agency?

Free Scan fits a buyer who needs a first look at visibility, a competitor snapshot, and a basic playbook without signup. It is the right starting point for understanding the product’s presentation, not for claiming a trend.

Pro fits one business that wants a recurring weekly process across the four named engines. It is the natural plan for an in-house marketer who can own review and response. At $29 per month, the decision should turn on whether the team will use the alerts and history, not merely whether the price is low.

Agency fits a service provider managing several client businesses. The up-to-15-business ceiling, white-label PDF reports, and priority support are the differentiators to evaluate. If the agency expects fewer than four active clients, compare the operational value of those features against separate Pro subscriptions; if it expects to use most of the slots, the bundle becomes easier to justify mathematically.

## What evidence and limitations should buyers understand?

The official description verifies plan names, prices, engines, cadence, and headline capabilities. It does not verify scoring methodology, sampling, answer accuracy, retention, query or export limits, taxes, refunds, uptime, or guaranteed improvements.

Do not turn “visibility score,” “change alerts,” or “competitor tracking” into a guarantee. Ask what an archived observation contains and whether your team can export or retain it. A score without its query and answer may be difficult to audit.

No adoption statistics, customer outcomes, or performance benchmarks should be inferred. This guide makes no claim that AERank increases citations, rankings, leads, or sales.

## What should you check before buying AERank?

Use [AERank’s official site](https://www.aerank.com/) as the live source of truth, then verify:

1. Which exact prompts or queries are included, and can your team define them?
2. Are weekly scans scheduled on a fixed cadence, and how are missed runs handled?
3. What does each engine response preserve: full answer, citation, position, date, or only a score?
4. How long are historical scans and competitor observations retained?
5. Can reports be exported, branded, and stored for client or compliance records?
6. Does Agency’s priority support match your response-time needs?
7. Are annual billing, cancellation, taxes, refunds, and renewal terms shown before payment?

Before buying, run a small evidence checklist. Choose representative queries, record the date and engine, preserve the answer, and define what would count as a meaningful change. If your workflow cannot keep that record, the headline score may be less actionable than the price suggests.
