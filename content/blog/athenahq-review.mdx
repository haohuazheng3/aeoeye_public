AthenaHQ is worth paying for if your team will act on prompt-level AI visibility data every week and can estimate its credit usage before signing. As of 3 October 2026 it has a Free plan (300 credits), a $295-per-month Starter plan (3,600 credits) and quote-only Enterprise. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on.

## What is AthenaHQ, and what has changed since earlier reviews?

AthenaHQ is a platform for [generative engine optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization) (GEO): tracking whether AI answers mention and cite your brand, then recommending what to fix. The idea has academic backing. The [GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) reports that optimization methods can boost visibility in generative engine responses by up to 40%.

Two things changed since our first version of this page (July 2026). The old advice that AthenaHQ has "no free tier" is out of date: [its pricing page](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing) now lists a Free plan. And it is no longer enterprise-only in practice: the plan comparison labels Starter "Best for Self-Guided SMBs".

## What does AthenaHQ actually do?

AthenaHQ's [platform page](https://www.athenahq.ai/platform) organizes the product into three jobs: monitor, understand, act. It is a measurement tool with a built-in content workflow, not just a dashboard.

- **Monitor:** visibility, mentions, citations, sentiment and competitor movement across AI platforms and markets, plus prompt and demand intelligence.
- **Understand:** source intelligence (which sites shape answers), brand-integrity checks for inaccurate claims or inaccessible pages, and prioritized opportunities.
- **Act:** content creation, citation outreach and commerce optimization, with brand-voice rules and review steps.
- **Ask Athena:** a copilot that answers plain-language questions from your own tracking data, per the [homepage FAQ](https://www.athenahq.ai).
- **Integrations:** its [integrations page](https://www.athenahq.ai/integrations) lists 17, including Google Analytics 4, Search Console, Shopify, WordPress, Webflow, Looker Studio, weekly Slack reports and MCP connections for ChatGPT and Claude.

Customer results on the site (for example a share-of-voice rise from 2.0% to 12.6% in 60 days) are vendor-reported. We cannot verify them, so treat them as marketing, not evidence.

## How does AthenaHQ's credit pricing work?

AthenaHQ sells monitoring in credits, and its [pricing page](https://www.athenahq.ai/pricing) defines 1 credit as 1 AI response. Checking one prompt on one platform once uses about one credit, so cost grows with prompts, platforms and how often you rerun. The plans, as of 3 October 2026:

| | Free | Starter | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Price | $0 | $295 per month | Custom quote |
| Credits | $25 free credit, "includes 300 credits" | 3,600 credits per month | Custom allocation |
| AI platforms | ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews, Gemini, Copilot | 11 models, adding AI Mode, Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Meta AI, Mistral | All supported |
| Extras | Content recommendations, Athena AI agent | CSV export, content optimization agent; API and extra credits as paid add-ons | SSO, multi-region and multi-language, BI dashboards, Citation Engine |

Annual billing is labeled "17% off" on the page; we did not confirm the resulting price. The rate works out to about $0.082 per Starter credit ($295 divided by 3,600). The credit calculator page says it estimates monthly credits from "prompts, locations, models, and cadence".

## What will a month really cost, including overages?

Your bill leaves $295 when prompts times platforms times runs passes 3,600. AthenaHQ does not publish an extra-credit price: the page says only "Contact us for add-on pricing". The figures below price overage at the implied Starter rate of about $0.083 per credit ($300 of credit for 3,600 credits), so treat them as an estimate, not a quote.

| Setup (monthly) | Credits used | Against 3,600 | Estimated total |
|---|---|---|---|
| 40 prompts × 5 platforms × 4 weekly runs | 800 | 22% of allowance | $295 |
| 100 prompts × 8 platforms × 4 weekly runs | 3,200 | 89% of allowance | $295 |
| 100 prompts × 11 platforms × 4 weekly runs | 4,400 | 800 over | about $362 |
| 100 prompts × 11 platforms × daily (30 runs) | 33,000 | 29,400 over | about $2,745 |

Two rules fall out of this. Weekly tracking of more than about 80 prompts on all 11 models (3,600 ÷ 44) overflows Starter. Daily tracking at any real scale is an Enterprise conversation, not a Starter one.

The deeper trap is not the rate but the forecasting. In a demo you test a few prompts and the meter barely moves. Months later you add competitors, prompts and regions, and the bill drifts. Model your real prompt list in the calculator, and ask in writing what an extra block costs.

Earlier write-ups, including our own previous version, cited "$100 per 1,250 credits" for overages and a first month of about $95. Neither appears on AthenaHQ's pricing page today, so we removed both.

## Does AthenaHQ have a free trial?

AthenaHQ has a Free plan rather than a timed trial, and it is the cheapest way to test the product. The pricing page lists $25 of free credit, described as 300 credits, across five platforms. A signup button, "Start for Free", sits on the card.

Be careful with two details:

- **Does it renew?** Starter's credit is labeled "$300/month"; Free's has no period. We cannot confirm whether Free credit recurs.
- **How far does 300 go?** 10 prompts on 5 platforms is 50 credits per pass, so six passes. Enough for a sanity check, not for trend tracking.

AthenaHQ's [terms](https://www.athenahq.ai/terms) say paid subscriptions auto-renew until canceled and payments are non-refundable except as required by law or offered. If a plan includes a trial, the terms promise the length, price and deadline are shown before you enroll.

## Who is AthenaHQ worth it for, and who should skip it?

AthenaHQ is worth it for teams that treat AI visibility as a weekly channel with an owner, a prompt list and a content pipeline that can ship the fixes. Our position: the product is only as valuable as your capacity to act on it.

It fits when:

- You have an SEO or content team and a defined competitor set.
- You will track 40 to 80 prompts or more and review them weekly.
- You need GA4, Search Console or CMS integrations, or enterprise controls like SSO.

Skip it, or stay on Free, when:

- You only want to know whether AI engines name you at all. A one-off audit answers that.
- You need a fixed monthly cost. Metered credits and unpublished add-on prices make that hard.
- You have no content process. Recommendations without a team to ship them are a report.

## What did we see on athenahq.ai itself?

In AEOeye's crawler access study (October 2026; one fetch per site, per [our method notes](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory)), athenahq.ai's robots.txt blocked no AI bots and named none explicitly. It publishes an llms.txt. The served homepage HTML had no JSON-LD structured data.

That last point is not proof of no schema. It may be injected by JavaScript, which Google renders but many AI crawlers do not. This is a fact box, not a verdict on the product.

## What are the alternatives to AthenaHQ?

The right alternative depends on the job: a one-off check, cheaper monitoring, or enterprise depth. Compare by prompts, platforms and who ships the fixes, not by feature lists. See our [AthenaHQ alternatives](/alternatives/athenahq) page and [AEOeye vs AthenaHQ](/vs/athenahq) for head-to-heads.

- **One-off audit:** AEOeye's free audit asks three buyer questions on ChatGPT and shows the answers and the competitors named instead. The $29 one-time report adds Claude, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity and ten questions. It is not a daily tracker.
- **Cheaper trackers:** Peec AI and Otterly.AI list entry plans well below $295 per month, per our [comparison](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools), and are built around continuous prompt tracking.
- **Enterprise peers:** Profound and Scrunch AI compete at the top end; see [AthenaHQ vs Profound](/blog/athenahq-vs-profound).

If you are unsure you have a visibility problem, [run the free audit](/) first. It costs nothing, and the answer decides whether metered monitoring is worth buying.
