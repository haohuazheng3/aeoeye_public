To get cited by Perplexity, let PerplexityBot fetch your pages, open every section with a direct and checkable answer, keep the page dated and current, and earn mentions on independent sites Perplexity already reads. Perplexity publishes no ranking formula. This guide rests on its public documentation (as of October 2026), two research papers and our own crawler study, and it flags every inference as ours.

## How does Perplexity choose what to cite?

Perplexity searches the web to answer a question, pulls query-relevant passages from the pages it finds, and writes an answer with numbered citations to those pages. It does not publish how it ranks sources, so anyone quoting exact ranking weights is guessing.

Perplexity's [web search tool documentation](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/agent-api/tools/web-search.md) shows the ingredients. Each result carries a URL, title, snippet, publication date and last-updated date, and answers cite results with markers like [1][2]. This is [retrieval-augmented generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrieval-augmented_generation): the model retrieves outside information and can include sources so readers can verify them.

One buyer question can also fan out. LangChain's undated case study says Perplexity's Pro Search [plans steps and generates search queries for each step](https://www.langchain.com/breakoutagents/perplexity). Your page may be retrieved for a sub-question, such as price or alternatives, rather than the headline one.

Two bots do two jobs, per [Perplexity's crawler guide](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots):

- **PerplexityBot** surfaces and links sites in Perplexity search results. It is not used to crawl content for AI foundation models, and Perplexity recommends allowing it.
- **Perplexity-User** fetches a page when a user's question needs it. Because a person asked, it generally ignores robots.txt.

## Can you do SEO for Perplexity?

Yes, but it is two jobs rather than one ranking. First you must be fetched and retrieved, which is classic technical SEO. Then you must be quoted, which depends on whether a single passage answers the question on its own. There is no position to climb; every answer is assembled fresh.

Most "Perplexity SEO" advice is Google advice with a new name. The one Google habit with measured downside is keyword stuffing: in the GEO paper's Perplexity.ai test it scored about 10% below unoptimized content. Crawl access and answer quality matter far more than keyword density.

## What gets cited, and what doesn't?

Pages get cited when Perplexity can fetch them, one passage answers the question outright, and independent sources agree. The research below is thin and mostly lab-based, so treat it as direction, not a ranking formula.

- **Quotes and statistics help; stuffing hurts.** The [GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (KDD 2024) tested methods on Perplexity.ai with 200 queries. It reports gains of up to 22% on position-adjusted word count (Quotation Addition) and 37% on subjective impression (Statistics Addition). Caveat: source text was uploaded as files, so it shows how prominent a retrieved page becomes, not how to get retrieved.
- **Independent mentions outweigh your own pages.** A 2025 [study of AI search against Google](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08919) reports "a systematic and overwhelming bias towards Earned media" over brand-owned and social content. Its largest experiment used a web-enabled GPT, so read it as direction for AI search, not a Perplexity measurement.
- **Pages a crawler cannot read cannot be cited.** [Vercel's December 2024 analysis](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler) found PerplexityBot among AI crawlers that do not render JavaScript. That is almost two years old, so verify your own pages.

Now our data. In [AEOeye's crawler access study](/resources/ai-crawler-access-study-2026-10.csv) (October 2026, 384 sites with a usable robots.txt, a convenience sample), PerplexityBot was blocked by 26 of 38 news publishers (68.4%), 4 of 20 UGC and review sites, 4 of 90 e-commerce brands and 1 of 193 SaaS sites. Perplexity-User was blocked by 20 of 38 publishers and no SaaS site. Our inference, not a measurement: if Perplexity honors those blocks, a brand that stays open competes against fewer big publishers for retrieved slots.

Two limits apply. [RFC 9309](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc9309.html) says robots rules "are not a form of access authorization", and the [Tow Center's March 2025 test](https://www.cjr.org/tow_center/we-compared-eight-ai-search-engines-theyre-all-bad-at-citing-news.php) found Perplexity's free version identified all ten excerpts from paywalled National Geographic articles despite a block. A block is a request. Our [AI crawler directory](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) lists every bot's user-agent and purpose.

## How do you get cited by Perplexity?

Work through seven steps in order, because the first two decide whether the rest matter. Each step names the action, the reason, and how to verify it.

1. **Let PerplexityBot in.** Allow `PerplexityBot` in robots.txt. In your WAF or CDN, combine its user-agent with the IP ranges Perplexity publishes, as its guide advises, and allow up to 24 hours for changes. aeoeye.com explicitly allows PerplexityBot. In our study 60 of 375 homepages (24 of 88 e-commerce sites) challenged or blocked our self-identified research crawler, so check bot-protection logs too.
2. **Put the answer in the raw HTML.** Open view-source, not Inspect, and search for your key sentence. If it is missing, render it on the server.
3. **Open every section with the answer.** Perplexity's results return snippets relevant to the query, so write sections that survive being lifted out. Give a 40–60 word answer under a question-style heading, name your brand in that sentence instead of "it", and keep one claim per paragraph.
4. **Attach a number, a quote and a source to each claim.** Specifics such as prices, limits and dates are what the GEO tests rewarded. Link the primary source.
5. **Date pages honestly.** Perplexity's results include publication and last-updated dates, and its [filters](https://docs.perplexity.ai/docs/agent-api/filters) can select on both. Show a visible date and mirror it in markup: Google says visible and structured dates should [match](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/publication-dates), and schema.org defines [dateModified](https://schema.org/dateModified). Change the facts first, then the date. We found no Perplexity documentation of how freshness is weighted, and no documented use of schema.
6. **Own one narrow question with one strong page.** The 2025 study reports a "big brand bias", so a niche brand wins by being the clearest answer to a specific question. Our view, not measured on Perplexity: one strong page per question beats five thin ones. We retired 234 of our own posts on 2026-09-24 and merged 165 duplicate-intent pages into 42 on 2026-10-03.
7. **Earn mentions where Perplexity already looks.** Run your buyer questions, list the non-brand domains cited, and get accurate coverage there: reviews, comparisons, roundups, communities. You cannot buy a slot. Our guide on [getting recommended by AI](/blog/how-to-get-your-business-recommended-by-ai) covers the cross-engine version.

## How is Perplexity different from Google SEO?

Both need a crawlable, relevant, clearly structured page. They differ in what you compete for: Google ranks links on a results page, while Perplexity picks passages to quote and cite inside one generated answer.

| | Google Search | Perplexity |
|---|---|---|
| You compete for | A position in a ranked list of links | A numbered citation inside an answer |
| Crawler | Googlebot | PerplexityBot, plus Perplexity-User fetches |
| Unit retrieved | A page | Query-relevant snippets |
| robots.txt | Governs Googlebot | Governs PerplexityBot; Perplexity-User generally ignores it |
| How you see results | Rank trackers, Search Console | Ask the question, read the sources |

If ChatGPT matters to you as well, its retrieval differs; see [ChatGPT SEO](/blog/chatgpt-seo) and our [Perplexity vs ChatGPT comparison](/compare/perplexity-vs-chatgpt-for-brand-visibility).

## How do you check whether Perplexity cites you?

Ask Perplexity the questions your buyers ask and read the sources. Perplexity shows its citations, so a competitor's winning page is visible and you can compare it with yours.

1. Write 10–15 unbranded buyer questions, such as "best invoicing software for freelancers".
2. Run each two or three times on different days, because answers regenerate.
3. Record whether your brand is named, whether your URL is cited, which domains appear, and who is recommended instead.

Then diagnose what you see:

| What you see | Likely cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Competitors cited, your domain never | Not fetched, or no direct answer | Steps 1 to 3 |
| Your page cited, brand not named | The claim isn't tied to your name | Name the brand in the answer sentence |
| Third-party roundups cited, you absent | Missing corroboration | Step 7 |
