Choose Peec AI if you want a tracker with published prices that your team can start this week. Choose Profound if you need SOC 2, content-writing agents and a managed enterprise rollout, and you accept a quote instead of a list price. This comparison is based on both vendors' public documentation and pricing pages as of October 3, 2026; we have not tested either product hands-on.

## Which should you choose, Peec AI or Profound?

Most teams should start with Peec AI. It tells you what you will pay and lets you leave monthly. Profound earns its price only if you will use its agents or need its compliance. Our rules of thumb:

- **Pick Peec** if your question is "how are we doing in AI answers, and who beats us?"
- **Pick Profound** if your question is "how do we produce and publish content at scale to change that?"
- **Pick neither yet** if you have never measured. A tracker cannot tell you which questions matter.

The two do overlap on measuring. They diverge on what comes after: Peec stops at analysis and a to-do list, while Profound builds toward doing the work.

## What is Peec AI at a glance?

Peec AI is a Berlin-based, self-serve analytics tool for tracking brand visibility in AI answers. Brand plans cost $95, $245 and $495 a month, each tracking three chosen models daily with unlimited users. We cover it in depth in our [Peec AI guide](/blog/peec-ai-review).

- **Prompts and limits:** 50, 150 or 350 prompts; Enterprise is custom.
- **Engines:** pick three from ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI, per the [pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing).
- **Onboarding:** its [quickstart](https://docs.peec.ai/quickstart-guide) asks for 10 prompts and 3 to 4 competitors, with first data within 24 hours.
- **Scope:** Peec's AI-instructions page says it does not generate or publish content.

The [detailed price arithmetic](/blog/peec-ai-pricing) is on its own page.

## What is Profound at a glance?

Profound sells itself as an AI marketing platform: visibility tracking plus an AI Marketer and Agents that research, write and publish. Its brand pricing page lists a free 7-day Trial and a custom Enterprise plan, so you cannot budget without talking to sales.

- **Trial:** 50 prompts daily for 7 days on ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. Prompts are a recommended set you cannot customise.
- **Enterprise:** up to nine engines, tailored prompts, API, CSV and JSON exports, all-time history, SSO and SOC 2 compliance.
- **Agents:** workflows that draft content and publish to your CMS, with an approval step before anything goes live, per its [Agents page](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/agents).

Profound's pricing page also calls setup "instant". See [Profound pricing](/blog/profound-pricing) for the quote checklist.

They measure the same things the same way but differ on price transparency, content automation and compliance. Both say they capture answers from the browser rather than APIs, so data collection is not the deciding factor.

## How do the pricing models differ?

Peec charges by prompts and models; Profound quotes by negotiation. Peec's FAQ says price follows prompts and models, with higher tiers cheaper per prompt. Profound's Enterprise plan is a tailored package, so only its Trial has a public shape.

A rough scale check, using arithmetic on published inputs: Peec Starter covers 50 prompts × 3 models × 30 days, or 4,500 AI answers a month, for $95. Profound's Trial runs 50 prompts across 3 engines for 7 days, about 1,050 answers if each prompt runs on each engine daily. It is free but frozen to a preset list.

For a fuller cost picture on each side, read the Peec and Profound pricing guides linked above, then [Profound vs Peec AI vs Otterly](/blog/profound-vs-peec-vs-otterly) if budget is the main constraint.

## How different is the first week with each tool?

Peec puts you in charge of the setup; Profound's Trial hands you a preset. In Peec you write the first 10 prompts, accept or add 3 to 4 competitors and wait up to 24 hours for data, per its [quickstart](https://docs.peec.ai/quickstart-guide). In Profound's Trial the prompt set is chosen for your industry and locked.

That matters for fit. A tracker is only as useful as its questions, and the questions that matter are yours. Profound unlocks prompt editing on Enterprise, after the sales process.

Questions to settle before you pay either vendor:

- **Peec:** which three engines you need, how long the trial runs (its pages state no length) and whether you will accept a 12-month term.
- **Profound:** your price for your prompt count and engines, how AI Marketer credits are metered, and which engines your tier includes.

## Can you trust each vendor's own comparison page?

Treat them as marketing and re-check every number. Both vendors publish a head-to-head, and both are out of step with the other's live pages today.

- **[Peec's comparison](https://peec.ai/comparison/peec-vs-profound)** is dated January 5, 2026 and lists Profound Starter at $99 and Growth at $399. Profound's live brand pricing now shows only a Trial and Enterprise.
- **[Profound's article](https://www.tryprofound.com/articles/profound-vs-peec-ai)** is dated April 16, 2026 and says Peec has no agents and no native Google Analytics 4 link. Peec's docs describe an in-app Agent for questions about your data and a [GA4 AI-referrals view](https://docs.peec.ai/ai-referrals). The fair version is narrower: Peec has no content-writing agents.

Use each vendor's own current pages for that vendor's facts, and ignore its claims about the rival.

## Which fits your team?

Fit follows team type more than feature counts. Match yours below.

- **In-house marketing team under about 20 people:** Peec. You can price it, trial it and, on monthly billing, leave at the end of the payment cycle, per its [terms of use](https://peec.ai/legal/terms-of-use).
- **Agency with many clients:** start with Peec's agency plans ($245, $495, $795 a month). Profound has a separate agency pricing view.
- **Enterprise with a security review:** Profound, if SOC 2 is non-negotiable. Peec says it is pursuing certification.
- **Content-heavy team that wants drafting automated:** Profound's Agents. With Peec you would write the content yourself.
- **Anyone who has not measured yet:** neither. Run a [free AEOeye audit](/) first; it asks three buyer questions to ChatGPT. The $29 one-time report covers 10 questions across five engines.
