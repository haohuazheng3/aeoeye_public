Rankscale is a self-serve AI visibility tracker that bills in credits, from $20 to $780 a month. The number that matters is not the plan price but how many credits each answer burns, and that depends on the engine. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on, so it covers what Rankscale says, how to price it, and what to check.

## What is Rankscale?

Rankscale (rankscale.ai, operated by Rankscale GmbH) is a tracker that asks AI engines the questions you choose, on a schedule, and records whether your brand is named. Its [homepage](https://rankscale.ai/) says it covers "17+ engines" for agencies, enterprise and SEO teams, and claims "1000+ active users." Those are vendor claims we cannot verify.

It belongs to the category we compare in [the best AI visibility trackers](/blog/ai-visibility-tracker). The shape is the same everywhere: you pick prompts, the tool re-asks them, you watch the trend. If you want the metric definitions first, read [how to measure AI visibility](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility).

## What does Rankscale do well?

On paper, its strongest points are breadth and agency workflow. The [pricing page](https://rankscale.ai/pricing) lists unlimited search terms on every plan, scheduling from hourly to monthly, and a feature table that includes team workspaces with unlimited seats. The homepage promises "no per-engine upsell." Four features stand out:

- **Query fan-out.** It shows the internal searches AI engines run before answering. Google confirms AI Overviews and AI Mode [may use a "query fan-out" technique](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features), so this exposes something real.
- **Agency tooling.** White-labeled dashboard links, a REST API and an agency program (listed on higher plans).
- **MCP access.** You can connect AI assistants to your Rankscale data and ask about your brand in plain language. The feature table lists it; confirm which plans include it.
- **Citation and source analysis.** It reports which domains AI engines cite when they mention you or a competitor. That is the most actionable output in this category, because it tells you which third-party pages to get onto.

Two things are labeled beta on the homepage: connecting GA4 and Search Console, and the Recommendations feature. Do not budget for them until they leave beta.

## How much does Rankscale cost?

As of October 2026 the [pricing page](https://rankscale.ai/pricing) lists five plans, billed in US dollars per month. Yearly billing is advertised as saving 15%, and Pro has a 7-day free trial.

| Plan | Price per month | Credits per month | Answers (up to)* | Dashboards | Page audits |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Essentials | from $20 ($17 yearly) | not readable on the page we fetched | not shown | not shown | not shown |
| Pro | $99 | 1,200 | 4,800 | 10 | 50 |
| Pro+ | $229 | 3,000 | 12,000 | 25 | 100 |
| Growth | $385 | 5,500 | 22,000 | 50 | 200 |
| Enterprise | $780 | 12,000 | 48,000 | 100 | 200 |

*"Answers" assumes the typical 0.25 credits per answer. Essentials is tagged "Yearly only." Unused credits roll over up to 2x your allocation on Pro and Pro+, and up to 3x on Growth and Enterprise. REST API access starts at Pro+. Plan-by-plan detail is in our [Rankscale pricing guide](/blog/rankscale-pricing).

The credit meter is where budgets go wrong. The pricing page's own calculator lists 0.25 credits for ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and AI Mode, 1 for DeepSeek and 2 for Claude. By our arithmetic, a Claude answer costs eight times a ChatGPT answer.

Here is what that does to Pro's 1,200 credits:

- 100 prompts run daily on ChatGPT only: 100 × 30 × 0.25 = 750 credits.
- The same prompts run daily on Claude only: 100 × 30 × 2 = 6,000 credits, five times the allowance.
- 100 prompts run weekly (about 433 runs a month) on Claude: roughly 866 credits, 72% of Pro.

Pick engines and cadence before you pick a plan.

Who does each tier suit? Pro fits a focused prompt set. Pro+ fits several brands or a team that needs the REST API. Growth and Enterprise are shaped for agencies and multi-brand programs, where dashboards and audits matter more than raw prompt count. Essentials is the one we cannot size, because its limits were not readable as text.

## What should you check before buying?

Check how the numbers are produced, not how the dashboard looks. AI answers vary run to run: in a SparkToro study of 2,961 runs, there was [under a 1-in-100 chance](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) that ChatGPT or Google's AI returned the same brand list twice. One run proves little.

1. **Credits per engine for your engine list.** Multiply prompts × runs per month × credit cost. Do this before the trial ends.
2. **App or API answers?** The calculator lists "GUI engines" (AI Overview, Copilot) and "API engines" (including GPT-5, Gemini and Perplexity Sonar models). Ask whether the answers match what a buyer sees in the consumer product, because model-level answers can differ.
3. **How often is each prompt repeated?** A single weekly run is a sample of one.
4. **Which audit claim is right?** The homepage says "94+ technical checkpoints" in one place and "200+ Factors" in another. Ask what is actually checked.
5. **How is prompt volume estimated?** The site says it uses "semantic reconstruction." There is no public panel to verify an estimate against, so treat it as a rough guide.
6. **Extra dashboard slots.** Slots can be bought in-app, but we found no published price.
7. **Your exit.** Confirm what exporting your history looks like if you cancel.

Treat the 7-day Pro trial as an experiment, not a tour. Load your real prompts, include the engines your buyers use, and write down how many credits a week of tracking consumed. Then compare that burn rate with the monthly allowance, and with the answers you actually read. If you cannot name one decision the data would change, do not subscribe yet.

## What are the alternatives?

The right alternative depends on how much monitoring you need and how you want to pay. Our position: buy a tracker only after you have read real AI answers about your own brand once, because a trend line is useless if you track the wrong questions.

- **Self-serve trackers with published plans:** Peec AI and Otterly. See our [Peec AI pricing](/blog/peec-ai-pricing) and [Otterly.AI pricing](/blog/otterly-ai-pricing) guides, and the wider [best AI visibility tools](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) comparison.
- **Enterprise, sales-led:** Profound. See [Profound pricing](/blog/profound-pricing).
- **A one-time snapshot, no subscription:** [AEOeye's free audit](/) asks ChatGPT three unbranded buyer questions and shows the full answers and the competitors named instead of you. The $29 one-time report adds Claude, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity and 10 questions.

AEOeye is not a recurring tracker. If you already know you need weekly or daily monitoring across many prompts and brands, Rankscale and the tools above are the right shape, and an agency running several clients will find its dashboards and white-label links the most relevant part of the product.
