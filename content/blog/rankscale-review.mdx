## What is Rankscale?

Rankscale (rankscale.ai) is an AI visibility tracking tool that monitors how — and how often — a brand shows up when people ask ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and other AI engines questions in its category. It sits in the broader [LLM visibility tools](/blog/llm-visibility-tools) category: software built to answer one question on a recurring basis — is my brand getting mentioned, by which AI engines, and is that changing over time? That's a different job than a classic Google rank tracker, which watches blue-link positions rather than what an AI assistant says out loud in a generated answer.

Rankscale is built around a dashboard, not a one-time report. That's a real design decision aimed at SEO teams and agencies who need to show a trend line in a monthly deck, not just a single snapshot. As of this writing, several independent write-ups and comparison posts already cover Rankscale in detail — a sign it's established enough in the category to have a track record worth weighing. We won't duplicate the UI tour those posts already do; what follows is the part they tend to skip: what actually matters when you're the one deciding whether to buy.

## What Rankscale does well

Rankscale's clearest strength is the thing a one-time report structurally can't do: show you a trend, not just a moment. If you already know your brand shows up in AI answers sometimes and you need to prove whether that's improving or slipping — to a client, a boss, or yourself — a [continuous AI visibility tracker](/blog/ai-visibility-tracker) built around a dashboard is the right shape of tool for that job.

Credit where it's due, on three specific fronts:

- **Trend visibility.** A dashboard you check weekly or monthly does something a single audit cannot: it shows direction, not just position, which matters when you need to demonstrate progress rather than just describe a starting point.
- **Agency-oriented breadth.** Managing several client brands rewards a tool built around that workflow from day one — multiple properties, recurring reporting, and screenshots you can drop straight into a client deck without redoing the analysis every month.
- **Multi-engine coverage.** Tracking visibility in ChatGPT alone would miss a lot of where buying research actually happens now. Rankscale tracks across ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and other AI engines, which is the right baseline for this category as of this writing.

That's a legitimate, different job from an audit, and Rankscale appears to be built around it deliberately rather than as an afterthought.

## What to check before you buy

Before you commit to any continuous AI visibility tracker — Rankscale included — get straight answers to five questions in the demo. Marketing pages in this category tend to gloss over exactly these details, and the answers are the difference between a tool that earns its subscription and one that just produces a number nobody on your team actually trusts.

1. **Which engines are actually queried, and how often does that list change?** ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, and Claude behave differently and get sampled with different frequency by different tools. Ask for the current list in writing, not the logo wall on the homepage — logo walls age faster than product roadmaps.
2. **Are the prompts customizable to your buyers' actual questions, or fixed templates?** A generic "best [category] tools" prompt misses the long-tail, comparison, and objection-handling questions your real buyers type into ChatGPT before they ever visit your site.
3. **Does it show you the actual AI answer text, or just a visibility score?** A score of "62" tells you far less than reading the actual sentence ChatGPT generated about you and the competitor it happened to mention instead. We wrote up why query [measurement methodology](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) matters more than the headline number on the dashboard — worth reading regardless of which tool you land on.
4. **How often does it re-query?** AI answers aren't static, and they're not fully deterministic either — the same prompt can return a different answer two days apart. A tool that re-queries daily tells a different story than one that samples monthly; ask which you're getting, because it changes how much you should trust a single week's dip or spike.
5. **What does a seat or brand slot cost once you scale?** The number on the marketing page and the number you actually pay once you add competitor tracking, more brands, or more team seats are often two different numbers. Get the real one before you compare it to anything else on this list.

## Verdict: who should pick what

Pick a continuous tracker like Rankscale if you already know AI visibility matters to your brand and need a recurring dashboard to prove movement over time. Pick an audit first if you don't yet know what AI engines are actually saying about you — the dashboard is only useful once there's something worth watching.

Here's our honest read, competitor bias fully disclosed: most brands buy a monitoring subscription before they've ever read one full AI answer about themselves. That's backwards. A trend line only means something once you know what's actually being tracked — what ChatGPT says when someone asks "best [your category] tools," whether Perplexity mentions you at all, whether Google's AI Overview cites a competitor by name instead of you.

Read the real answers first. Then decide whether you need a dashboard watching them every week.

If you're an agency or in-house team already sold on continuous tracking, Rankscale's dashboard-and-trend-line approach is a reasonable pick — run the demo and hold it against the five questions above. If you're earlier than that and genuinely don't know whether AI engines mention your brand at all, start with a free audit instead of a subscription, and let what you find decide the next step.
