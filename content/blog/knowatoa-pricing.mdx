## What are the Knowatoa plans and prices?

Knowatoa lists three commercial tiers: Starter at $59 per month, Growth at $199 per month, and Enterprise with custom pricing. These are USD figures from the official pricing information checked on September 23, 2026; taxes, discounts, and any regional adjustments are not included in the published amounts.

| Plan | Published monthly price | Published AI-service scope | Public question allowance | Best fit to investigate |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $59/month | ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and AI Mode | Numeric limit not exposed in accessible copy | A focused initial monitoring program |
| Growth | $199/month | States coverage of all 7 services | Numeric limit not exposed in accessible copy | Broader multi-service monitoring |
| Enterprise | Custom | Scope and terms require a quote | Must be confirmed in quote | Teams needing negotiated scope or procurement terms |

The table separates what is stated from what still needs confirmation. “All 7 services” is a Growth description, not a complete seven-service list in the accessible official information.

## Which AI engines are included?

Starter explicitly covers ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and AI Mode. Growth states that it covers all seven services, but the accessible public copy reviewed here does not provide the complete list in a form that can be verified for this comparison. Enterprise scope is custom and should be treated as a negotiated deliverable.

That distinction matters for AI visibility work. “More services” does not automatically mean more useful evidence: frequency, question set, geography, project structure, and export format determine whether a team can act on results.

Ask Knowatoa to put the service list in writing for the plan and date you are buying. Request whether “AI Mode” is a separately measured surface from Google AI Overviews, which queries are supported on each service, and whether a failed or unavailable run consumes an allowance.

## What is missing from the public limits?

The most important unresolved item is the numeric question allowance. Knowatoa’s public pricing language refers to question limits, but the accessible copy reviewed for this guide does not show the number. Without that figure, a buyer cannot calculate a defensible Knowatoa cost per question, compare quotas, or know how quickly a monitoring program will exhaust its allocation.

The missing limit is not a minor footnote. A $59 subscription with 50 questions and a $59 subscription with 500 questions have radically different operating economics, even though the monthly invoice is identical. Because the allowance is unverified, this guide deliberately does not compute a per-question price.

Before treating either tier as production-ready, request answers to these questions:

1. How many questions are included per month on Starter and Growth?
2. Does a question mean one prompt, one engine run, one location, or one scheduled observation?
3. Are reruns, failed jobs, historical refreshes, or additional locations deducted?
4. What are the complete seven services included in Growth?
5. How often are results refreshed, and can the schedule be changed?
6. How many brands, projects, users, or competitors can a plan contain?
7. Are CSV, API, PDF, or other exports included, restricted, or paid separately?
8. What annual price, renewal terms, refund policy, and cancellation timing apply?

Written answers make the comparison auditable and prevent comparing a visible monthly price while leaving the unit of measurement undefined.

## What does Knowatoa cost over a year?

If the monthly price stays unchanged for 12 monthly payments, Starter totals $708 and Growth totals $2,388. Growth therefore costs $1,680 more over that year before tax or discount: $2,388 − $708 = $1,680.

This is an access-tier comparison, not a usage comparison. It assumes 12 unchanged charges and does not claim equal question volumes, service coverage, users, projects, refreshes, or exports. Confirm the annual amount and discount rather than inferring them from monthly arithmetic.

Use this calculation as a budget ceiling, not a forecast of total procurement cost. A quote, tax, currency conversion, implementation work, or added allowance could change the real total. Enterprise is custom-priced and cannot be calculated from public information.

## How should a team choose a plan?

Choose Starter when the three named surfaces—ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and AI Mode—cover the immediate monitoring question and the written quota is large enough for your schedule. It is the sensible starting tier only after you know how many observations fit inside the allowance.

Choose Growth when the complete seven-service scope is relevant and you can explain why broader coverage changes a decision. Do not upgrade solely because “all 7 services” sounds larger; confirm the list, quotas, refresh behavior, and exports first.

Choose Enterprise when requirements depend on negotiated scope, procurement terms, governance, or volume the public tiers cannot document. Ask for a quote itemizing services, questions, users, projects, refreshes, retention, exports, support, renewal, and cancellation.

A practical decision rule is:

- If the service list is the only missing fact, get the list and map it to your buyer questions.
- If the question allowance is missing, pause the ROI calculation; price alone cannot establish value.
- If refresh and export terms are unclear, test whether the workflow can produce repeatable evidence for reporting.
- If you cannot compare the written quote with your required question schedule, treat the purchase as unresolved rather than “good enough.”

## What should you verify before checkout?

Run a short procurement check using your real monitoring design. Write down the engines, number of questions, locations, cadence, competitors, users, and reporting outputs you need for one month. Then ask Knowatoa to map each item to the selected tier and identify every overage or exclusion.

Also separate vendor capability from measurement quality. This guide has not tested whether Knowatoa’s answers are accurate, stable, complete, or representative of buyer experience. A price page establishes a fee, not signal quality. Plan a small, repeatable pilot if available, recording inputs, outputs, dates, and failures.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is Knowatoa billed monthly or annually?

The published Starter and Growth prices are monthly—$59 and $199—and annual billing is available. Confirm the annual total, discount, renewal treatment, and cancellation timing in writing because the accessible pricing copy does not provide those figures.

### Which AI engines are included in Starter?

Starter names ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, and AI Mode. Growth states all seven services, but the complete seven-service list was not exposed in the accessible copy reviewed here. Ask for the exact list and any per-service question rules before comparing tiers.

### Why can’t I calculate Knowatoa cost per question?

The official public pricing information refers to question limits, but the accessible copy does not expose numeric allowances for Starter or Growth. A per-question calculation would therefore invent a denominator. Request the quota definition and included number first.

### Can I cancel a Knowatoa subscription?

The official pricing page states that subscriptions are cancelable. Confirm the notice period, effective cancellation date, refund treatment, and whether annual subscribers follow different terms before paying.

Knowatoa’s headline pricing is easy to state; its usable cost still depends on limits and measurement scope. Confirm the missing terms, then use a focused AEOeye snapshot to establish a baseline for the visibility questions your team actually needs to monitor.
