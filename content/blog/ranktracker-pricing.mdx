## How much does Ranktracker cost?

As of August 29, 2026, Ranktracker lists monthly prices of $39 Launch, $89 Growth, $149 Scale, and $299 Authority; annual billing advertises a 20% saving.

Those prices refer specifically to Ranktracker.com, not to rank-tracking software generally. That distinction matters because “rank tracker pricing” can describe products with different counting rules, refresh schedules, search engines, and project limits. The [Ranktracker pricing page](https://www.ranktracker.com/pricing/) is therefore the appropriate reference for this comparison.

On a monthly basis, the four entry points are $39, $89, $149, and $299. Annual billing lowers the effective cost according to the advertised 20% saving, but buyers should confirm the displayed checkout total, billing currency, taxes, and any selected add-ons before committing.

The most useful question is not simply which plan is cheapest. It is whether the plan can collect the required ranking data at the frequency your reporting or optimization process demands.

## What does each Ranktracker plan include?

Daily Top-100 capacity rises from 500 to 5,000 keywords, while monthly data rows rise from 40,000 to 800,000 and competitor allowances from five to 50 per project.

| Plan | Monthly price | Daily Top-100 keywords | Monthly data rows | Competitors per project |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Launch | $39 | 500 | 40,000 | 5 |
| Growth | $89 | 1,000 | 150,000 | 10 |
| Scale | $149 | 2,000 | 350,000 | 20 |
| Authority | $299 | 5,000 | 800,000 | 50 |

The [rank tracker product page](https://www.ranktracker.com/rank-tracker/) frames the central use case: monitoring keyword positions over time. The limits above show why plan selection is a capacity decision. A site with a modest keyword set may still need Growth or Scale if it monitors many locations, competitors, projects, or frequent changes.

Other Ranktracker tools address adjacent workflows. The [Keyword Finder](https://www.ranktracker.com/keyword-finder/) supports keyword discovery, while the [SERP Checker](https://www.ranktracker.com/serp-checker/) is designed for checking search results. Those activities should not automatically be treated as substitutes for recurring rank tracking.

## Why does tracking frequency change the value?

The same plan supports seven times as many keywords at weekly cadence and 30 times as many at monthly cadence, so update frequency is the largest hidden pricing lever.

Daily tracking uses the published daily Top-100 capacity directly. Weekly tracking spreads the same allowance across fewer update cycles, while monthly tracking spreads it further. In practical terms, a plan that appears too small for daily monitoring may be sufficient for a lower-frequency portfolio review.

That flexibility is valuable for buyers whose decisions do not change every day. A quarterly content program, for example, may need reliable trend evidence rather than daily alerts. Conversely, an active SEO team responding to launches, migrations, or volatile queries may need daily checks even with fewer total keywords.

This is why the [Ranktracker pricing information](https://www.ranktracker.com/pricing/) should be read alongside your operating calendar. Count how often a keyword must be refreshed, then determine whether the resulting rows and capacity fit the selected tier.

## What is included in every plan?

Every plan lists unlimited projects, reports, and users, plus AI Overview tracking, API access, MCP access, Search Console integration, and Looker Studio connectivity.

Those inclusions reduce the importance of user-seat math for teams that need shared access. They also make reporting and data movement part of the plan comparison rather than separate assumptions. The relevant question becomes whether your required volume fits the plan’s rows and tracking capacity.

Search Console integration can add first-party performance context to ranking data. Google explains that Search Console reports a site’s performance in Google Search, including queries, pages, and search appearance information in the [Search Console Help documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/google-analytics-search-console). Google’s setup guidance is also available in its [Search Console documentation](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/monitor-debug/search-console-start).

AI-search visibility can change the buying decision when a team is specifically measuring how visibility evolves in AI-generated search experiences. For a conventional position-monitoring workflow, that feature should be treated as useful scope—not as a reason by itself to buy a higher tier.

## What is the cost per tracked decision?

Sticker price divided by keyword capacity can be misleading; divide instead by the monitored keywords that influence a page, campaign, or client action each month.

Use this calculation:

`Monthly plan cost ÷ monitored keywords that inform a decision`

For example, if Launch costs $39 and 100 keywords directly guide monthly content decisions, the working cost is $0.39 per decision-informing keyword for that month. If Growth costs $89 and 500 keywords matter, the equivalent is $0.178 per monitored keyword. These are planning figures, not claims about performance or savings.

A second calculation can test cadence:

`Monthly plan cost ÷ monthly decision events`

If a team uses ranking data for 20 monthly decisions, Launch works out to $1.95 per decision event. That figure changes when the team adds projects, tracks more competitors, or needs daily rather than weekly updates.

This method is more realistic than dividing $299 by 5,000 and assuming every available keyword creates equal value. ## What should buyers verify before subscribing?

Verify whether daily Top 100 checks, instant checks, row limits, local coverage, add-ons, tax, and current checkout currency match the workflow you priced.

“Keywords” can conceal several different units. A keyword tracked across multiple search engines, devices, locations, or schedules may generate more data than a simple keyword count suggests. Buyers should map their actual configuration before deciding that a tier has sufficient headroom.

Instant checks also deserve separate attention. A recurring rank-tracking allowance and an occasional manual SERP check serve different jobs; the [SERP Checker page](https://www.ranktracker.com/serp-checker/) should be reviewed if urgent or one-off queries are important to the workflow.

Finally, confirm how local coverage is represented, whether add-ons alter the total, and whether tax is added at checkout. If Google data is part of the decision process, maintain a separate view of clicks and impressions through [AI rank tracking](/blog/ai-rank-tracking), rather than expecting rank position alone to explain traffic.

## Which Ranktracker plan should you choose?

Launch fits a disciplined small portfolio, Growth fits a broader operating set, and higher tiers only pay when their extra rows, competitors, and daily capacity are actively used.

Choose Launch when 500 daily Top-100 keywords, 40,000 monthly rows, and five competitors per project cover the operating plan with room for normal variation. It is the sensible starting point for a focused site, a small set of priority pages, or a process that reviews rankings weekly rather than constantly.

Choose Growth when the portfolio is broader or when daily monitoring is becoming operationally important. Scale becomes reasonable when multiple projects, larger competitor sets, or heavier reporting make Growth’s limits restrictive. Authority is justified only when its 5,000-keyword capacity, 800,000 rows, and 50-competitor allowance will be actively consumed.

Ranktracker’s value is strongest when its data feeds a repeatable decision process. Pair the tool with a defined reporting routine, such as the one described in [LLM rank tracking](/blog/llm-rank-tracker), and reassess usage before upgrading. If most available capacity remains idle, the higher price is unlikely to improve the decision.

### Is this Ranktracker.com or another rank tracker?

This article addresses Ranktracker.com specifically. Its published plans are Launch, Growth, Scale, and Authority, with the prices and limits shown on the [official pricing page](https://www.ranktracker.com/pricing/).

### What is the cheapest plan?

Launch is the cheapest listed monthly plan at $39. It includes 500 daily Top-100 keywords, 40,000 monthly data rows, and five competitors per project.

### Does annual billing save money?

Yes. Ranktracker advertises a 20% saving for annual billing. Confirm the current checkout total, currency, tax treatment, and billing terms before subscribing.

### How many keywords should I track daily?

Track the keywords tied to decisions you make frequently, not every possible query. Start with priority pages, commercial terms, and strategically important queries; expand only when the resulting data will change an action.
