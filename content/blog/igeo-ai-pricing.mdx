## What does iGeo AI cost?

iGeo’s public plan structure is easiest to compare by monthly equivalent and configured prompt allowance. Lite and Grow both list 50 prompts, while Pro lists 150; Enterprise is positioned for 300 or more prompts and requires a custom quote.

| Plan | Monthly equivalent with annual billing | Configured prompts | Annual equivalent | Best fit to investigate |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Lite | $49/month | 50 | $588/year | A small team establishing a baseline |
| Grow | $140/month | 50 | $1,680/year | A team assessing higher-tier workflow needs |
| Pro | $232/month | 150 | $2,784/year | A larger monitoring set |
| Enterprise | Custom | 300+ | Custom | Procurement-led or high-volume requirements |

Lite and Grow have the same prompt count, so prompt volume alone cannot justify their price difference. Ask iGeo what capabilities, limits, or support distinguish those tiers in your account and region. The documentation does not establish engine, project, or regional differences as universal facts.

## How should you read iGeo’s prompt allowance?

A prompt allowance is configured monitoring capacity, not a promise that every answer will mention your brand. It tells you how many questions you can plan around; it does not measure recommendation quality, citation quality, or conversion impact.

For an AEO team, a useful prompt set should represent real buying situations: category discovery, alternatives, comparisons, local or industry-specific needs, and questions where a buyer expects a recommendation. A list of 50 near-duplicates can look comprehensive while revealing very little about demand.

Use the allowance to build a balanced panel:

- **Core category prompts:** the questions a buyer asks before knowing your brand.
- **Comparison prompts:** “best,” “versus,” and alternative questions that expose competitive positioning.
- **Problem prompts:** questions framed around the customer’s pain rather than your product name.
- **Branded prompts:** questions that test whether an AI system understands and recommends your existing entity.
- **Change prompts:** a repeatable group for month-over-month comparison.

An independent baseline matters: a tracker can tell you that a prompt ran, not that your brand is winning. Compare the questions with a free [ChatGPT visibility check](/tools/chatgpt-visibility-checker) before committing to a larger allowance.

## Is Lite or Grow the better value?

Lite is the clear lower-cost starting point at the published annual equivalent, but Grow requires a justification beyond “more prompts,” because both list 50 prompts. If your buying decision depends on workflow, collaboration, reporting, refresh frequency, or support, request those details in writing before comparing the tiers.

The practical question is “What recurring decision will this plan improve?” For one brand’s monthly visibility review, Lite may establish a baseline. If stakeholders need additional reporting or workflow support, Grow may be relevant—but only after iGeo confirms what the extra $91 per month buys.

Treat the annual commitment as a real purchasing decision. Annual billing lowers the displayed monthly equivalent but increases the upfront commitment and reduces the value of a quick trial if your prompt set is not yet stable.

## What does Pro cost per configured prompt?

Pro costs more in absolute terms but offers three times the published prompt allowance of Lite. A simple nominal ratio makes the difference visible, while also showing why this is not a per-response price.

For Lite:

`$49 monthly equivalent ÷ 50 configured prompts = $0.98 per configured prompt per month`

For Pro:

`$232 monthly equivalent ÷ 150 configured prompts = $1.546..., or about $1.55 per configured prompt per month`

The result is a planning ratio, not a usage fee. It assumes the full configured allowance is useful to you and says nothing about the number of responses, refreshes, people, projects, or downstream work included. Do not present $0.98 or $1.55 as the cost of an AI answer.

The annual totals are also reproducible:

- Lite: `$49 × 12 = $588/year`
- Grow: `$140 × 12 = $1,680/year`
- Pro: `$232 × 12 = $2,784/year`

These are the published monthly equivalents multiplied by 12, before tax; Enterprise is custom.

## What should you ask before buying iGeo AI?

Ask questions that turn a headline allowance into an operationally testable purchase. Keep answers with your procurement record.

1. **What exactly counts as a prompt?** Ask whether edits, reruns, scheduled refreshes, or multiple locations consume the same allowance.
2. **What happens at the limit?** Confirm whether monitoring pauses, overages are available, or a plan upgrade is required.
3. **What is included in the annual commitment?** Ask about renewal timing, cancellation, refunds, taxes, and currency conversion.
4. **Which workflow features are tier-specific?** Get a written comparison of Lite, Grow, and Pro rather than inferring from the names.
5. **How are results exported and reviewed?** Confirm whether your team can retain evidence, compare periods, and assign follow-up work.
6. **Are engine, project, or region rules different?** Treat these as checkout questions; do not assume a difference unless stated for your account.
7. **What does Enterprise custom pricing cover?** For 300+ prompts, request the included allowance, onboarding, support, security terms, and overage model separately.

Ask how iGeo defines a visibility improvement. More mentions can coexist with weak positioning, wrong-category mentions, or citations that miss your target buyer. Pair any dashboard with a definition of a useful recommendation and a repeatable prompt sample.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is iGeo AI billed monthly or annually?

The verified figures in this guide are monthly equivalents with annual billing: $49 for Lite, $140 for Grow, and $232 for Pro. Confirm the checkout cadence, renewal, taxes, and cancellation terms before payment.

### How many prompts are included in each plan?

Lite and Grow list 50 prompts, Pro lists 150, and Enterprise is custom for 300+ prompts. Ask iGeo how edits, reruns, refreshes, and overages are counted.

### What is the cheapest iGeo AI plan?

Lite is the cheapest published plan at $49 per month equivalent with annual billing, or $588 for 12 monthly equivalents before tax. That is not a guarantee of the lowest final invoice in every region.

### Is $0.98 the cost of a Lite AI response?

No. $0.98 is the nominal monthly-equivalent price divided by 50 configured prompts. It is a planning ratio per configured prompt, not a measured per-response cost or an accuracy claim.
