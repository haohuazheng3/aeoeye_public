Peec AI costs $95 a month for Starter (50 prompts), $245 for Pro (150 prompts) and $495 for Advanced (350 prompts); Enterprise is custom. Agency plans run $245, $495 and $795 a month. Annual billing cuts each by 15–16%, and prices exclude VAT. This guide is based on Peec's public pages as of October 3, 2026; we have not used the product hands-on.

Our view: at list price Peec is cheap per answer. Money is lost elsewhere: daily runs of questions that never change, prompt slots nobody fills, and a 12-month term you cannot leave.

## How much does Peec AI cost in 2026?

Peec lists three self-serve brand plans, Starter at $95, Pro at $245 and Advanced at $495 per month on monthly billing, plus a custom Enterprise plan sold annually. Annual billing brings the monthly rate to $80, $205 and $420. These are the figures on Peec's [brand pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing) on October 3, 2026.

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing (per month) | Euro (monthly / annual) | Prompts |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $95 | $80 | €85 / €70 | 50 |
| Pro | $245 | $205 | €205 / €180 | 150 |
| Advanced | $495 | $420 | €425 / €360 | 350 |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom (annual) | Custom | Custom |

Three details change what you actually pay:

- **Currency.** The page's own code picks euro or dollar prices from the visitor's detected country. The euro column is read from that code; we saw dollars.
- **Tax.** Peec's [terms of use](https://peec.ai/legal/terms-of-use) say all prices are net of VAT, which is added where the law requires it.
- **The "15% off" is a little more.** Peec's FAQ promises 15% for annual billing. Its own figures work out to 15.2% to 16.3%.

## What does each Peec plan include?

All three brand plans include unlimited users, daily tracking and the same three-model choice; they differ in prompt count, projects, countries and extras. Advanced adds multi-country tracking and a Looker Studio integration. API access and single sign-on appear only in the Enterprise card.

| Plan | Models you pick | Projects | Countries (per project) | AI answers per month | Support |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | 3 | 1 | 1 | 4,500 | Chat |
| Pro | 3 | 2 | 3 | 13,500 | Chat + email |
| Advanced | 3 | 5 | 3 | 31,500 | Chat + email |

Those limits come from the comparison table on the same page. Peec's [documentation](https://docs.peec.ai/setting-up-your-prompts) says the country limit applies per project. Extra countries and languages carry no surcharge; Peec's FAQ says price follows prompts and models, not regions.

### What do extra models cost?

A fourth model costs $35, $85 or $165 a month on Starter, Pro or Advanced (monthly billing), or $30, $70 and $140 on annual billing. The price is set by your plan's prompt allowance, not by how many prompts you actually use. Euro prices on the page are €30, €70 and €140 monthly.

Check which models the add-on covers. The add-on card lists ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity and Gemini. Claude, Grok, DeepSeek, Qwen and Mistral appear only in the Enterprise column, labelled as API models. If Claude matters to you, ask sales whether it is available below Enterprise.

### How do Peec's agency plans work?

Agency plans are priced in credits, where one credit is one prompt on one model for one day. At three models tracked daily, that is 90 credits per prompt per month, so Essential's 10,000 credits cover about 111 prompts. Peec's [credits documentation](https://docs.peec.ai/agencies/understanding_credits) gives this formula.

| Agency plan | Monthly billing | Annual (per month) | Credits | About prompts (3 models, daily) | Projects | Countries |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Essential | $245 | $205 | 10,000 | 111 | 3 | 1 |
| Growth | $495 | $420 | 25,000 | 277 | 10 | 3 |
| Scale | $795 | $675 | 65,000 | 722 | 25 | 3 |
| Comprehensive | Custom | Custom (annual) | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

All four include unlimited team and client users. Scale and Comprehensive can switch a project to weekly runs, which use one-third of the credits. Each plan also includes free pitch projects for prospects (3, 5, 7 and 10 active at a time), per the [agency pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing-agencies).

## What does Peec cost per prompt in practice?

On brand plans each prompt costs $1.41 to $1.90 a month, or $0.016 to $0.021 per AI answer, and the cost per prompt falls as you move up. Peec's FAQ says higher tiers get a lower price per prompt. The arithmetic below confirms it.

An AI answer is one prompt run once on one model, which is how Peec counts it: 50 prompts × 3 models × 30 days = 4,500 answers on Starter.

| Plan | Per prompt, monthly | Per prompt, annual | Per AI answer (monthly) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Starter | $1.90 | $1.60 | $0.021 |
| Pro | $1.63 | $1.37 | $0.018 |
| Advanced | $1.41 | $1.20 | $0.016 |

A worked example. A software company tracks 40 buyer questions on three models. That fits Starter: 40 × 3 × 30 = 3,600 answers for $95, about 2.6 cents each, and $1,140 a year ($960 on annual billing). Add Perplexity as a fourth model and it becomes $130 a month, 37% more.

Annual billing only pays if you stay. Starter's annual price breaks even after about 10.1 months of monthly billing, Pro's after 10.0. Peec's terms say a 12-month subscription ends only after 30 days' notice at the end of the term, with no automatic refund for early exit. Pay monthly until you have used the dashboard for a quarter.

## Is Peec AI worth it, and for whom?

Peec is worth it if you need daily trend lines across several engines and someone will review them every week. It is not worth it as a first step, because a tracker cannot tell you which questions matter. The price is fine. The usual failure is paying daily for answers nobody reads.

**Worth it when:**

- You already know your 25–50 commercial buyer questions and want to see them move over weeks.
- Several people need dashboard access. Unlimited users on every brand plan avoids per-seat fees.
- You run reporting for clients. Pitch projects let you show a prospect their data before they sign.

**Skip it, or wait, when:**

- You have never measured anything. Run a one-off check first. [AEOeye's free audit](/) asks three buyer questions to ChatGPT, and the $29 full report covers 10 questions across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity. That costs less than one month of Starter and tells you whether tracking is worth buying.
- Your questions are informational. Peec's [prompt guide](https://docs.peec.ai/setting-up-your-prompts) says AI often answers "how do I" questions without naming brands, so those prompts waste slots.
- You need Claude tracked on a small budget (see the add-on note above).
- You want weekly checks only. Brand plans run daily; weekly appears on Enterprise and the agency Scale plan.

## What should you verify before buying?

Verify six things at checkout, because Peec's own pages disagree in places and the dollar amounts are not in the page's served HTML. Peec's [AI-instructions page](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions) is dated July 2026 and lists the same brand prices, but it gives two different agency client counts for Growth (10 projects in one table, 5 clients in its FAQ).

1. **Currency and VAT.** Confirm the checkout currency and that VAT is added on top.
2. **Selectable models.** The July instructions page lists Perplexity as included on every plan. The comparison table lists ChatGPT, AI Mode, AI Overviews, Copilot, Gemini and Naver AI. Check in the trial that your three models are selectable.
3. **Trial length.** We found no length on Peec's own pages. The terms say Peec sets it.
4. **Term and refunds.** Monthly plans end at the end of the payment cycle; 12-month plans need 30 days' notice and have no automatic early-exit refund.
5. **How prompts count.** Only active prompts count toward the limit; archived and unaccepted suggested prompts do not. Peec's [project docs](https://docs.peec.ai/manage-your-project) say Starter cannot pause a project.
6. **Agency credit steps.** The page's code lists larger credit steps (15,000, 35,000 and 85,000 credits at $345, $695 and $1,195 a month) that we did not see offered. Ask whether they exist.
