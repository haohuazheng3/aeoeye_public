## What is Peec AI pricing in 2026?

Peec AI pricing is organized around the workspace you need to monitor AI visibility: brand plans for one organization and agency plans for teams managing multiple clients. The headline question is not simply “What is the cheapest Peec plan?” It is “Which plan gives your team enough prompts, models, projects, and permissions to run a repeatable monitoring program?”

This is a documentation-based buyer guide, not a hands-on test. The public pricing pages and official instructions were reviewed, but no paid Peec account was used and accuracy of live outputs was not measured. Peec’s interactive pricing page also did not expose every amount in text extraction. The dollar figures below are attributed to the official dated July 2026 instruction page; verify the amount, included limits, taxes, and discount shown at checkout before purchasing.

## What does Peec AI cost for brands?

The official July 2026 instructions list four brand tiers: Starter at $95 per month, Pro at $245 per month, Advanced at $495 per month, and Enterprise with custom pricing. The first three tiers include a stated prompt allowance, three models, and a defined number of projects.

| Brand plan | Monthly price | Prompts | Models | Projects | Best fit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Starter | $95 | 50 | 3 | 1 | A single brand beginning structured monitoring |
| Pro | $245 | 150 | 3 | 2 | A growing team comparing more questions or properties |
| Advanced | $495 | 350 | 3 | 5 | A broader brand program with several projects |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom | Custom | Custom | Requirements that exceed published tiers |

The dated brand figures come from [Peec’s AI instructions page](https://peec.ai/ai-instructions). Check the public [Peec pricing page](https://peec.ai/pricing) for current checkout terms.

### What does the Starter plan include?

Starter is listed at $95 per month for 50 prompts, three models, and one project. It is the clearest fit when one brand needs an initial visibility baseline and the team can keep its monitoring scope narrow.

Fifty prompts can be enough for a focused question set, but “prompts” are a finite operating budget. Define which buyer questions matter, how often you will repeat them, and whether the allowance is consumed across the three models. A low monthly price can become restrictive if you track many categories or competitors.

### What changes at Pro?

Pro is listed at $245 per month for 150 prompts, three models, and two projects. The additional project is important if you need to separate two brands, two properties, or two distinct monitoring workspaces. The larger prompt allowance also gives a team more room to build a repeatable question set rather than checking only a handful of queries.

### When does Advanced make sense?

Advanced is listed at $495 per month for 350 prompts, three models, and five projects. It is aimed at a broader monitoring design, particularly when one team needs several project containers and a much larger recurring prompt set.

Enterprise is custom. If your requirements involve larger limits, specialized permissions, exports, or a procurement process, ask Peec to put the relevant limits and service terms in writing rather than assuming the Advanced tier scales automatically.

## How do Peec’s agency plans differ?

Peec lists a separate agency offering with three named tiers: Essential at $245 per month, Growth at $495 per month, and Scale at $795 per month, with custom arrangements available where needed. The agency page is [Peec AI Pricing for Agencies](https://peec.ai/pricing-agencies).

The key distinction is workspace design. A brand plan is framed around monitoring one organization; an agency plan is intended for a team coordinating work across clients or accounts. Compare the agency offer on the dimensions that affect delivery: client and project count, prompts spread across models, team permissions, and export or reporting needs.

| Agency plan | Monthly price | Decision question |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Essential | $245 | Can a small client portfolio fit the included workspace and prompt limits? |
| Growth | $495 | Do additional clients, projects, and recurring checks justify the middle tier? |
| Scale | $795 | Will a larger delivery team use the higher operating capacity consistently? |
| Custom | Custom | Do account, permission, export, or volume needs require a negotiated package? |

Check the agency page for the current feature matrix and exact limits. If a limit is not explicit, make it a pre-purchase question. “Agency” alone does not tell you how many client workspaces, users, exports, or model checks are included.

## How much is Peec annually with the advertised discount?

Peec advertises 15% off for annual billing. Using the published Pro brand price as a worked example, the calculation is:

`$245 × 12 × 0.85 = $2,499`

That produces an estimated $2,499 before tax if the displayed annual discount applies to the full twelve-month Pro amount. Without the discount, twelve months would be $2,940, so the implied saving is $441.

This is a calculation, not a promise about the checkout total. Confirm whether the 15% offer applies to your plan, currency, renewal term, and any add-ons.

## Which Peec plan should you choose?

Use a decision framework tied to operating behavior, not a preference for a particular label.

1. Start with workspace count. Choose a brand plan when one organization and its projects can stay together. Consider an agency plan when client separation and team delivery are central.
2. Estimate prompt demand. Count the questions you will repeat, the models you will compare, and the review cadence. Leave room for exploratory checks instead of budgeting to the last prompt.
3. Map projects to decisions. A project is useful when it represents a meaningful brand, client, market, or property boundary. Do not pay for project capacity you cannot connect to an owner and a reporting workflow.
4. Confirm collaboration requirements. Ask how permissions, exports, and client-facing reporting work in the exact tier.
5. Validate checkout. Recheck price, annual discount, taxes, included limits, and renewal terms before payment.

The best plan makes monitoring repeatable. A larger prompt allowance matters only when someone reviews findings and turns them into decisions.
