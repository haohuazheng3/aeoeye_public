## What does GetMentioned cost?

GetMentioned’s official [pricing page](https://www.getmentioned.co/pricing), checked September 29, 2026, lists prices in euros per month. The page shows €89/month for 25 prompts, €199/month for 100 prompts, and a custom quote for Enterprise. It advertises 10% savings for annual billing. Its seven-day trial requires a card before data access.

| Published option | Listed price and allowance | Billing note |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 25 prompts | €89 per month | Page advertises 10% annual saving |
| 100 prompts | €199 per month | Page advertises 10% annual saving |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Confirm price and scope in writing |
| Trial | Seven days | Card required before data access |

The public price and prompt allowance do not specify every part of a package. Confirm the engines, regions, refresh cadence, seats, exports, and limits that matter to your team. The figures below are arithmetic from the published rates, not a checkout quote or an assumption about product scope.

At the monthly rate for a full year, the 25-prompt plan totals €89 × 12 = €1,068. If the 10% annual saving applies to that amount, the estimated annual total is €1,068 × 0.90 = €961.20. For 100 prompts, €199 × 12 = €2,388; applying the advertised saving gives €2,388 × 0.90 = €2,149.20. Verify the final annual price, payment schedule, and taxes at checkout.

## How much does each prompt slot cost?

Dividing the monthly price by the listed prompt allowance gives €3.56 per included prompt per month for the 25-prompt option (€89 ÷ 25), and €1.99 for the 100-prompt option (€199 ÷ 100). The larger plan has a lower headline unit cost, but it is only better value if your team needs and can use the added capacity.

Prompt count is a capacity measure, not a complete measure of monitoring value. A prompt is a question or query a team wants to track, but the allowance alone does not establish how often results refresh, which AI answer products are included, or how markets and languages affect counting. Confirm the vendor’s definitions instead of assuming that one prompt equals one answer, one engine, or one refresh.

Before choosing, inventory the distinct buyer questions that influence decisions: category discovery, comparisons with named alternatives, and high-intent use cases. Separate materially different markets or languages where relevant, and remove near-duplicates that would not change what the team does. Ask GetMentioned how edits, markets, and engines consume the allowance. If a focused set fits within 25 prompts, the €89 tier may be a sensible starting point. If your confirmed set approaches 100 and the extra coverage will be used, assess the €199 tier.

A lower per-prompt calculation is not by itself a reason to buy more capacity. Compare plans against the work and decisions required, not just the displayed unit arithmetic.

## What does the seven-day card-required trial mean?

The official pricing page says the seven-day trial requires a card before data access. A buyer should therefore treat the trial as a payment-enabled evaluation and understand its billing transition before starting. The published statement confirms the duration and card requirement; it does not, by itself, state the cancellation deadline, whether conversion is automatic, or what charge follows.

Read the current checkout terms for the trial start and end dates, billing start date, cancellation method, and any reminder. Save the terms and confirmation. If your organization needs approval before a paid subscription begins, assign an owner to track the deadline and confirm that cancellation before conversion is available under the current terms. Do not enter a card until the person responsible understands the next billing event.

Also confirm which plan the trial is associated with and what access it includes. Do not assume feature limits, retention, or trial scope from the seven-day duration alone. Ask the vendor if those details affect whether your evaluation can answer the buying question.

## Which GetMentioned plan should a buyer choose?

Choose the smallest tier that covers your defined questions and verified operating needs. The 25-prompt option at €89/month may suit a focused evaluation. The 100-prompt option at €199/month may fit a broader program when the team has enough distinct prompts to use that capacity. Enterprise is custom quote, so its price and package require a written proposal.

Make a requirements sheet before evaluating tiers. Record the buyer questions, brands and competitors to monitor, markets and languages, and how frequently the team needs results. Then confirm the engines supported, refresh schedule, exports, reporting, and collaboration options for the specific plan. If the vendor has not confirmed an item, mark it unknown rather than treating it as included.

## What should you verify before checkout?

Check both the commercial terms and the package scope. The published prices help estimate spend, but checkout determines the amount and interval offered to your account. Confirm whether the annual price is paid upfront or in installments, how taxes are handled, and when renewal occurs.

Before paying, verify:

- Selected plan and prompt allowance: 25, 100, or custom Enterprise scope.
- Currency, billing interval, total due, taxes, minimum term, renewal, and cancellation terms.
- How prompts are counted, including whether edits, engines, languages, or markets use separate capacity.
- Included engines, refresh frequency, seats, reporting, exports, and support.
- For the card-required trial, its dates, access scope, billing conversion, and cancellation deadline.

Ask GetMentioned to confirm material unknowns in writing. For Enterprise, request a proposal that identifies both the price and included scope. Save the final offer with the procurement record so a future reviewer can tell which terms informed the decision.
