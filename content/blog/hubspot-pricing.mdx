HubSpot pricing is easiest to budget when you model four separate variables: contacts, seats, usage credits, and one-time onboarding. For a defensible first-year estimate, treat promotional prices as temporary, use standard pricing for renewal planning, and ask sales to confirm which limits apply to your exact portal.

This guide uses HubSpot’s published US pricing and catalog information checked September 17, 2026. Prices and promotions can change, so use the official [HubSpot pricing page](https://www.hubspot.com/pricing) and [Product and Services Catalog](https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog) as the final commercial references.

## What should you budget for HubSpot in year one?

A realistic first-year budget is:

**software subscription + additional seats + contact-related costs + credits + required onboarding + tax or negotiated adjustments.**

The headline monthly price is not the whole cost. Marketing Hub Professional, for example, displays at $800 per month with annual commitment or $890 monthly, but it also includes required $3,000 onboarding. Enterprise displays at $3,600 per month and requires $7,000 onboarding.

Free and Starter can be much less expensive, but their limits may become restrictive as your database, automation, reporting, or team grows.

## What do the Marketing Hub tiers include?

| Tier | Published price or promotion | Included contacts | Included Core Seats | Required onboarding | Important budget note |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Free | $0 | Not specified in supplied facts | Up to 2 users | None stated | Useful for testing basic workflows |
| Starter | $7/seat/month promotional price | Confirm with sales | Confirm with sales | Confirm with sales | $20 standard display price; label as promotion |
| Professional | $800/month annual commitment, or $890 monthly display | 2,000 marketing contacts | 3 | $3,000 | Credits included; verify overages and add-ons |
| Enterprise | $3,600/month | 10,000 marketing contacts | 5 | $7,000 | Built for larger operational complexity |

The Starter figure is promotional: $7 per seat per month is shown against a $20 standard display price. Do not use the promotional price as your long-term renewal assumption unless HubSpot confirms its duration and eligibility.

Professional includes 3,000 credits, while Enterprise includes 5,000 credits. Additional credits are listed at $9 per 1,000 annually. The exact feature and usage definitions should be confirmed against the [catalog](https://legal.hubspot.com/hubspot-product-and-services-catalog), because plan names alone do not explain every metered component.

## How do you calculate the first-year HubSpot cost?

Use this formula:

1. Choose the tier and billing basis.
2. Multiply the monthly subscription by 12.
3. Add the annual cost of extra seats, if required.
4. Add required onboarding.
5. Add expected credit purchases beyond the included allowance.
6. Add any contact-related or negotiated charges confirmed in writing.
7. Build a renewal case using standard, non-promotional pricing.

For Professional on annual commitment, the displayed subscription baseline is:

**$800 × 12 = $9,600**

Adding required onboarding produces a first-year baseline of:

**$9,600 + $3,000 = $12,600**

For Enterprise:

**$3,600 × 12 + $7,000 = $50,200**

These are published-price baselines, not quotes. They exclude taxes, optional products, extra seats, negotiated discounts, and usage above included credits.

If you are comparing HubSpot with other software, keep the same structure. Our [Mailchimp pricing guide](/blog/mailchimp-pricing) and [Slack pricing guide](/blog/slack-pricing) use a similar separation between recurring subscription cost and expansion costs.

## What would three common HubSpot budgets look like?

### What is a serious small-team Professional budget?

A small marketing team that needs Professional should begin with the published annual-commitment baseline:

- Subscription: $9,600 per year
- Required onboarding: $3,000
- First-year baseline: **$12,600**

Professional includes 3 Core Seats and 2,000 marketing contacts. If your team needs more Core Seats, ask whether seats are priced per user, whether different seat types exist, and whether any seat is required for a workflow owner or administrator.

Professional also includes 3,000 credits. If your forecast needs 5,000 credits, the incremental requirement is 2,000 credits, or two additional 1,000-credit units:

**2 × $9 = $18 annually**

That calculation is intentionally simple; confirm whether your specific credit type and billing treatment match the published annual rate.

### What is an Enterprise operating budget?

Enterprise has a much higher commitment:

- Subscription: $43,200 per year
- Required onboarding: $7,000
- First-year baseline: **$50,200**

Enterprise includes 5 Core Seats, 10,000 marketing contacts, and 5,000 credits.

Enterprise may be financially sensible when operational complexity—not just contact volume—drives the purchase. Before accepting the tier, make HubSpot demonstrate the exact governance, automation, permissions, reporting, and data requirements that Professional cannot satisfy.

## How should you model contacts and database hygiene?

Contacts are a budget variable, not merely a marketing metric. A contact who is stored, synced, and counted for marketing purposes can affect the plan you need even if that person is not currently receiving a campaign.

Before buying, classify your database:

- Active marketable contacts
- Suppressed or unsubscribed contacts
- Duplicates
- Employees and test records
- Old leads with no realistic reactivation path
- Contacts retained for sales or compliance reasons

Then ask HubSpot exactly which categories count toward your marketing-contact allowance. The supplied facts identify 2,000 contacts for Professional and 10,000 for Enterprise, but they do not define every counting rule.

A clean database can delay an upgrade more effectively than negotiating a small discount. Export a representative sample, estimate monthly net-new contacts, and model the database at months 3, 6, 9, and 12.

## How sensitive is the budget to credits?

Credits deserve their own forecast because usage can grow faster than seats. Professional includes 3,000 credits and Enterprise includes 5,000. Additional credits are published at $9 per 1,000 annually.

Build three usage cases:

- Low: 50% of included credits
- Expected: 100%
- High: 150% or more

For the high case, calculate the excess in 1,000-credit increments and ask whether unused credits expire, roll over, or are shared across hubs or portals. Also confirm whether the $9 rate applies to your exact credit type and whether additional purchases require an annual commitment.

## What should you take into the sales call?

Prepare a one-page worksheet with:

1. Current and projected marketing contacts at months 3, 6, 9, and 12.
2. Required Core Seats and likely users by function.
3. Expected monthly credit consumption.
4. Required hubs, integrations, automations, and reporting.
5. Annual-commitment versus monthly-cash-flow preference.
6. Onboarding requirements and implementation ownership.
7. Renewal price assumptions after any promotion ends.
8. Export, cancellation, overage, and data-retention terms.

Ask the representative to return the answers in writing. Request a line-item quote that separates subscription, seats, contacts, credits, onboarding, discounts, taxes, and optional products.

## What is the defensible HubSpot pricing verdict?

For a small team, Free or Starter can be a sensible validation path, but the published Starter price is promotional and its missing limits must be confirmed. Professional is the clearest serious-team baseline: $12,600 in first-year published subscription plus required onboarding, before extras. Enterprise begins at $50,200 on the same basis and needs a specific operational justification.

The decision is defensible when your forecast explains contacts, seats, credits, onboarding, and renewal—not just the monthly sticker price.

## Frequently asked questions

### Is HubSpot Professional really $800 per month?

HubSpot’s supplied US pricing displays Professional at $800 per month with annual commitment or $890 monthly. Annual commitment also carries required $3,000 onboarding, so the first-year published baseline is $12,600 before extras.

### Is the $7 Starter price permanent?

No. The supplied facts label $7 per seat per month as a promotion against a $20 standard display price. Confirm the promotion’s duration, eligibility, renewal price, and included limits before using it in a long-term budget.

### How much onboarding does HubSpot require?

Professional requires $3,000 onboarding, while Enterprise requires $7,000. The supplied facts do not state a required onboarding charge for Free or Starter; confirm the treatment in your quote.

### What happens when I exceed included credits?

Professional includes 3,000 credits and Enterprise includes 5,000. Additional credits are listed at $9 per 1,000 annually. Confirm your exact credit type, overage behavior, expiration rules, and purchase requirements with HubSpot before signing.
