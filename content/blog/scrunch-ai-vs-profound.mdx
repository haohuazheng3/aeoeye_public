Pick Scrunch AI if you want a published price, a self-serve trial and a tool that can change what AI crawlers receive from your pages. Pick Profound if you run an enterprise marketing team that wants daily tracking across up to nine engines plus agents that draft content, and you accept a sales conversation. This comparison is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested either product hands-on.

## Which should you choose?

Choose by buying process first and features second. Scrunch lets you start alone for $250 a month; Profound's only paid brand plan, Enterprise, is quoted through sales. If you cannot name the person who will act on weekly findings, choose neither yet.

- **Choose Scrunch** when you want a public price, a trial you start yourself, and your buyers use ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews or Copilot.
- **Choose Profound** when you need Claude, Gemini and the rest tracked daily, and you want agents producing content, not just charts.
- **Choose neither** when the question is "does AI mention us at all?" That is a baseline problem, and a one-time audit answers it for far less.

Our own view: most teams comparing these two are earlier in the process than they think. In [AEOeye's audit records](/resources/aeoeye-recommendation-benchmark-2026-09.json) (127 audits, June to September 2026), a brand was named in 157 of 892 engine answers, or 17.6%. That sample is small and self-selected, so do not read it as an industry rate. It does show why a baseline comes first: for many brands the first problem is not being named at all, and no dashboard fixes that on its own.

## Scrunch AI at a glance

Scrunch AI is a Sitecore company: [Sitecore announced the acquisition](https://www.sitecore.com/company/newsroom/press-releases/2026/06/sitecore-acquires-scrunch-to-help-brands-influence-discovery--and-buying-decisions) on June 3, 2026. It started in 2023 and, per its own pages, raised $26 million before the deal.

[Scrunch's pricing page](https://scrunch.com/pricing/) lists Core at $250 a month with 125 prompts, 5,000 responses a month and four platforms. Its distinctive layer, AXP, serves AI-friendly pages to AI retrieval bots, and it is Enterprise-only. We cover legitimacy, features and fit in our [Scrunch AI guide](/blog/scrunch-ai-review) and the budget math in [Scrunch AI pricing](/blog/scrunch-ai-pricing).

## Profound at a glance

Profound began as an analytics tool and now calls itself an AI marketing platform. Its [Series D announcement](https://www.tryprofound.com/newsroom/profound-raises-usd180m-series-d-at-usd1-8b-valuation-to-build-the-ai-platform-for-marketing-teams) of September 15, 2026 reports $180 million at a $1.8 billion valuation, and says it serves over 1,000 enterprise brands. The customer count is Profound's own figure.

[Profound's pricing page](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) shows two brand plans. The first is a free Trial: 50 prompts run daily for seven days across ChatGPT, Gemini and Google AI Overviews, with a recommended prompt set you cannot edit. The second is Enterprise, custom-priced, with up to nine engines, daily tracking, exports and SSO. Our [Profound pricing guide](/blog/profound-pricing) explains what changed on that page.

## How do Scrunch AI and Profound compare?

The two overlap on monitoring and split on price transparency, entry-level engines and what the "action" side touches. All figures below come from each vendor's own pages, checked October 3, 2026.

| | Scrunch AI | Profound |
|---|---|---|
| Entry offer | Core $250/month; 7-day trial of Core, card required | Free 7-day Trial: 50 prompts daily, 3 engines |
| Paid brand pricing | Enterprise custom | Enterprise custom, no public price |
| Entry engines | ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot | ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews |
| Top-tier engines | 9, including Claude, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Grok, Meta AI | Up to 9, including Claude, Gemini, Google AI Mode, Grok, DeepSeek |
| Refresh | Daily for 14 days, then every 72 hours by default | Every tracked prompt daily, per Profound |
| Data capture | [Browser automation and official APIs](https://scrunch.com/faqs/what-methods-does-scrunch-use-to-collect-data-from-ai-platforms) | [Front-end experiences, "not API outputs"](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/answer-engine-insights), per Profound |
| Action layer | Insights, content briefs, AXP delivery (Enterprise) | AI Marketer and Agents on credits, CMS publishing |
| Agency route | Agency Core $500/month; partner program | Agency Growth plan, 400 credits a month per client workspace |

Two caveats apply. Capture method and refresh rate are vendor statements we cannot test. And Scrunch Core and Profound's trial are different things: one is a paid plan with a trial attached, the other is a seven-day sample.

## Is "monitoring versus action" still the right split?

No. Both now sell an action loop, so the useful question is what that action changes. Scrunch changes the delivery layer of your existing pages; Profound produces new marketing assets.

[Profound Agents](https://www.tryprofound.com/features/agents) research what AI engines cite, draft content, require an approval step and publish to your CMS. Per its pricing page, credits are consumed each time an agent runs. The Series D release adds paid-ads tooling and earned-media work, so Profound is no longer an AEO-only product.

Scrunch's loop runs through [content delivery](https://scrunch.com/faqs/what-is-scrunchs-content-delivery-and-how-does-it-work): it detects AI traffic and serves restructured HTML to AI bots while people see the normal site. That reaches pages you already have, but it is a technical change your SEO lead should review.

A tool that drafts content needs editors. A tool that rewrites delivery needs engineers. Pick the one your team can staff.

## What do the two vendors' own sites show?

When we fetched both sites on October 3, 2026 for [AEOeye's crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory), neither blocked AI bots in robots.txt or named any explicitly. Scrunch publishes an llms.txt file; tryprofound.com returned 404 for it. Neither homepage's served HTML contained JSON-LD.

Read that as a fact box, not a quality verdict. Structured data can be injected by JavaScript, which Google renders and many AI crawlers do not. It tells you little about the product.

## What should you ask both vendors before paying?

Ask the questions the public pages leave open. Each one changes the real price or the real coverage, and a written answer is worth more than a demo.

1. **Which engines are in my plan, and which are extra?** Both vendors list nine platforms but attach full coverage to Enterprise.
2. **How often is each prompt refreshed?** Profound says it runs every prompt daily because AI platforms do not return the same answer twice. Scrunch's default is every 72 hours after the first 14 days.
3. **What happens at the limit?** Profound says accounts can be set to overage billing or to pause. Scrunch's overage answer is not visible on its pricing page.
4. **Can I export my data and see the raw answers?** Scrunch says it [offers CSV and API export](https://scrunch.com/faqs/can-you-export-data-from-scrunch); Profound lists CSV and JSON exports under Enterprise. Confirm what your plan includes.
5. **Can I see the SOC 2 report?** Both state SOC 2 Type II compliance, and Scrunch points to a [trust center](https://scrunch.com/faqs/is-scrunch-soc-2-type-ii-compliant-and-what-security-standards-does-it-meet). Ask for the document itself.

## Who should pick which?

Match the tool to the team you actually have, not the one in the pitch deck. These are our readings of the public documentation, not results from testing.

- **A five-person startup.** Neither, yet. If you must pick, Scrunch Core is the only one with a public price and a self-serve start, but 125 prompts and a card-backed trial are a lot for a baseline.
- **A mid-size SaaS team with a content group.** Either can work. Choose Scrunch if your engines are ChatGPT, Perplexity, AI Overviews and Copilot; ask Profound for a quote if Gemini and Claude matter.
- **A large brand on Sitecore.** Scrunch, because Sitecore says it will automate Scrunch's recommendations inside SitecoreAI.
- **An enterprise with many functions.** Profound, if you want one workbench for AEO, content and ads, and have the budget for credits.
- **An agency.** Compare Scrunch's $500 Agency Core and referral program with Profound's per-client credit plan. Price both for your actual client count.
