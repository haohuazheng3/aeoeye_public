## How does Goodie AI billing cadence work?

Goodie’s page states “Monthly cost shown · billed quarterly or annually.” That wording means the displayed monthly amount should not automatically be read as a confirmed month-to-month invoice.

At the displayed rates, the minimum quarterly invoice illustrations are:

- Brand Core: $399 × 3 = $1,197 per quarter.
- Brand Pro: $999 × 3 = $2,997 per quarter.
- Agency Growth: $275 × 3 = $825 per quarter.

These are arithmetic illustrations, not confirmed invoices. They exclude taxes and any commercial terms absent from the official pages reviewed. The documentation also does not establish whether every offer can be paid monthly, how renewal works, or what cancellation and refund rights apply. Ask for the payment cadence and total due in the checkout or quote.

## What do prompts, models, and actions include?

Brand Core lists five models, 120 prompts, and MCP plus 50 optimizations per month; Brand Pro lists eight models, 250 prompts, and MCP plus 100 optimizations per month. Those are separate quantities and should not be collapsed into one invented usage formula.

The official pages reviewed do not provide a verified engine roster, so do not infer exact model names from the model count. They also do not define whether each optimization consumes a prompt, how an optimization is measured, or whether prompt checks vary by model. Enterprise is described as offering up to 13 models, tailored prompt volume, full attribution, multi-brand support, API/MCP, and SOC 2, but the final scope is custom.

For planning, record prompts, models, optimizations, attribution requirements, and seats as separate units.

Do not claim that more prompts or optimizations guarantee better output. A useful program needs stable questions, clear ownership, and evidence that stakeholders can review. ## How should an agency calculate workspace cost?

Agency Growth displays $275 per month and includes 10 pitch workspaces per month, unlimited seats, and MCP. Full client workspaces are add-ons starting at $399 per month, so an agency’s real cost depends on how many client workspaces it needs.

The only safe arithmetic from the documented starting prices is a base-plus-add-on illustration:

- Agency Growth base: $275 displayed monthly equivalent.
- One full client workspace add-on starting price: $399 per month.
- Base plus one starting add-on: $275 + $399 = $674 displayed monthly equivalent.
- Quarterly illustration for that combination: $674 × 3 = $2,022.

The $674 figure is not a confirmed quote. It assumes one compatible add-on at its starting price and excludes taxes and unlisted charges. Ask whether a pitch workspace becomes a client workspace, whether unused capacity carries over, and how client data is separated. Unlimited seats do not mean unlimited client workspaces.

## Which Goodie AI offer fits a brand or agency?

Brand Core is the focused option for a brand that can work within five models, 120 prompts, 50 monthly optimizations, and five seats. Brand Pro is aimed at a larger or more collaborative program needing eight models, 250 prompts, 100 optimizations, agentic commerce visibility, and unlimited seats.

Agency Growth is built around pitch workspaces, unlimited seats, and MCP. It may fit an agency developing proposals or early client work, but client delivery can require full workspaces beginning at $399 per month. Agency Enterprise is custom and should be compared through a written scope rather than the Growth display.

Choose the smallest offer that covers the complete workflow: usable prompt capacity, action allowance, model coverage, seats, workspaces, and invoice cadence. The displayed monthly equivalent is only one part of that decision.

## What should you verify before buying?

Ask for a written breakdown of what is included in a prompt, optimization, pitch workspace, and full client workspace. Confirm whether those units are monthly, whether unused capacity expires, and how they appear in exports or reports. None of those behaviors should be inferred from the headline table.

Verify the exact model roster and which models belong to each count. Confirm what “direct revenue attribution,” “agentic commerce visibility,” and “full attribution” mean in the product workflow, without treating the labels as proof of an outcome. If security matters, request the Enterprise SOC 2 scope and relevant documentation.

Finally, confirm the invoice cadence, annual discount calculation, taxes, trial terms, renewal, cancellation, refunds, and add-on pricing. Test the seven-day free trial if appropriate, but do not present a trial as a quality or attribution result.
