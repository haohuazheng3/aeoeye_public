Goodie AI's Core plan costs $399 a month if you pay annually and $478.80 if you pay monthly. Pro is $999 or $1,198.80, and Enterprise is quote-only. The $399 headline is the annual-billing rate, not the month-to-month price.

This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on. We read the numbers from [Goodie's pricing page](https://higoodie.com/pricing/), including the price data behind its billing toggle, and checked them in a browser on October 3, 2026. A correction: our October 2 version of this page computed $3,990 for a year of Core. That treated $399 as a pre-discount rate, and the page's own data shows it is already the discounted one.

## How much does Goodie AI cost in 2026?

Goodie publishes three prices: Core, Pro and Agency Growth. Each shows a lower monthly rate under annual billing and a higher one under the other option. Enterprise plans, for brands and agencies, are quote-only.

| Plan | Annual billing, per month | Other billing, per month | Per year at annual rate | How you buy |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Core (brand) | $399 | $478.80 (monthly) | $4,788 | Self-serve, 7-day trial |
| Pro (brand) | $999 | $1,198.80 (monthly) | $11,988 | "Get a demo" |
| Enterprise (brand) | Quote | Quote | Quote | Demo |
| Growth (agency) | $275 | $330 (quarterly) | $3,300 | "Get Started" button |
| Enterprise (agency) | Quote | Quote | Quote | Demo |

The yearly column is our arithmetic (12 × the annual rate). The page prints no annual totals and does not say whether a year is charged upfront. Confirm that, and any taxes, at checkout.

## Is $399 a month really the monthly price?

No. The page loads on "Billed Annually [2 Months Free]". Switch the toggle to "Billed Monthly" and Core becomes $478.80 and Pro $1,198.80. For agencies the other option is quarterly billing, and Growth moves from $275 to $330.

The arithmetic matches "2 months free." Ten months at $478.80 is $4,788, the same as twelve months at $399. So monthly billing costs 20% more than the annual rate, or $957.60 extra per year on Core.

This is not new. An [archived copy from August 4, 2026](https://web.archive.org/web/20260804172119/https://higoodie.com/pricing/) shows the same pattern: Explorer at $399, with $479 behind the toggle. An [April 9 copy](https://web.archive.org/web/20260409151428/https://higoodie.com/pricing/) shows no prices at all, only demo buttons. If you saw "Explorer" with three engines and 100 prompts elsewhere, that describes an older plan. Some write-ups, including our earlier answer page, also cited a 30-day money-back guarantee; the current page lists Core with a 7-day trial and no guarantee.

## What do the plans include?

Core and Pro share daily tracking, full reporting and revenue attribution; they differ in scale, engines and extras. Per the page's comparison table, Core covers five answer engines, Pro eight, and Enterprise up to twelve.

| | Core | Pro | Enterprise |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engines | ChatGPT, AI Overview, Perplexity, AI Mode, Copilot | Core's five, plus Gemini, Alexa, Sparky | Adds Claude, Meta, DeepSeek, Grok |
| Prompts | 120 | 250 | 500+ |
| Countries / languages | 2 / 2 | 5 / 5 | 8+ / 8+ |
| Action credits per month | 50 | 100 | 100+ |
| Seats | 5 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Export / API | No / No | Yes / No | Yes / Yes |
| Brands tracked | 1 | 1 | 1+ |

Three details matter. Claude appears only in the Enterprise column, and Gemini starts at Pro. Core and Pro track one brand each; multi-brand work is an Enterprise feature. Agentic commerce visibility (SKU-level) starts at Pro.

The page also contradicts itself in places. The Enterprise card says "up to 13 models," while its FAQ says "12 engines" and the table lists twelve. Ask which number is true.

All plans include MCP access. [MCP](https://modelcontextprotocol.io/introduction) is an open standard for connecting AI applications to external systems. Goodie also labels three tools "free tool" in its navigation; its [Agent Readiness Audit](https://higoodie.com/agent-site-audit/) says "no signup, no account."

## What drives the Goodie AI bill?

The plan decides most of it, because the usage limits are fixed per tier. Seven levers separate the tiers, and you will hit them in roughly this order.

- **Prompts:** 120, 250 or 500+. The page lets you swap tracked prompts up to the limit.
- **Engines:** five, eight or up to twelve. This is where Claude and Gemini coverage gets decided.
- **Countries and languages:** two, five or eight-plus. A multinational brand outgrows Core first.
- **Action credits:** 50 or 100 a month for content, outreach and technical fixes.
- **Agent tokens:** 40M, 100M or 300M+ a month for Goodie's AI assistant. The page says the limit can change as the assistant gains abilities, so ask for it in writing.
- **Brands:** one per plan until Enterprise.
- **Seats:** five on Core, unlimited above.

For agencies the driver is workspaces. Growth includes 10 pitch workspaces a month, each lasting 7 days with 30 prompts. A client workspace is billed on a brand plan "starting at $399 per month."

Prompts alone do not justify Pro. At annual rates Core costs $3.33 per prompt per month ($399 ÷ 120) and Pro $4.00 ($999 ÷ 250), about 20% more. Pro pays for engines, countries, export and commerce coverage.

## What does a realistic annual budget look like?

A brand on Core pays $4,788 for a year, or $5,745.60 if billed monthly. On Pro the figures are $11,988 and $14,385.60. These are list-price illustrations that exclude tax. Goodie's page does not quote annual totals.

![Hands holding receipts and a bank card in front of a laptop](https://images.pexels.com/photos/4968390/pexels-photo-4968390.jpeg?auto=compress&cs=tinysrgb&w=1200)

The break-even is ten months. Monthly billing for ten months costs $4,788 on Core, the same as a year at the annual rate. Stay under ten months and monthly is cheaper; stay longer and annual wins.

An agency with three client workspaces at Core level pays $275 + 3 × $399 = $1,472 a month at annual rates, or $17,664 a year. That assumes each client workspace matches the Core price, which the page says is only a starting point. It also says client workspaces on Growth track "the three core models," while Core lists five. Ask which applies.

The contract changes the risk. Goodie's [service agreement](https://higoodie.com/service-agreement/) says fees are non-refundable and subscriptions auto-renew for equal terms. Monthly plans cancel at the end of the billing cycle; other terms need 30 days' written notice before renewal. Goodie also commits to notice of price changes at least 30 days before renewal. Our advice: pay monthly until you have run the 7-day trial and one full cycle, then switch to annual only if you will stay past ten months.

## Who is Goodie AI worth it for?

Goodie fits brands that will use the whole loop: daily tracking, revenue attribution, and a team that can act on 50 to 100 optimizations a month. It is built for ongoing programs. Its own FAQ argues that some cheaper tools "stop at visibility tracking."

It is a poor fit if the question is still "does AI mention us at all?" Spending $4,788 on non-refundable annual billing to learn that is backwards. Run a one-off check first.
