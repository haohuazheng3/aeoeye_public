Brandlight is an enterprise platform that measures how AI engines talk about a brand, and you can only buy it through a sales conversation. It is built for large marketing organizations, not for a founder who wants a quick answer. This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on, so everything below is what Brandlight says about itself, plus the questions we would ask before signing.

## What is Brandlight?

Brandlight (brandlight.ai) is an AI-visibility platform aimed at enterprise marketing teams. Its [public product summary](https://www.brandlight.ai/llms.txt) says it tracks brand mentions, sentiment and the sources cited by ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Grok and Claude.

"AI visibility" means how often, and how favorably, AI assistants name your brand when buyers ask. The same field is called AEO or GEO; Wikipedia notes there is [no consensus definition](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization). Our own plain-language version is in the [AI visibility glossary entry](/glossary/ai-visibility).

[Brandlight's homepage](https://www.brandlight.ai/) also calls it "the #1 AEO platform globally." That is a vendor claim. Treat it as marketing until you see the analyst report behind it.

## What does Brandlight do?

Brandlight is organized into modules: visibility measurement, content, technical crawlability, publisher partnerships, commerce and ads. The [Visibility & Insights page](https://www.brandlight.ai/product/visibility-insights) says it asks AI engines thousands of questions from different viewpoints, then studies how answers mention your brand, whether they sound positive or negative, and which sources they use.

| Module | What the site says it does | Status on the site |
|---|---|---|
| Visibility & Insights | Mentions, sentiment, competitor positioning, query intent and citation sources | Listed as a product |
| Content | Analyzes your content's structure, tone and metadata; suggests topics; studies competitors' content | Listed as a product |
| Technical | Identifies AI crawlers and agents visiting your site, flags denied access, analyzes raw server logs | Listed as a product |
| Partnerships | Shows which publishers and formats influence your visibility | Listed as a product |
| Agentic Commerce | Product visibility in AI shopping results | Listed as a product |
| Ads | Ad share, category visibility, competitor spend inside AI answers | ["Apply for Enterprise Beta"](https://www.brandlight.ai/product/ad-analysis) |
| Attribution | Ties AI visibility to business outcomes | "Coming soon" |

The most distinctive piece is the [Technical module](https://www.brandlight.ai/product/technical). Server-log analysis answers a question that a tracker which only sends prompts cannot: which AI crawlers actually reach your pages, and which are refused. We think that is the strongest argument for a platform like this.

The Content module deserves the same scrutiny. A recommendation to restructure a page is only as good as the evidence that the restructure changes what AI engines cite, and that evidence is thin across the whole category. Ask what outcome data supports each recommendation type.

The visibility metrics are proprietary. The site lists sentiment analysis, a Source Impact Score, Mention Frequency and a Direct Bias Score. Only the first and third are self-explanatory.

## What did we see on Brandlight's own site?

On 3 October 2026, our research crawler found that www.brandlight.ai is fully open to AI crawlers. It is one of 43 AI-visibility vendor sites in [AEOeye's crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) (October 2026, 391 sites; per-site data in the [CSV](/resources/ai-crawler-access-study-2026-10.csv)). This is a neutral fact box, not a verdict on the product.

- **robots.txt:** `User-agent: *` / `Allow: /`. No AI bot is blocked and none is named.
- **llms.txt:** present, and unusually detailed: it lists every product module and states that pricing is quote-based.
- **Homepage JSON-LD:** Organization only in the served HTML. Schema injected by JavaScript would not show up in a single fetch, so this does not prove there is none.

None of this says anything about the quality of Brandlight's data. It does show the vendor has made its own site easy for AI engines to read.

## How much does Brandlight cost?

Brandlight publishes no price. Its llms.txt says "Pricing is enterprise and quote based. There are no public self-serve tiers," and that access begins with a scoped visibility assessment rather than a free trial. A /pricing URL on brandlight.ai returned a 404 when we checked on 3 October 2026.

Expect a contract negotiated per organization. The variables that usually move the number in this category are brands, regions and languages, modules, and seats, so get each one itemized.

## What should you verify on a demo?

Ask for evidence of how the numbers are produced, not a tour of the dashboard. AI answers are noisy: in a SparkToro study of 2,961 runs from 600 volunteers, there was [under a 1-in-100 chance](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) that ChatGPT or Google's AI gave the same list of brands twice. Any single-run score is therefore weak evidence.

1. **How many times is each prompt repeated, and how is the score averaged?** "Thousands of questions" is a volume claim, not a repeatability claim.
2. **Who writes the prompts?** Prompts you choose reflect your buyers. A fixed library reflects the vendor's guess.
3. **Can you read the raw answers behind a score?** A Direct Bias Score or Source Impact Score you cannot trace back to text cannot be acted on.
4. **Which engines run which tier?** Free-tier and paid-tier models answer differently. Ask what buyers actually see.
5. **Which modules are live today?** The site marks Ads as beta and Attribution as coming soon. Get the roadmap items out of the quote.
6. **Is the SOC 2 report Type 2, and can you read it?** The enterprise page says [Type 2](https://www.brandlight.ai/enterprise), while the llms.txt says only "SOC 2 compliant." Per [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/System_and_Organization_Controls), a Type 2 report also covers whether controls worked over a period, usually 9 to 12 months. Ask for the report and its dates.
7. **What does the contract floor look like?** Minimum term, minimum spend, and what adding a brand mid-term costs.

Ask every vendor on your shortlist the same seven questions so you compare like with like.

A good answer to question 1 is a number and a method, for example "each prompt runs N times per engine and we report the average with a range." A weak answer is "we monitor continuously." If the vendor cannot show the raw text behind a score in the demo itself, assume you will not see it after signing either.

## Who is Brandlight for?

Brandlight fits organizations that need AI visibility measured across many brands, regions or languages, with security review and hands-on support. Its enterprise page lists multi-brand and multi-region tracking, weekly automated reports, a dedicated account executive and "No PII or internal data needed" for onboarding. There is also a partner program for agencies serving global brands.

It is a poor fit if you have one brand, a few dozen buyer questions and no procurement process. You would pay for governance you will not use. Our position: do not buy a monitoring contract for a problem you have not yet looked at with your own eyes.

## What are the alternatives?

The right alternative depends on whether you need enterprise monitoring, self-serve tracking, or a one-time look first. Here is how we would sort them.

- **Enterprise, sales-led:** Profound is the closest comparison. See our [Profound pricing notes](/blog/profound-pricing) and [AEOeye vs Profound](/vs/profound).
- **Self-serve trackers with published plans:** Peec AI and Otterly. Compare them in our [AI visibility tools roundup](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) and the [buyer's guide](/blog/ai-search-optimization-tools-buyers-guide).
- **A one-time snapshot, no contract:** [AEOeye's free audit](/) asks ChatGPT three unbranded buyer questions and shows the full answers and the competitors named instead of you. The $29 one-time report adds Claude, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity and 10 questions.

AEOeye is not a daily tracker and does not replace a platform with server-log analysis and multi-brand governance. If you need that, Brandlight belongs on your demo list. If you do not yet know what AI says about you, start with [how to measure AI visibility](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) and a snapshot, then decide.
