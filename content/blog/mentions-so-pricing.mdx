Pro lists 50 prompts, five sites, and all LLMs. Business lists 100 prompts and 10 sites, while Agency lists 300 prompts and unlimited sites. ## Which plan fits a brand or agency?

Starter is scoped to one site, 25 prompts, and three LLMs; Pro adds five sites, 50 prompts, and all LLMs. Business offers 100 prompts and 10 sites. Agency lists 300 prompts, unlimited sites, white label, and a custom domain.

An in-house team should count the sites and buyer questions that need recurring attention. If one property and three LLMs cover the work, Starter may suffice. A team with several properties or a need for all LLMs can evaluate Pro. Business may fit a broader prompt and site inventory, but confirm model coverage before relying on it for reporting.

An agency should map its client roster to sites, prompts, and reporting needs. Agency’s unlimited sites and white-label features may fit client-facing work, but ask how clients, access, and data are separated. Confirm that 300 prompts can support the intended scope; the listed price does not specify prompt allocation across accounts.

Choose the smallest tier that covers the prompt-site-model combinations your team will actually review. More capacity is useful only when the questions support a decision and the resulting observations can be interpreted consistently.

## What should you verify before checkout?

Confirm tier coverage, unit definitions, yearly billing, and any charges before subscribing. The published monthly figures and toggle do not resolve every detail that determines actual cost and coverage.

Ask the vendor:

- Which LLMs are included in Business and Agency?
- How are prompts counted, and can they be shared across sites or models?
- How frequently do prompts run, and can the schedule be controlled?
- What exact annual amount appears at checkout, and is it charged upfront?
- When does renewal occur, and how do plan changes or cancellation work?
- For Agency, how are client sites, prompts, access, and reports separated?
- What does white label cover, and what is available on the custom domain?
- Are there taxes, usage charges, or other fees beyond the list price?

If the checkout flow presents a trial or another purchase condition, review its renewal terms; no trial terms are established in the facts used here. During any evaluation, use a fixed prompt set and save dated examples. This guide did not assess answer accuracy or repeatability.
