## What is the v0 pricing verdict?

For occasional experimentation, v0 Free is enough to evaluate the product without a subscription. It includes $5 in monthly credits and up to seven messages per day.

For serious solo work, Plus is the best value if you regularly build interfaces, prototypes, or full-stack features. Its current promotional price is $30 per user per month, reduced from a crossed-out $90 price, and it includes $30 in monthly credits plus $2 in free daily credits when you log in.

Business costs $100 per user per month. It uses the same monthly and daily credit structure described for Plus, but adds team-oriented value such as training opt-out. Enterprise pricing is custom and aimed at organizations that need SAML, role-based access control, service-level agreements, and negotiated terms.

The short version: choose Free to test v0, Plus for an individual production workflow, Business for governed team use, and Enterprise when procurement, identity, and contractual requirements drive the purchase.

## How much does each v0 plan cost?

These prices were checked on September 15, 2026. Promotional pricing can change, so confirm the current amount on the [official v0 pricing page](https://v0.app/pricing) before purchasing.

| Plan | Subscription price | Included credits and limits | Best for |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | $0/month | $5 monthly credits; 7 messages/day | Trying v0 and occasional personal use |
| Plus | $30/user/month promotional | $30 monthly credits; $2 daily credits on login | Frequent individual building |
| Business | $100/user/month | Same monthly/daily credit structure; training opt-out | Teams needing control and privacy |
| Enterprise | Custom | Negotiated terms and usage | SAML, RBAC, SLA, procurement |

The largest jump is between Plus and Business: $70 more per user every month. That difference should be justified by organizational requirements, not simply by wanting more prompts. If your main need is model volume, first examine how credits are consumed and whether a more focused workflow reduces waste.

## How do monthly and daily credits work?

v0 combines recurring monthly credits with a smaller daily benefit. Free includes $5 monthly and a seven-message-per-day limit. It is useful for learning the interface, testing prompt quality, and creating small experiments, but not uninterrupted daily development.

Plus includes $30 in monthly credits per user and $2 in free daily credits when you log in. Business follows the same monthly and daily structure. Daily credits can matter for consistent users, while the monthly allowance is the larger predictable pool.

Think of the allowances this way:

- Monthly credits are the recurring budget for the billing period.
- Daily credits are a smaller login-linked benefit that supplements it.
- Message limits and credit limits are separate constraints.
- Token-based model charges determine how quickly credits disappear.

A $30 plan with $30 of monthly credits is not unlimited use. The effective work depends on model choice, context size, and generated tokens.

## What do v0 models cost per token?

v0 quotes model rates per 1 million tokens, with separate input and output prices. Input is what the model reads—prompts, files, and conversation context. Output is what it generates.

| Model | Input per 1M tokens | Output per 1M tokens |
|---|---:|---:|
| Mini | $0.20 | $1.20 |
| Pro | $2.00 | $10.00 |
| Max | $5.00 | $25.00 |
| Max Fast | $10.00 | $50.00 |

Model choice changes the economics dramatically. Max Fast output is more than four times Max output, while Pro input is ten times Mini input. Difficult work may justify stronger or faster models, but premium models should be reserved for tasks that benefit.

Suppose a request uses 100,000 input tokens and 20,000 output tokens:

- Mini: (0.1 × $0.20) + (0.02 × $1.20) = $0.044
- Pro: (0.1 × $2) + (0.02 × $10) = $0.40
- Max: (0.1 × $5) + (0.02 × $25) = $1.00
- Max Fast: (0.1 × $10) + (0.02 × $50) = $2.00

These are token-rate illustrations, not a promise about how every v0 action converts into credits. Actual consumption depends on the request and supplied context.

## What might v0 cost in real usage?

### Casual monthly use

A casual user might stay on Free and spend nothing. The $5 monthly allowance and seven daily messages provide room to test layouts, ask implementation questions, and build small demos.

If you use v0 only a few times monthly, Plus may not pay for itself. Its main benefit would be greater consistency and a larger allowance, not a lower per-request price.

### Weekly solo building

A solo builder using v0 every week will usually find Plus easier to justify. At $30 per month, it provides $30 monthly plus $2 in free daily credits on login.

If you log in on 20 days, the stated daily benefit would total $40, in addition to the monthly allowance. Treat that as an illustration—not a guarantee that every day’s benefit accumulates without conditions.

Compare [Cursor AI pricing](/blog/cursor-ai-pricing), [Bolt.new pricing](/blog/bolt-new-pricing), and [n8n pricing](/blog/n8n-pricing), too. Their workflows differ, so the cheapest headline may not be the cheapest way to finish your project.

### Small team usage

For a four-person team, Business subscription fees alone are $400 per month before usage: four users multiplied by $100. That premium can be sensible when training opt-out and organization-oriented governance are required.

It is difficult to justify when each person uses v0 occasionally and nobody needs team controls. Compare individual workflows with the operational value of Business rather than assuming every teammate needs the same plan.

### High-context model usage

Premium models consume budget faster than Mini. In the 100,000-input/20,000-output example, one Max Fast request represents $2 at listed rates versus $0.044 for Mini.

A workflow repeatedly sending large files, long conversations, or broad project context can exhaust credits quickly. Start with the least expensive model that reliably completes the task, then escalate when quality, depth, or speed requires it.

## When is v0 Business justified?

Business is justified when the organization needs controls an individual plan does not provide. The clearest stated differentiator is training opt-out, which matters when company policy governs how business inputs are handled.

The $100-per-user price also buys a formal team purchasing model. That may simplify budgeting, onboarding, and approval compared with employees managing personal subscriptions.

Before upgrading, check whether training opt-out is required, multiple users will actively use the workspace, the team can manage usage, and the operational benefit exceeds the extra $70 per user over Plus.

Enterprise is appropriate when SAML, RBAC, SLA commitments, custom procurement, or contractual requirements are mandatory. Pricing is custom, so there is no honest universal estimate.
