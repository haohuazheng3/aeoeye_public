## What is the best Bolt.new plan in 2026?

For most individual builders, Pro is the practical Bolt.new plan. It costs $25 per month, includes 10 million tokens, removes the daily usage limit and Bolt branding, supports custom domains, and includes up to 1 million web requests.

Free is best for evaluating Bolt.new, making a small landing page, or building a limited experiment. Teams is designed for organizations that need admin controls, not simply people who want more tokens. Enterprise is for companies needing a custom commercial arrangement.

Choose Free to test, Pro to build and iterate regularly, Teams when centralized administration matters, and Enterprise when custom terms drive the purchase.

## How much does Bolt.new cost?

Current plans appear on the official [Bolt.new pricing page](https://bolt.new/pricing). These were checked September 15, 2026.

| Plan | Price | Tokens and limits | Main features |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | $0/month | 300,000/day; 1 million/month | Bolt branding; uploads up to 10MB |
| Pro | $25/month | 10 million; no daily limit | No branding, custom domains, up to 1 million web requests; one-month rollover |
| Teams | $30/member/month | Tokens are not pooled | Admin controls and team management |
| Enterprise | Custom | Custom arrangement | Organization-specific requirements |

Free has both daily and monthly limits. Pro has a monthly allowance with no daily limit, making it more predictable when working through many iterations in one session.

Teams is not “Pro plus a shared token bank.” Each member’s tokens are not pooled. If three teammates need capacity, each requires an appropriately licensed seat. Confirm current language in Bolt’s [subscriptions documentation](https://support.bolt.new/account-and-subscription/subscriptions).

## What do Bolt.new tokens mean?

Tokens represent usage while the system interprets prompts, inspects project context, generates code, and modifies the application. A short request can use little; a request asking Bolt to understand a growing codebase, change files, add dependencies, and repair errors can consume much more.

Token usage is driven by work complexity, not just prompt count. Consumption rises when you:

- ask for broad changes across many files;
- repeatedly regenerate the same feature;
- include a large codebase in context;
- ask Bolt to diagnose multiple errors at once;
- iterate on backend, database, or authentication behavior;
- rebuild a feature instead of making a focused change.

Stretch the allowance with narrower prompts. “Add email validation to the signup form and preserve its visual design” is more controlled than “Improve signup, authentication, security, and onboarding.”

Exact request cost depends on context and generated work. Do not treat the monthly allowance as a fixed number of development hours.

## Which plan fits your project?

### Is Free enough for a landing page?

Free can cover a small landing page when validating an idea rather than maintaining production marketing. The allowance includes 300,000 tokens daily and 1 million monthly, with Bolt branding and a 10MB upload limit.

A simple project might include a homepage, a few responsive sections, a contact form, basic visual adjustments, and small assets. Free becomes restrictive with repeated revisions, many sections, larger assets, or a need for a custom domain without branding. For a public launch, Pro is usually easier to justify.

### Is Pro suitable for an SaaS prototype?

Pro is the strongest default for an individual building an SaaS prototype. Ten million tokens and no daily limit provide room for product iteration. You also get no branding, custom domains, and up to 1 million web requests.

A prototype may need authentication, dashboard states, database-backed forms, billing settings, error handling, responsive layouts, and deployment configuration. Those changes create more context and debugging than a one-page site.

Still manage the budget deliberately. Save stable milestones, make focused requests, and avoid a complete rewrite when a targeted patch works.

### When does a production team need Teams?

Teams makes sense when multiple people work in Bolt.new and the organization needs administration. At $30 per member monthly, cost is seat-based.

Tokens are not pooled. If one developer uses less, another cannot draw from that balance. Plan usage per person rather than assuming one combined pool.

A production team must also evaluate code review, source control, deployment ownership, secrets, observability, and support. A team plan helps administration but does not replace an engineering operating model.

### Who needs Enterprise?

Enterprise is for requirements beyond self-serve plans. Consider it when procurement, security review, legal terms, or organization-specific administration are central. Ask for written included usage, seats, support, and limits before comparing.

## What is the rollover trap?

Pro tokens roll over for one month. That does not mean unused tokens accumulate forever.

Suppose you use little during a month. The unused portion may remain available in the next month, but if still unused after the rollover period, it expires instead of becoming a permanent reserve.

Rollover is a short-term buffer, not a savings account. Before subscribing, ask whether you will actively build during this billing period, expect a concentrated launch, and are paying for current work rather than hoping to preserve future capacity.

Occasional users may save with Free. Continuous users benefit more from Pro’s lack of a daily cap and one-month rollover.

Review actual usage carefully before each renewal.
