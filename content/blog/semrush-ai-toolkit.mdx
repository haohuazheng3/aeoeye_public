The Semrush AI Toolkit, which Semrush calls the AI Visibility Toolkit, is a paid product that counts how often AI answers mention your brand. As of October 2026 the Base plan lists at $99 a month per domain, billed annually, with 25 daily-tracked prompts. Buy it if you already live in Semrush. Skip it if you want a one-off read of what ChatGPT says about you.

This guide is based on public documentation as of October 2026; we have not tested the product hands-on. Every price and limit below comes from Semrush's own pages, which we fetched on 2026-10-03.

## What is the Semrush AI toolkit?

It is a separate toolkit inside Semrush that measures brand visibility in AI-generated answers. Semrush's [knowledge base](https://www.semrush.com/kb/1493-ai-visibility-toolkit) says it is built for SMBs, agencies and mid-market teams who want to see how their site and competitors are positioned across AI systems.

Practically, it has two halves. One half estimates visibility from a large pool of prompts Semrush runs itself. The other half, Prompt Tracking, re-runs a short list of prompts you choose, every day.

That second half is where your own buyer questions live, and it is the small one: 25 prompts on the Base plan.

## What does it measure?

It measures mentions, share of voice and sentiment in AI answers, plus a technical check for AI crawler blockers. The knowledge base lists six components:

- **Visibility Overview:** an AI Visibility Score, worldwide or by region.
- **Competitor Research:** which prompts and topics name competitors but not you.
- **Prompt Research:** topic-level volume, difficulty and intent, described as keyword research for AI.
- **Brand Performance:** share of voice, sentiment and brand narratives, with recommendations.
- **Prompt Tracking:** daily visibility for specific prompts.
- **AI Search Site Audit:** flags technical issues that may stop AI bots crawling your pages.

The score is relative, not absolute. Semrush says it reflects how often your brand is mentioned compared with the median for your top industry competitors, which the tool picks automatically. You do not choose the benchmark set.

Refresh rates differ by report: Visibility Overview, Competitor Research and Prompt Research update daily on a rolling basis, Brand Performance weekly, and Prompt Tracking daily.

### Which AI engines does it cover?

Semrush is not consistent about this. The [pricing page](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/ai/) lists mentions from ChatGPT, Google AI, Gemini and Perplexity. The knowledge base names ChatGPT and Google AI Mode, and the [features page](https://www.semrush.com/features/ai-visibility/) says only "ChatGPT, AI Overviews, and other LLMs".

None of the three names Claude. If Claude matters to your buyers, ask Semrush in writing before you pay.

### What do the pages not say?

The documentation we read describes scores, share of voice and sentiment. It does not say whether you can read each full AI answer, which model version is used, or how many times a prompt is re-run. Those are the details that decide how much you can trust a number, so put them on your demo checklist.

## What does it cost?

The Base plan lists at **$99 per month per domain, billed annually**, on the [pricing page](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/ai/). The knowledge base lists the same $99 and adds the limits.

| Item | Listed price | What you get or add |
|---|---|---|
| AI Visibility Base | $99/mo per domain, billed annually | 1 domain for Brand Performance, 25 tracked prompts, 300 daily queries in AI Analysis reports, 1,000 daily queries in Prompt Research, site-audit AI checks for up to 100 pages, 10 CSV exports a day |
| Extra prompts | $60/mo | +50 prompts |
| Extra domain or location | $99/mo each | One more Brand Performance domain |
| Extra user | Starting at $45/mo (pricing page) | The knowledge base says a sub-user needs another license at $99 |
| Report add-ons | $10/mo (Base), $20/mo (Pro) | Pro adds white-labeling and AI summaries |
| Enterprise | Custom | Quote only |

Two things stand out. First, the extra-prompt add-on is far cheaper per prompt than the plan itself: $99 for 25 prompts is $3.96 each, while $60 for 50 is $1.20 each. The base price pays for the reports around the prompts, not for the prompts.

Second, the user price is inconsistent between two Semrush pages. Budget the higher number until sales confirms otherwise.

On trials, the knowledge base says plainly that the toolkit has no free trial. The site footer advertises a seven-day trial of Semrush in general, and we could not confirm it covers this toolkit.

## When is an all-in-one suite enough?

An all-in-one suite is enough when AI visibility is one more column in a report you already read. Then a shared login, shared exports and one invoice are worth more than depth, and Semrush's SEO data sits next to the AI data.

Check the bundle before the standalone price. Semrush's [SEO + AI Search plans](https://www.semrush.com/pricing/seo-ai-search/) list the Starter bundle at $165.17 a month billed annually ($199 billed monthly), with 50 prompts and one AI domain. The SEO plan alone lists $117.33 a month billed annually. Add the $99 toolkit and you pay $216.33 for half the prompts.

So, on listed prices, adding the standalone toolkit to an SEO plan is the expensive route. Pro+ lists 100 prompts at $248.17 annual-billed ($299 monthly), and Advanced 200 prompts at $455.67 ($549 monthly).

The suite also wins on one technical point. Its AI Search Site Audit checks crawler access inside a tool you already use. Google's own [AI features guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says you need no special files or markup to appear in Google's AI features, so ordinary crawl access and indexing remain the basics.

### When should you skip it?

Skip it in four cases:

1. **You are not a Semrush customer.** You would pay for a suite you do not use, or for a narrow add-on that works best inside one.
2. **You want a one-off answer.** A $99 monthly commitment is built for tracking, not for a single look.
3. **Your questions are many and varied.** At 25 prompts the base plan is a watch list, not research.
4. **Your real risk is not crawling.** In AEOeye's [crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) (October 2026, 384 sites), only 1 of 193 SaaS sites blocked any AI retrieval bot, against 27 of 38 publishers. For most brands the gap is absence from the sources AI reads, not a robots.txt block. It is a convenience sample, not the whole web.

## What are the alternatives?

The right alternative depends on what you are replacing. Prices below are from each vendor's own page, checked 2026-10-03.

| Option | What it is | Listed cost |
|---|---|---|
| Google Search Console | Generative AI performance report: impressions in AI Overviews and AI Mode, by page, device and country ([Google](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/16984139)) | Free, rolled out to all sites as of August 31, 2026 |
| [AEOeye](/) | Free audit: your visibility score from 3 buyer questions asked live to ChatGPT, answers shown in full. The $29 one-time report adds Claude, Gemini, Google AI and Perplexity and 10 questions | $0, then $29 once; no subscription |
| [OtterlyAI](https://otterly.ai/pricing) | Dedicated daily tracker | Lite $29/mo (15 prompts), Standard $189/mo (100 prompts) on monthly billing; free trial offered |
| [Profound](https://www.tryprofound.com/pricing) | Enterprise tracker | Free 7-day trial (50 prompts daily), then custom-priced Enterprise; see our [Profound vs Semrush](/blog/profound-vs-semrush) comparison |

AEOeye is not a daily tracker, and we do not claim it replaces one. It answers "what do AI engines say about us today?"; Semrush, Otterly and Profound answer "how is it changing?"

### How should you decide?

Run a free check first, then pick a tool for the job that remains. Our [best AI visibility tools](/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools) roundup sorts tools by that job, and [measuring AI visibility](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) explains which metrics are worth tracking at all.

Our position: do not pay for daily tracking until a one-off audit has shown you which questions you are missing from. Daily tracking of the wrong 25 prompts is an expensive dashboard.
