Answer engine optimization (AEO) is getting your brand named and cited inside AI-generated answers — in ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Claude and Gemini — instead of only ranked in a list of links. GEO, LLMO, AIO and *LLM SEO* are other labels for the same job. The work has three layers: be retrievable, be quotable, be corroborated. Then measure, because answers vary from run to run. Most of it is good SEO plus off-site proof; measurement and corroboration are what is new.

## What is answer engine optimization (AEO)?

AEO is the work of earning a place inside the response an AI engine writes. Wikipedia lists it as another name for [generative engine optimization](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization). An answer engine reads several sources and writes one answer; your brand is in it or it isn't, and the reader may never click.

With a Google AI summary on the page, 900 US adults tracked by [Pew Research Center](https://www.pewresearch.org/short-reads/2025/07/22/google-users-are-less-likely-to-click-on-links-when-an-ai-summary-appears-in-the-results/) in March 2025 clicked a traditional result in 8% of visits, against 15% without one, and a link inside the summary in 1%. An answer can recommend you without sending a visit, so being named is now a goal separate from being ranked.

## Are AEO, GEO, LLMO, AIO and LLM SEO the same thing?

Yes. In practice they are five labels for one job: getting named and cited in AI answers. [Wikipedia notes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generative_engine_optimization) there is no consensus definition separating them, and Google's guide treats optimizing for its AI features as [still SEO](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide). Pick the label your buyers search for, then stop debating it.

| Label | Who uses it | What it emphasizes |
|---|---|---|
| AEO | Marketers, SEO platforms; named in Google's guide | The answer an engine gives, wherever it gives one |
| GEO | Researchers (coined in a 2023 paper), vendors | Visibility in responses a generative engine writes |
| LLMO (large language model optimization) | People who frame the work around models | What the model retrieves and remembers about you |
| LLM SEO, SEO for LLMs | SEO teams extending their playbook | SEO fundamentals applied to AI answers |
| AIO (AI optimization) | Wikipedia lists it; [trade press](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/data-shows-google-aio-is-citing-deeper-into-websites/543325/) uses it for Google AI Overviews | The loosest label; see below |

Two labels hide a trap. *LLM optimization* also means making a model cheaper to run; [Hugging Face's guide](https://huggingface.co/docs/transformers/main/en/llm_tutorial_optimization) covers quantization and efficient attention. *AI optimization* can mean optimizing for AI engines, using AI tools for SEO work, or tuning a model. Only the first decides whether ChatGPT recommends you; writing content faster with AI is not AEO. See our [AEO vs GEO vs SEO comparison](/compare/aeo-vs-geo-vs-seo) for finer distinctions.

## How do AI answer engines choose what to cite and whom to recommend?

They blend what the model learned in training with pages retrieved at answer time. Retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) lets a model [pull in outside documents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retrieval-augmented_generation) before it answers. [Google describes](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) its AI features the same way: they retrieve indexed pages and run several related searches at once (*query fan-out*).

Three consequences follow:

- **Retrieval is a gate.** OpenAI says sites opted out of OAI-SearchBot [will not be shown in ChatGPT search answers](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots); Google says a page must be indexed to qualify for its AI features.
- **Passages compete.** The engine uses a few passages out of many; clear, evidenced answers tend to make the cut.
- **Independent sources win.** A [2025 study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08919) found AI search leaned overwhelmingly on earned media (independent reviews and comparisons) over brand-owned pages. In its software tests, earned media supplied 73–74% of an OpenAI search model's citations, against 32–45% of Google's top ten.

In [AEOeye's recommendation benchmark](/blog/ai-brand-recommendation-measurement) (127 audits of self-selected brands, repeats included; not a market estimate), the audited brand was named in 17.6% of 892 answers. Of the 147 mentions that came with a ranking, 71.4% were first place. Being named is the hard part.

## What does an AEO program actually involve?

Work three layers in order — retrievable, quotable, corroborated — and measure across all three. An engine cannot quote a page it cannot fetch, and has little reason to trust a claim only your own site makes.

### Retrievable: can engines reach your pages?

Usually, yes. In AEOeye's [crawler access study](/blog/ai-crawler-user-agent-directory) (391 sites, fetched once on 3 October 2026; a convenience sample of established brands), 1 of 193 SaaS sites blocked any AI retrieval bot in robots.txt. But 24 of 88 e-commerce and consumer homepages challenged our self-identified research crawler. Check three things:

- **Separate switches.** As of October 2026, [OpenAI](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/bots), [Anthropic](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/8896518-does-anthropic-crawl-data-from-the-web-and-how-can-site-owners-block-the-crawler) and [Perplexity](https://docs.perplexity.ai/guides/bots) each document a search crawler apart from training; blocking GPTBot is not blocking OAI-SearchBot.
- **Public, server-sent facts.** In December 2024 Vercel found OpenAI's, Anthropic's and Perplexity's crawlers [did not render JavaScript](https://vercel.com/blog/the-rise-of-the-ai-crawler). Put key facts, pricing included, in public HTML your server sends.
- **Bot protection.** A firewall that cannot tell an AI crawler from a scraper can quietly keep you out; check your CDN logs.

### Quotable: can one passage stand alone?

Lead each section with the answer, then prove it with specifics. The [GEO paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.09735) (KDD 2024) built a 10,000-query benchmark: adding citations, quotations or statistics lifted visibility 30–40% on its position-adjusted word-count metric. Gains varied by domain and the tests used GPT-3.5 and Perplexity, so treat it as direction, not guarantee.

Evidence is not formatting. [Google says](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) its AI features need no special writing style, schema or content chunking. Don't slice pages into robot-sized pieces; add real numbers, named sources and original observations that make a passage worth citing.

### Corroborated: do independent sources agree?

Your own site is the weakest witness. Keep your name, category and pricing identical everywhere and earn real coverage on reviews, comparisons and communities; [Google says](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) inauthentic mentions help less than they seem. One caution from our study: 27 of 38 news publishers (71.1%) blocked at least one AI retrieval bot. In our reading, coverage that ChatGPT, Claude and Perplexity cannot fetch is less likely to corroborate you in their answers, so favor sources that stay open to them.

## What doesn't work for AEO?

Three things are oversold: keyword stuffing, mass-produced pages, and special files sold as a silver bullet.

- **Keyword stuffing.** The GEO paper found little to no improvement from it, and about 10% worse than baseline on Perplexity.
- **Mass-produced pages.** Google's spam policy lists [generating many pages with AI](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies), without adding value, as scaled content abuse. We learned the volume lesson ourselves: on 24 September 2026 we retired 234 posts and merged 27 after Search Console showed about half of our 496 were unrelated to AI visibility, with no clicks in 90 days; on 3 October we merged 165 duplicate-intent pages into 37. One strong page per question beats five thin ones.
- **llms.txt as a lever.** The [llms.txt proposal](https://llmstxt.org/) is a Markdown file of curated links for LLMs; 52.4% of the 330 sites we could check publish a valid one. [Google says](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) Search does not use it, and OpenAI's, Anthropic's and Perplexity's crawler pages never describe using it for answers. We keep ours because it is cheap, not because we have seen it move citations.

## How do you measure AEO success?

Ask your buyers' real questions across several engines, many times, and track the share of answers that name you, not any single answer. Cover discovery (*best X for Y*), comparisons, alternatives, pricing and risk.

[SparkToro's study](https://sparktoro.com/blog/new-research-ais-are-highly-inconsistent-when-recommending-brands-or-products-marketers-should-take-care-when-tracking-ai-visibility/) (2,961 runs, November–December 2025) found less than a 1-in-100 chance that ChatGPT or Google's AI gave the same brand list in two responses, and advises asking at least 60–100 times. Report engines separately; the earned-media study above found them differing in domain diversity and freshness. Record:

- **Mention and recommendation rate:** the share of runs that name you, and that recommend you.
- **Position and competitors:** where you appear when named, and who is named instead.
- **Citations:** which pages, yours or third-party, are cited.
- **Accuracy:** price, features and category, stated correctly. A confident wrong answer is the first thing to [fix](/guides/how-to-fix-ai-getting-your-brand-wrong).

Keep the raw answers: a score alone cannot tell you what to fix. In AEOeye's benchmark, 144 of 157 mentions were positive, so invisibility, not bad press, is the usual problem. For Google's own features, [Search Console has a Generative AI performance report](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide).

AEOeye's [free audit](/) asks three unbranded buyer questions in ChatGPT and shows the full answers; the one-time $29 report covers 10 questions across five engines. For method, see [measuring AI visibility](/blog/measuring-ai-visibility) and the [AEO audit checklist](/blog/aeo-audit-checklist).

## Is AEO replacing SEO?

No. AEO sits on top of SEO. [Google's guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide) says its generative AI features are rooted in core Search systems, and a Microsoft Bing product manager [calls crawlability, metadata and internal linking the baseline](https://about.ads.microsoft.com/en/blog/post/october-2025/optimizing-your-content-for-inclusion-in-ai-search-answers). What changes is the scoreboard, not the foundation.

Run one program with two scoreboards: rankings and clicks, and mentions and citations. Most new AEO tactics are SEO we already knew plus off-site proof, [as we argue here](/blog/most-aeo-advice-is-recycled-seo). The new parts are measurement across engines and corroboration. For the order of work, see our [AEO strategy](/blog/aeo-strategy) and [AEO vs SEO](/blog/aeo-vs-seo).
